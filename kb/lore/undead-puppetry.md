## What it is
A practitioner **skilled in the kind of magic [[codex-mortis|Kiona's notes]] describe** can make an
**undead puppet** out of a corpse and **channel their awareness through it** — along with **a
reasonable slice, though not all, of their magical power**. The stated purpose is plain: it is used by
people who want to **stay safe and hidden while still doing things out in the world and interacting
with it**.

[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] half-recognised the description on meeting
[[the-high-priest|the High Priest]] (S2.37) and went back to the book during his watch that night to
read the section properly.

## How it is made
- **It does not require killing anyone.** The sooner after death the better — so killing the subject
  and working immediately is the most efficient method — but a corpse **recently dead, up to about
  eighteen hours**, will serve.
- **Condition matters.** A corpse in poor condition, or one killed violently or heavily, makes a
  **weaker puppet**. You want it as intact as possible. The book's own recommendation is to
  **suffocate** the subject, since the puppet will not need to breathe and the method does no damage to
  the faculties.
- **It is slow.** The magic takes considerable time and effort, which is why — although a puppet is
  more disposable than the practitioner — having one damaged or destroyed is a serious nuisance.

## What the puppet is like
- **Physically** it keeps the qualities it had in life, strongly: a big, strong subject makes a strong
  puppet much more easily. A sufficiently skilled practitioner can also **imbue it with strength,
  speed, or other physical attributes** during the process.
- **Mentally** it is simply the practitioner. Its mental attributes are theirs.
- **It does not see or hear with the corpse's organs.** Perception runs through a kind of **magical
  sensing** instead.
- **The practitioner must be focusing on it.** They do not passively know what their puppet is doing.
  Running a puppet *and* their own magic at once — or **two puppets at once** — would take an unusual
  degree of mastery, on the order of concentrating on two spells.

## Range
Limited by distance — but the limit can be pushed out a long way by setting up **magical buffers or
relay points** around an area beforehand. Somebody who expects to work a puppet through a large
building, say, can prepare the ground for it. Out in open country the range is comparatively short.

## Casting through it
- **Low-level magic projects through the puppet without difficulty.**
- **The more complex the magic, the harder it is**, and the more likely to fail outright — there is a
  roll involved, and an **upper limit** on complexity.
- That ceiling is set by **how well the puppet was made**: a good subject, well worked, makes a good
  conduit; a poor subject or sloppy work makes a bad one.

## Healing it
Magically, not mechanically — it is not mended like a [[constructs|construct]]. But **ordinary healing
magic will not do it**: it takes **necromantic magic belonging to this same discipline**.

## The strange part
**This magic does not work like other magic.** Caspian is used to learning across schools and adapting
between them — this principle, and most of what the book describes, **feels different in kind from any
magic he has encountered**. His attempt to work out why got him nowhere.

## Open questions
- **Who is working [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]]?** That he **is** a puppet is the working truth
  — Caspian's reading, never confirmed in the fiction and never tested by the party, but the frame
  everything about him should be read through. **Who holds him, and from where, is completely
  unknown.** The range limit means the operator is either nearby or has prepared relay points; and the
  figure is a *fresh* undead in a temple complex that is ancient.
- **Could it perceive [[rell-aetris|Rell]]?** Rell spent part of the encounter blinking in and out of
  the ethereal plane and asked whether the puppet's magical sensing would have found him there. The
  only answer the party has is that **it did interact with him**. Unresolved.
- **Where does this magic come from, and why is it unlike the schools?** Unanswered.
