## The claim
First stated in S2.37 and then **drawn out in full in S2.38**, when
[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] asks whether holy power runs on a sliding scale from radiant to necrotic.
Greyface corrects him and cannot stop:

> *"Magic is not a scale from good to evil. It is a balance."*

And more than that — **it is a combination**. His central grievance:

> *"The ancients could do things that we could never imagine. And it's not because they were stronger.
> It's because they understood that magic is not separate strands, but woven — different elements
> brought together."*

## The demonstration
He asks [[mags|Mags]] to cast something **central to [[naelos|Naelos]]'s practice**, and she taps his
nose and casts **Guidance**. He makes the thread of it **visible**, and casts something slightly
stronger of his own alongside it.

What the party sees:

- The two magics are **extremely different**. His is **uncomfortable** even in so small a casting.
  To Mags, closest to it, it feels **hollow in a profound way** — not chilling, but a real **absence**.
- His magic **does not combine with hers. It encircles it.**
- **The effect of both is strengthened**, in a way wholly unfamiliar to anyone present.
- He holds the connection **only a moment** before it collapses, and is left **visibly drained**.
  *"Well, that was not my finest work, but perhaps you get the idea."*

His explanation: they are **antithetical poles**, which makes them **difficult to combine — especially
when there is no conduit** — but which also makes them **a powerful combination**.

## The diagram
He sketches it out and [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] copies it down. **[[itharis|Itharis]] sits at the
centre** as the conduit; **four opposed pairs** surround her.

- **[[naelos|Naelos]]** — life, connection — **opposed to [[thalparaneth|Thalparaneth]]**, un-life and
  the negation of life.
- **[[calvyr|Calvyr]]** — creation, innovation, discovery — **opposed to [[vraxyn|Vraxyn]]**,
  destruction and the reduction of all things to ash and dust.
- **[[korrathian|Korrath]]'s magic** — principle unnamed, placed **beside Vraxyn** — **opposed to
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s magic**, which he reads as **control, order and defence**.
- **[[rhynharran|Rhynharran]]** — wild magic, harmony, going with the flow of the system — **opposed to
  a broken power — Sarasthanae**, an aspect of Rhynharran covering the bardic arts, storytelling and creativity.

Some care is needed with the last two rows: he places **Rell's magic** on the diagram opposite
**Rhynharran** — *"no order save the order that already exists"* — and **Korrathian magic** beside
**Vraxyn**, saying it **fits within the balance** but is **"odd — channelled outside its proper
place."** *"The magic of Korath — its principle must have a name, but I don't know it."* He suggests
that if **great temples were raised to its principle**, it might sit more naturally.

**The ninth is broken.** Of the last: *"that power was broken, I think, long ago. We still speak of it,
at least the elves do."* He names it **Sarasthanae**; [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] knows it as **an aspect of
Rhynharran** concerned with **the bardic arts, storytelling and creativity**, generally regarded as a
subset rather than a power in its own right.

**He regards the diagram as complete.** Four pairs plus the pivot is **nine** — and nine is **generally
regarded as significant in magic**. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] would also note that **the elves,
apart from the Archon, are ruled by a council of nine**.

## Rell's magic, placed
[[rell-aetris|Rell]] volunteers a demonstration — **Arms of Hadar** — and asks what Greyface makes of
magic drawn from worlds beyond. Greyface watches without alarm and reads it back to him:

> *"Drawing power from another source, but reordering things such that they resemble something which
> comes from another place — realigning the energy, the magic of this plane into a shape less
> familiar."*

Rell accepts the reading: the magic is **of this plane**, but its application is **not natural to this
world** — forced into the wrong shape. Asked its purpose he says **survival and defence**, evolved
rather than designed, and Greyface looks **dissatisfied**: *"too often our own purposes blind us to
reality."* Pressed, Rell reframes it as **control** — it is easier to bend a will away from you than to
stand against it — and that lands.

> *"There are — or there were — magics which bent that way, toward control, order, defence. I think
> they were practised by people who lived in this land a long time ago. **I have found traces of them
> down here. You may have seen them yourself.** But as a separate school of magic, it has not been part
> of our world for a long time."*

And then, half to himself: *"Such things are not taught… nor was Thalparaneth remembered until a very
little time ago. **Perhaps more old things are surfacing than I realised.** The timing could be
better."*

## Why it matters to him
He breaks off twice to say he should not be telling them any of this, and tells them anyway:

> *"Your understanding is woefully out of balance. **And so is ours.** And that is going to be a
> problem for us all."*

Asked how: **without an understanding of both sides of the scale, "there will be no opportunity to even
try to actually change anything. To actually fix anything."** This is the argument he **made, and
lost**, and for which he is in exile — see [[the-high-priest]].

## Open questions
- **What is Itharis the pivot between?** [[mags|Mags]] has heard **Itharis called the axis or pivot
  point** in Itharian contexts, but cannot place what the pivot is supposed to be between. Whether
  Greyface's version is orthodox, heterodox, or not Itharian doctrine at all is **unresolved**.
- **What are the traces of order-magic he has found down here**, and has the party already walked past
  them? He says so directly and nobody followed it up. The **ward-lines beyond Dunton's palisade**
  (see [[the-plain]]) are an obvious candidate and **nothing connects them**.
- **Who broke Sarasthanae, the ninth power, and when?**
- **Is the nine-fold structure real, or his own theory?** He regards it as complete. Nobody has
  independently corroborated any of it.
- **Nine, and the elves' council of nine.** Noted at the table as a coincidence worth noticing.
  **No link is established.**
