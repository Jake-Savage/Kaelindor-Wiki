## The claim
Pressed by [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] on whether holy power comes on a sliding scale — radiant at one
end, necrotic at the other — **[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] leans forward and corrects him**, and then
cannot stop himself giving the lecture:

> *"There is a difference. And the scale does not slide. **It balances.**"*

He sets out his two hands:

- **On the one hand: vitality, life, energy of a generative sort.**
- **On the other: unlife, death — but not death as the priests of [[naelos|Naelos]] would have it.
  Destruction, stifling, iron control.**
- **Between them: [[itharis|Itharis]], the pivot point, the balance.**

Then he catches himself — *"In our day, at least… I should not be saying any of this. And certainly not
to someone who is going to go back to Crownspire."* And carries on anyway:

> *"But truth is truth. And your understanding is woefully out of balance. **And so is ours.** And that
> is going to be a problem for us all."*

## Why it is a problem
Asked how, he answers:

> *"Because without a balance — without an understanding of all parts of the spectrum, of both sides of
> the scale — there will be no opportunity to even try to actually change anything. To actually fix
> anything."*

And then collapses out of it: *"But I made my peace with this a long time ago, and there's no point in
any of this."* He goes **despairing** — not hostile, simply given up, in [[mags|Mags]]'s reading of
him. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] pulls him back out of it by telling him that as a wizard he ought to
know that **anything is capable of change — that that is the point, and the fun of it** — and the
figure visibly **pulls himself together**, called back to something he clearly once believed. **What he
says next is where S2.38 ends.**

## What the party could check against it
- **[[mags|Mags]] half-recognises it.** She has heard, in Itharian contexts, talk of **Itharis as the
  axis or pivot point** — but either she does not know, cannot remember, or it was never made clear
  **what Itharis is supposed to be the axis between**. The [[itharis|Church]]'s own public teaching
  frames the goddess around **transitions, death and thresholds**, not around a two-sided balance of
  generative and destructive magic.
- **"And so is ours"** puts his own side — whoever that is — in the same error as Crownspire's.
- He is explicit that **"most untouched folk don't understand more than half of how magic works,"** and
  numbers the [[korrathian|Korrathians]] firmly in that majority.

## Open questions
- **Is this orthodox Itharian doctrine, heterodox Itharian doctrine, or something else entirely?**
  Mags's religion check could not place it. The Itharian framing of *pivot* is familiar; the two things
  being pivoted between are not.
- **Who is "us" and "ours"?** He contrasts "your understanding" (Crownspire, the League) with "ours" —
  and he was **educated in the principles of his craft from a very young age** by someone, somewhere.
  He will not say where he is from.
- **What is it he thinks needs fixing?** He speaks of there being *"no opportunity to even try to
  actually change anything"* — without ever naming the thing.
- **Does this bear on [[undead-puppetry]]** — a magic he says works unlike any other, and which he says
  has **"worst side effects"** that surrogates protect him from? Nothing connects the two explicitly.
