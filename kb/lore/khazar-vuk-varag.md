## The inscription
Cut into the **floor** of the trapped corridor at the head of [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]], beside
the relief of the **brewing saint** (see [[the-seven-saints-of-calvyr]]), is a line of **Dwarvish**,
read by [[rell-aetris|Rell]], who speaks it (S2.34):

---

**Khazar Vuk Varag — I defy you.**

---

**Khazar Vuk Varag** is presented by every linguistic marker as a **proper name**, in three parts. It means something like
**"ancient hatred," "the oldest of hatreds," or "oldest enemy."** The words that follow are addressed
directly to it.

## What's known
- **It was added later.** The line does not match the coherence of the rest of the corridor's work and
  was **definitely carved after** it (DM, confirmed). It is **not graffiti scratched in haste** — it
  was cut with care and attention, and took time.
- **Read in the context of [[grommar|Grommar]], it reads as death.** On a history check of 15 or above,
  the party knows that anyone encountering this line in a text about Grommar — whose classic adversary
  was **death** — would **almost certainly interpret it as a reference to death** (DM, confirmed).
- **But it is not how dwarves say "death."** "Khazar Vuk Varag" is **not a traditional dwarvish way of
  referring to death by any means, and certainly not a Calvyran one** (DM, confirmed). The reading
  above holds *because of Grommar*, not because the word means death.
- **Mags finds nothing more.** A religion check of 19 turns up no tradition, adversary or entity of
  that name that she can bring to mind. The party can hypothesise; nothing leaps out.

## Open questions
- **Is Khazar Vuk Varag death, or something else that is merely old and hated?** [[mags|Mags]] presses this
  distinction repeatedly: *"oldest adversary" is not the same as "death."* Unresolved.
- **Who carved it, and why carve it at all?** The party's strongest reading is
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s — that this is [[grommar|Grommar]] himself, having come through the
  trap-corridor alive, announcing that he has defied death. Mags's objection stands against it: a
  private act of defiance does not need to be **patiently cut into stone for others to read**. Whoever
  carved it **wanted an inference drawn**.
- **Was Grommar actually here?** [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s argument that he was: the
  [[the-gleaners|gleaners]] know Grommar's name while knowing nothing about any of the other structures
  inside the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] — so the name reached them from somewhere.
- **Why beside the saint of hospitality** — the one relief at which the party found no trap?
