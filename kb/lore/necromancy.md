## How it works
Established in S2.38, partly from **[[the-high-priest|Greyface]]** and partly confirmed outright:

- **It is fuelled by the life energy of other people and things.** The power **cannot come from
  nothing**.
- **It grows with use.** Unlike most kinds of magic, the practitioner **gains power from the practice
  rather than spending it** — it becomes more powerful the more it is used.
- **Place matters enormously.** Worked inside a temple raised to [[thalparaneth|Thalparaneth]] — as
  [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] is — necromancy is **very much easier** than it would be anywhere else.
  Greyface says the Ossuary has **enhanced his magic greatly**, enabling things that would otherwise
  have been beyond his power.
- **It is not like other branches of magic**, which Greyface concedes is a trivial thing to say, since
  none are like the others — but he means it structurally. It is one of the **strands no longer
  recognised** (see [[the-balance]]).

[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] is **rationing himself severely** and is **efficient** with what he takes.
That efficiency is why he needs a subject at all when he does something substantial.

## What it costs
> *"Use of necromancy — real necromancy — is not a healthy thing for a living person to do."*

- **It corrupts the body**, and **over time it breaks the mind**.
- **It severs bonds, much as [[naelos|Naelos]] strengthens them.** In Greyface's words: *"You lose your
  ability to see others for what they are to you, what they are to one another. You lose compassion,
  empathy, a sense of people as anything other than instruments."*
- The toll is **not evenly distributed**. Told by [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] that surely such things
  weigh each other out, he answers: *"In the round they do. In individuals, no — not at all."*
- **Containment is possible but partial.** Greyface has **developed methods**, and keeps **rules for
  himself** — and admits *"even I have days when I cannot recall any of the reasons for the rules I
  have made for myself. And I have been raised to this power."*
- **Working through proxies lessens the effect** — but only somewhat. *"I cannot pretend that I am
  unmarked by it."* (See [[undead-puppetry]].)

His warning to Caspian is flat: **"If you choose to use it, you will lose yourself."**

## The shortcut, and why he refuses it
**For a practitioner as advanced as Greyface, the normal course would be to become undead himself.** It
makes the magic far easier, and it **stops some of the deleterious effects** of practising it.

**The reason not to** is that becoming undead also **removes the ability to think the way a living
person thinks** — the very faculty the magic is already eroding. Greyface has **taken great pains not
to**, using **[[undead-puppetry|puppets and surrogates]]** instead, in order to **remain in his mortal
body** and keep thinking as a living man does. The party's judgement is that the choices he made
instead are questionable in themselves, but the decision is deliberate and he has held to it for twelve
years.

## Caspian
[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] already knew most of the cost from [[codex-mortis|Kiona's infernal notes]],
and did not hide his recognition. Greyface noticed — *"you know something, I see. That's good"* — and
then went further than the party expected:

- *"You are almost halfway toward being able to practise no other sort of magic in any case. And you
  should know better than most the cost that comes with it."*
- Told the toll had already been taken, he answered simply: **"I know."**
- **"Better perhaps it is in your hands than those already lost to other considerations. I could teach
  you its principles, I suspect. I am loath to do it."**
- And, crucially, that **Caspian's "condition" might offer him some protection** — a word
  [[mags|Mags]] and [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] both caught and Caspian ignored.

The teaching would **not be costless**: *"not to you, not to me, and not to the subject on which we
would have to practise."*

## Open questions
- **What is Caspian's "condition"?** Unexplained in the fiction; Greyface has now referred to it twice
  in front of the party and Caspian has explained nothing. **Working truth (DM): he is some kind of
  undead, most likely a dhampir** — which is why Greyface thinks it might protect him. See
  [[caspian-talon]].
- **What does "almost halfway" mean**, and halfway to what?
- **How many other strands are lost?** Greyface believes Thalparaneth's is not the only one.
