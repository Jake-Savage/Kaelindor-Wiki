## What it is
The **broken map** is a large **mural-map** covering the whole length of one wall of the pillar
chamber beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] (S2.31). It glows very faintly and has been
**smashed and reformed many times** — some pieces cut deliberately into angular shapes, others
shattered and set back irregularly, most in the right place but some misaligned, like a jigsaw
reassembled again and again. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s **Detect Magic** reads **strong abjuration
magic** on it — preservation that has kept the (clearly very old) map from crumbling entirely, though
much of it has decayed. Caspian **copies it** with his enchanted map-pen, over some time.

## What's known (S2.31)
It is a **map of this region / continent — but of a different, older configuration** of the same
landscape. The geography is recognisably the same land, but the works upon it are not the present
ones:

- **Fewer mountains.** Where the jagged **Stonemarch** range now stands there are only **low, gentle
  hills before the Elan plain**; the mountains do not begin until far north, down to the line of the
  **Ridgeway** (currently the northernmost east–west path). Isolated peaks are shown, but no full
  range.
- **A vaster forest.** The **Branweald** is drawn **much larger** than today — swallowing the Dernan
  and half of Bran of Caerweth — consistent with what is remembered of it roughly **a thousand years
  ago**.
- **Misplaced settlements.** Marks in the convention of settlements sit **where no major
  settlements are now** (including a sizeable one in the impossible northern mountains, near where
  [[trevaryn|Trevaryn]] would be), while a settlement is plausibly shown where **Mooncross** now
  stands. There is **nothing notable at Greywatch** (founded recently) or a substantial place at
  Cranespire/Crownspire's coast. The area of the present [[the-mirrowen|Mirrowen]] is the **most
  densely marked** part of the whole map, but nothing sets it apart as a separate, damaged region.
- **Unknown script.** The notation is in a script **none of the party recognises** — the names are
  unfamiliar even in their *sound*; it resembles **Draconic** most, but is **not** Draconic (both
  Rell and Fabian speak it). [[trevick|Trevick]]'s **Comprehend Languages** glasses do not help.
  Caspian copies some names down to try to translate later.

## Open questions
- **Does the map predate the Mirrowen's fragmentation?** It shows the region **before** the
  present jagged terrain and (implicitly) before whatever damaged it — suggestive of the
  [[the-mirrowen|Mirrowen]]'s unsolved pre-historical origin. **Not confirmed** to depict that event
  or its aftermath; kept as an open lead, not fused with any other thread.
- Who made it and in what language? What is it doing in a buried complex now inhabited by the
  [[the-gleaners|gleaners]], on the same wall as the [[the-glass-smoke|glass smoke]]?
- **Related murals elsewhere.** S2.32: the walls of [[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]],
  below and across the chasm, carry murals described as **not that dissimilar** to this one. They have
  not been examined, and whether they are the same hand, the same period, or more maps is **unknown**.
