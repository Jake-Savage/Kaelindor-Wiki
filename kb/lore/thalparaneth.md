## What it is
**Thalparaneth** is the deity to whom **[[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]** was raised. The party had never
heard the name — [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] and [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] both cast back and found
nothing — and **[[the-high-priest|Greyface]]** names it for them in S2.38.

The word **sounds Elvish** — and **[[mags|Mags]]**, who as a cleric knows the ordinary Elvish names for
all the gods, establishes **by elimination that it is not one of them**. She does not speak Elvish; she
simply knows the names.

## The principle
Greyface's account:

- Thalparaneth **was seen as a god for a long time**, and he believes the Ossuary's existence bears that
  out.
- He **embodied the principle of un-life**. Greyface is precise about the word: *"death is not correct.
  Better to say un-life. It is more of an opposite to life than any sense of death as its end."*
- It is **an energy**, one that works **quite differently to the vital energies** that [[naelos|Naelos]]
  and other gods represent. On [[the-balance|his diagram]] of magic it is the **direct opposite of
  Naelos**: life and connection on one side, **un-life and the negation of life** on the other.
- The temple was **crafted to sharpen Thalparaneth's influence** — which is why *"to use necromancy here
  is very much easier than it might be in other contexts."* This is ordinary magical theory, which the
  whole party recognises: temples are built to resonate with the magic core to their deity.

## The revival
This is the part that matters, and Greyface gives it almost in passing:

- The principle **"has been almost completely lost — or was, until relatively recently."**
- **His kinsman rediscovered some of Thalparaneth's ways**, practised them, and **taught them to
  others — Greyface included**.
- **"There has been a revival of his… worship is not quite the right word, but practice."**
- **Greyface was raised to such disciplines** from a very young age.

He also believes Thalparaneth is **not the only lost strand**: *"there are others, other strands to
magic that we no longer use, and that is a problem."* He **argued that they should be recovered and
used, and lost that argument** — which is why he is in exile. See [[the-balance]].

## Open questions
- **Who was the kinsman who rediscovered it?** Unnamed. Greyface says only that there are *"others in
  my family"* with whom he had *"a disagreement of policy, strategy."*
- **How far has the revival gone, and who else practises it?** He speaks of it as an ongoing thing he
  was raised into, not a private hobby.
- **Is this the "unholy power" of [[the-bleeding-years|the Bleeding Years]]?** Thaloriel's armies came
  south wielding "dark magics not seen before," and a god of un-life was revived inside the Archon's
  own family in living memory. Against that: Lae'ethorian, who was raised in the practice, lost his
  family's argument rather than leading it.
- **Who raised the temple?** Greyface dates the Ossuary's construction to **around the time the
  [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] was imprisoned**, and says it was **not built underground** but
  swallowed later.
