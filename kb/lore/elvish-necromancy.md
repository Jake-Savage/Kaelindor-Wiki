## What is known
Prompted in S2.38 by [[the-high-priest|Greyface]] — an elf-featured undead wearing borrowed bodies —
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]], [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] and [[rell-aetris|Rell]] all cast back for
anything they have heard about **elves taking bodies**.

**The common version**, known to Caspian and Rell: it is **a fairly commonplace rumour that the Elvish
armies use necromancy** on the battlefield. The sort of thing everybody has heard and most people file
as a horror story.

**The informed version**, which [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] has from **friends and relatives in the
military**:

- The conflict between the **Erathian League** and the **Elvish Empire** is **largely frozen** in terms
  of direct contact. But the eastern kingdoms, though largely conquered and suppressed, still produce
  **engagements and uprisings**, and there are **limited engagements across the mountain passes**.
- **There is serious concern about bodies left behind on a battlefield.** The understanding is that an
  army which cannot recover its dead and wounded should **burn them, so that only ash is left**. Bodies
  **should not be left to be recovered by the enemy**, for fear they will be used in dangerous magic.
- **The fear is not primarily infiltration.** Crude reanimation — raising corpses and sending them at
  your enemies — is a known and practised thing on several battlefields, but that is not the worry.
  The worry is that, through a recovered body, **information can be gathered**, and that the magic
  worked on it might be used to **harm, influence, or in some way control the people that person had
  been close to** — those with whom they had **close links or ties**.
- Separately, deception across a frozen border — people crossing in disguise — is a **constant**
  background concern, but the body-recovery fear is **not framed as a doppelganger problem**.

## Why it matters here
Greyface's **face is human, altered to look more elvish**; the change came on him during
[[black-barrow|the disaster]] when he began working serious magic, and it frightened the villagers,
none of whom had ever seen an elf. He describes himself as **an exile** who thinks it **in his interest
to lie low**, and says he was **educated in the principles of his craft from a very young age**. He
will not say where he is from.

## Open questions
- **Is Greyface connected to the Elvish Empire at all?** The party has drawn no line out loud, and the
  fiction has drawn none. He is elf-*featured*, not established as an elf; the features arrived with
  the magic rather than before it.
- **Is [[undead-puppetry]] the magic the League's burn-your-dead doctrine is actually afraid of?** The
  doctrine's stated fear — leverage over the dead person's living ties — is **not** what the puppetry
  described in [[codex-mortis|Kiona's notes]] does. Keep the two separate until something joins them.
- **Who taught Greyface, and from what tradition?** Unanswered; he deflected.
