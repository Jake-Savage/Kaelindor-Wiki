## What it is
The party's name for the thing that came out of [[dunton|Dunton]] after the convoy in S2.40 and fell on
it in S2.41. It is directed from inside by **an elf** on a throne of bones —
**[[the-high-priest|Lae'ethorian]]**, in his own body rather than the puppet.

## What it looks like
- **A wall of loose bones**, **twenty to twenty-five feet high**, **floating a couple of feet off the
  ground** — the bones scrape the rock and kick up dust now and then, but mostly it hovers.
- From above it is **crescent-shaped**: its wings are **disparate bones caught in what looks like a
  swirling wind**.
- At its heart is **a dark, pulsing orb** that darkvision cannot penetrate and Daylight does not touch.
  It is a **magical darkness**; [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] dispels it and finds it **high-level magic**.
  Inside is **a platform of bones** about fifteen feet up, where the elf sits, **untouched by the
  storm**.
- Around the bones is **a penumbra**, hazy and gloomy even in Daylight, that reaches **beyond the
  physical bones**. Near it the air goes **very cold** and tastes **sharply metallic**.
- It sounds like **a storm driving sticks and debris down a street**, vastly magnified, a **clattering**
  that carries a long way on the plain, and smells of **dry, dusty bone**.

## What it does
- **It moves fast** across the plain, and it can **split into two** and close from two directions.
- **It batters whatever it rolls over** with whirling, spiking bones.
- **It holds what it catches.** Getting out takes a struggle; something in it is **stronger than
  gravity**, and people caught inside are carried round within it rather than falling.
  [[hawk|Hawk]], only brushing the penumbra, was nearly drawn in.
- **It takes up the dead and the senseless.** Villagers knocked down under it are drawn up into the
  swirl and **become part of it**; it does not let them go.
- **Its controller can protect someone within it.** [[rell-aetris|Rell]] was not struck by the bones
  while the elf held him, and was as soon as he let go.

## What works against it
- **The power of [[naelos|Naelos]]** — not by fear. There is none of the dread ordinary undead show when
  turned, only a raw blow against a wall. But **some of its ancient bones crumble to dust**.
- **Spirit Guardians** clash with it head-on — the opposed magics of [[the-balance|Greyface's diagram]]
  — and are **volatile** to hold. Held, they hurt it and **shelter the souls** of the newly dead it has
  taken (see [[mags]]). Overwhelmed, they collapse.
- **Dispel Magic** clears its darkness, and can clear **a small patch** of it, enough to drop a person
  out; dispelling the whole is out of reach.
- **Thunderwave** shatters bones into shards and opens a way out, without destroying many.

## Open questions
- **Are these the bones of [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]?** They are ancient, and the Ossuary is a short
  walk from Dunton.
- **What happens to the people it took?** Their bodies became part of it. What becomes of their life
  energy, and of the souls Mags did not reach?
- **How far can it go from the Ossuary?**
