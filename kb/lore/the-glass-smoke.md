## What it is
The **glass smoke** (the gleaners' name; [[turley|Turley]], S2.31) are **undead spirits** housed in
six tall pillars of a **dark, glassy substance** in the antechamber beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's
Fold]]. Within each pillar, something moves **like smoke swirling in glass** — visible only in rare
flickers, otherwise passing for a solid, faintly-shiny column. They are **dangerous**: Turley says
they have killed a gleaner "a time or two if they get too hungry."

## What's known (S2.31)
Established by the party's spells in the pillar chamber:

- **Undead / necrotic.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s **Detect Magic** reads relatively strong
  **necrotic** magic from the pillars, and his Arcana check makes him **almost certain** the
  signatures are **undead**.
- **A dual mind.** [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s **Detect Thoughts** on one finds a bleak, **dual nature**:
  one part a **predator's cunning**, lost to reason, **lying in wait to feed**; the other a deep
  **loneliness** and longing — it responds to living visitors by thinking of **its home and what has
  happened to it**, wanting both to **eat** them and to **learn** from them what became of its world.
  (The contact left Rell rattled — he failed a save resisting it.)
- **Sheltered, not caged.** Caspian's **Identify** on a pillar finds the glass is **enchanted to
  shelter** the creature, not to imprison it: it **could come out**, but the spirit is **bound to
  the pillar** and cannot go far from it. As Caspian read it, the thing within **coiled like a snake
  about to strike**, and he broke off in fright before fully identifying it.

## Open questions
- **Jailer or guard-dog?** The party first read the room as a **prison** — the repaired
  [[kethrans-fold|gargoyle golem]] a jailer, the pillars its cells. The DM pushed back: the
  predatory mind held **no frustration**, only an **expectation of getting what it wants**, which
  reads less like a caged prisoner and more like a **guardian / defence system** lying in ambush.
  The party revised toward "guard dogs." **Unresolved.**
- What are they, exactly, and what "home" and lost "world" does the lonely half remember? The map on
  the same wall (see [[the-broken-map]]) shows a region in an older configuration — whether the two
  are connected is **not established**.
