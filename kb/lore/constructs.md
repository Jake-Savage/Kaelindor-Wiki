## What it is
Constructs are artificial beings. Around 300 years ago the priests of [[calvyr|Calvyr]] developed "autonomous constructs capable of carrying out simple menial tasks on command," alongside other innovations (airships, reliable compasses) — see [[the-disappearance-of-dragons]] for that era of change.

## What's known
- Ordinary constructs are **simple and menial**, built for set tasks.
- [[dakir|Dakir]] is a **uniquely advanced** construct: a Church-of-Calvyr creation roughly ten years old, who casts bronze draconic magic and believes (or believed) himself a living dragonborn-lineage sorcerer. His true nature — human/construct origin — is a slow reveal (quest [[dakirs-draconic-heritage]]).
- The [[church-of-calvyr|Church of Calvyr]] (the Unity) made Dakir roughly ten years ago (where is never stated); its artificer-priest [[talan-pryce|Talan Pryce]] later finished the spell-storing device installed in him, at a Calvyr temple (S2.10).
- **Not all constructs are the Church's.** In S2.34 the party met **spider-form constructs** nesting
  deep inside the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]], at [[the-foul-pool|the foul pool]] on
  [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]] — dog-sized, metallic, webless, immediately hostile, and lowering
  themselves on what appear to be **steel cables** rather than scuttling. The DM's ruling is that they
  are **either fully constructs or very significantly modified by artifice**. They sit inside a Calvyr
  temple complex; who built them is open. The suggestion that they are Calvyran automata with
  authentication protocols was flagged at the table as a **player's** idea, not a character's
  knowledge.
- **They were made to a standard beyond what is normally made.** Seeing them close up in S2.35,
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]] found them **very intricate**, moving **naturalistically** — and he is **not
  aware of constructs being made to that standard** as a rule. The forge directly above them
  ([[the-ancient-forge]]) holds construct parts that are, even at a glance, **sophisticated**, though
  none are usable now. Whatever was done in that room was advanced work done a very long time ago.
- **Age and damp beat them in the end.** After centuries in a wet cave the spiders were **rusting** and
  in poor repair, and an upcast **Shatter** destroyed the lot of them in S2.35.

## Open questions
- How was a construct as advanced as Dakir made, and why? Is his draconic magic part of his design?
- Are there other advanced constructs like him?
- **Who made the spiders at [[the-foul-pool|the foul pool]]?** They nested over the chamber's only
  exit, directly beneath a forge full of sophisticated construct parts — which is where the work was
  most likely done, though nobody has said so.
- **Who else can build to that standard?** [[dakir|Dakir]] is described as uniquely advanced for the
  present age; these spiders are ancient and, on Rell's eye, better than the norm. Whether the craft was
  lost, or whether the two traditions meet anywhere, is unexplored.
