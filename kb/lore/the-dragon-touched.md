## What is told
Recalled by [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] in S2.33, on a history check, when the party examines the
[[the-draconic-regalia|regalia]] found beneath [[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]]. These are
described as **not even stories — closer to legends**: accounts of a time **well before the recorded
history of the current age**, when there were people who had been **physically changed by the magic of
dragons**, in a manner not wholly unlike the way **[[korrathian|Korrathian]] planar magic** changes a
tiefling.

Nothing further is established: not what they were called, where they lived, what the change consisted
of, or what became of them.

## Why it comes up
The ceremonial clothing in the cache is cut for someone **taller and more slender than a typical
human**, and the [[the-golden-courtyard|courtyard]]'s arches imply builders of **six to seven feet**.
The courtyard's centrepiece is a naturalistic **[[the-golden-dragon-statue|gold dragon]]** and its
inscription is in **archaic Draconic**. Fabian's judgement is that it is **plausible** the regalia was
made for one of the dragon-touched, and that this "lines up with all the other stuff here."

That is a reading of converging evidence, **not** an identification — and it is the party's, not the
world's.

## Open questions
- **Were the courtyard's builders the dragon-touched?** Height, script, subject and regalia all point
  the same way, but nothing states it.
- **How do they relate to [[the-disappearance-of-dragons|the disappearance of dragons]]?** The
  disappearance was roughly 300 years ago; these legends are of an age far older. The two are **not**
  connected by anything in the fiction.
- Is the **"Lord of Light"** of the statue's inscription one of them, a dragon, or something else
  again?
