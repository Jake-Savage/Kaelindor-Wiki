## Recap

**The prisoner talks.** [[rell-aetris|Rell]] puts the party's questions to
[[the-patrol-commander|the commander]] mind to mind. He is **not reluctant** to answer — angry and
bombastic, but willing — though every question takes time to get across.

- **The surface means nothing to him.** He is baffled by the question; as far as he is concerned there
  **is no way to the surface**, and he cannot see why anyone would want one.
- **He and his company are the acolytes of the Devourer**, servants of the
  [[the-stonehunger|Devourer]] *"in its holy work of destroying everything above."* They are, he says,
  **limitless** — hundreds and thousands of them — and the party can expect to be **hunted**.
- **The plains are constantly patrolled and watched.** Any non-believers who pollute **the body of the
  God** will be caught and **sacrificed**. The idea of *leaving* makes no sense to him: the Devourer
  devours everything, here or above, and since the party is here, they are **good candidates for
  sacrifice**.
- **The witch.** After some effort to make the question understood, he knows what Rell means: a place
  that is **serious pollution of the body of the Devourer**, a long-standing problem. They can **see it
  from far away**, but whenever they mount an attack and get close, **it is gone**. He is not sure it is
  real. What he describes as hideous is, Rell gradually realises, **a garden** — a green oasis down
  here.
- **Lae'ethorian.** He calls him **a high priest of the Devourer** who came **some time ago** (he is
  vague about time in general). He has been **behaving very strangely**: he has kept **sacrifices in
  readiness** for a long time rather than sacrificing them — but he is a high priest, and must have his
  reasons. When the patrol spoke to him earlier tonight he told them he would **take what he needed,
  and whatever was left they could have**.
- **Himself.** He has been down here **a long time**. He is humanoid, plainly **undead**, his skin
  papery and ancient over the bones, shrunken and shrivelled; what species he was is impossible to
  tell, and Rell's study of his frame finds nothing obvious. He was one of the casters.

Judging there is nothing more worth the time, with more of his kind surely coming, **Rell kills him**,
quietly.

**What they carry.** The six bodies (one patrolman got away) carry **no obvious magic items**.

- **Robes**: a uniform, with the same insignia on each, **old and preserved by magic** — unlike
  [[the-high-priest|Lae'ethorian]]'s robes, which were new. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] takes one and
  suggests everyone does; [[mags|Mags]] does.
- **Rings as spellcasting foci**, worn on the **three middle fingers of the left hand**: the two casters
  have three each, the mace-wielders, who could also cast the bolt of death, **one each**. **Rell
  takes them.**
- **Rell studies the commander's face** so that he can disguise himself as him later, and **mashes the
  faces** of the bodies so that nobody can tell which of them is missing. To the party's eyes they all
  look much alike anyway.

**The march.** The caves are still **about three hours** away, by where the party suspects them to be,
and harder to find without [[the-provost|the Provost]]. The convoy moves under **low lights pointed at
the ground**, enough not to trip. Mags leads the way with [[trevick|Trevick]]'s help; Rell scouts ahead;
Caspian watches the rear. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s **fruit cake** is shared out among the wounded
over the night — Rosie Tryn, Trevick, Sarah Farlin, Mothrigg Tosscobble, Rell — and by the end of it
**one piece is left**. The first half hour goes the right way.

**The cave shadows.** As the lantern light crosses a dark, desolate stretch, the shadows in the lee of a
rock to the south-east **twist and move of their own accord** and reach hungrily for the convoy —
**five cave shadows**. One chills Sarah Farlin. The villagers fight: **May Tegg** shoots one, **Olo
Halfkettle** twists her ankle swinging at it and later charges with a halfling battle cry, **Del
Zoric** stabs and shrugs off a hit, **Tom Pruett** hits himself in the face with his own club, and
Mothrigg puts himself in the line of fire. Rell's **acid** bursts dissolve two; Caspian's **Fire Bolt**
burns two, one of them to a bonfire; Trevick shoots one down. **Nobody dies.** Del refuses any cake; Olo
paints blood under her eyes.

**Losses.** The next half hour, with Caspian leading, goes **the wrong way**. Somewhere along it
**Bohdan Tarek** drops out of sight; by the time anyone asks where he is, he has been gone a while, and
**something has taken him**. He was the person **Max** the dog was most attached to; Max whimpers, and
Trevick soothes him, and from then on **Max sticks to Trevick's heels**. The half hour after, going the
right way again, **Finnan Highcroft** falls off the back of the group, and by the time anyone realises,
**something has taken him**. Nobody knows what. The convoy is now **seventeen and the dog**.

**The rider.** Mags's next stretch goes well. In the distance something big moves fast across the plain,
roughly the opposite way — like a rider on horseback, but not quite. Rell creeps closer. It is **a woman
riding a large tiger**, bent low over its neck to urge it on, **little sparks of light** flying around
her; she is **living**, wears **a very impractical dress** for the plain, carries **a staff**, and Rell
is fairly sure she is **a caster**. He hails her. She pulls up with the staff ready to cast; he steps
out with his hands up, and she lowers it, impatient at the interruption: *"Unexpected, but yes, well
met."*

- **Her advice:** make haste. The creature, she believes, is **about to return to the depths**; if they
  want out, they should go now.
- **She takes them for escapees from the Keep**, which takes prisoners and sacrifices anyone it finds
  down here. Rell corrects her: the villagers were held by a man who calls himself **a high priest**.
  Her eyes narrow — *that might explain a few things.*
- **"He let them go? He really is leaving."** Told the party drove him off, she is openly surprised,
  and asks what caused **this change of heart**. Rell says the party was the catalyst.
- **No way out that she knows of.** She is sympathetic but will not go out of her way; as far as she
  knows there is **no ready way out**.

Rell opens **a telepathic link** to her; she takes it readily, and it feels strong. She must go — she
wants to find out what is happening — and rides on, **unmistakably toward [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]**.
Over the link, as she goes:

- She is **an elf**, and **cagey** about who she is; she gives **no name**. She clearly **lives down
  here** rather than travelling through, but will not say for how long.
- She is **very surprised** that Rell knows the name **Lae'ethorian**. They are **allies of a sort**,
  she says, though her feelings about him are evidently **mixed at best**.
- **He sent her a message** saying he means to **return to the surface**, and she is going to him to
  find out what is going on.
- Asked not to mention the party to him, she **acknowledges** it but makes **no promise and no
  objection**; Rell cannot read her.
- **She is the witch** the Keep's people spoke of, and finds it very funny that the party heard of her
  from them; she plainly **enjoys running rings round them**.

**The tiger is not flesh and blood** but a summoned creature. Rell shares all of this with the party.
Caspian dislikes "allies of a sort"; Mags is mistrustful on principle; Rell reasons that if she meant
them harm she has had her chance, and that she keeps apart from the Keep, which Lae'ethorian does not.
The party speculates that he is leaving because they took his supply, and that "what he needed" was
enough life to make the journey.

**The cave spitters.** The next stretch goes the wrong way and past **a pool**, which Mags has the
villagers kept clear of. A **cave spitter** comes out of the water: large, gross, with **something
smeared across its hands** that it tries to press onto bare skin, and **a poisonous sheen** on its own
skin that splashes back on anyone who hits it. It bites Caspian and breaks for the child **Elsie
Bowditch**, who gets away. Rell meets it with **Shadow Blade** and scimitar; Trevick shoots it dead,
then spots **a second** lurking at the water's edge.

The second **smears its goo onto Mags**. It is sticky and acidic, and it **melds with her flesh and
burrows in**, independent of the creature. Mothrigg throws himself at it and is **brought down** by the
splash-back; Mags keeps him alive. Maddie Pruett tries and fails to cut the thing out of Mags. **Del
Zoric** stabs the spitter in the foot with a triumphant cry. Then Mags, with **Sarah Farlin** — trained
in medicine — helping, cuts it out at real cost: in the goo is **a grub, a parasite**, and Mags
throws it in the pool before Trevick can keep it. **Rell kills the spitter and the splash-back drops
him**; Mags brings him round. The last cake goes to Mothrigg and Rell, with a stern word to Mothrigg to
keep out of fights.

**Spirits.** For all that, the convoy is **still about two hours from the caves**: an hour gone the
right way, an hour the wrong. Trevick leads the next stretch with Mags and it goes **the right way** —
and for the first time on the journey the villagers are **laughing**. Trevick had given one of them a
copy of **[[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s book**, and they are reading the smarter bits out loud to each
other. Spirits are high.

## Key facts learned
- **The Keep's patrols call themselves the acolytes of the Devourer**, and hold the plain to be **the
  body of the God**. They serve its work of **destroying everything above**, cannot conceive of
  leaving, and **sacrifice** the non-believers they catch. Their commander claimed **limitless**
  numbers.
- **Their foci are rings** on the left hand — three for a caster, one for the rest — and their robes
  are an **old uniform preserved by magic**.
- **Lae'ethorian is, to them, a high priest of the Devourer** who came some time ago and has oddly
  **kept his sacrifices in readiness**. He told the patrol it could have **whatever he left**.
- **The witch is real**: a living **elf**, a caster with a **summoned tiger**, who **lives down here**
  and keeps **a garden** the Keep cannot reach — it is gone whenever they get close. She is **an ally
  of a sort** of Lae'ethorian's, with mixed feelings; he has told her he means to **return to the
  surface**, and she is riding to him.
- **She believes the Stonehunger is about to return to the depths**, and knows of **no ready way out**.
- **Bohdan Tarek and Finnan Highcroft were taken** by something unseen. **Seventeen
  villagers** remain, with the dog.
- **Cave shadows** and **cave spitters** roam this part of the plain; a spitter's goo carries **a
  parasite** that burrows into flesh.
