## Recap
The session resumes exactly where S2.31 ended: the party is still in the passage above the chasm,
under [[trevick|Trevick]]'s **Pass Without Trace**, unobserved, with [[turley|Turley]] and
[[glorta|Glorta]] left behind in the tunnels under the trigger word "sleep."

Looking out, the party sees **dozens of [[the-gleaners|gleaners]]** at their ordinary work along the
**[[the-stone-river|river of churning stone]]** — most sitting on the edges and on the **three narrow,
widely-spaced bridges**, casting long **grapnel-tipped poles** down into the churn to pluck things
out, others carting the take away. On the **far side of the canyon** a sheer rock face carries a
**large carved archway** with no visible door in it — the gate the gleaners indicated. Other doors of
various kinds open off the area. The DM establishes that these people, though goblin-like, are **not
straightforwardly goblins**; they are roughly **one size smaller than a human**, and there are **no
other human-sized people down here**. [[mags|Mags]] is **not familiar with anything like this place**
from her own time underground, and she has **no knowledge of this "God"** either. What she recognises
is only the *form* — she has met faiths that call their deity simply "God," in the singular, and is
untroubled by them ("one god, many faces") — **not** the entity the gleaners describe.

Ruled out from crossing on stealth alone (Pass Without Trace will not cover a wide open cavern), the
party settles on a magical clearance. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] gives [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] a
**potion of diminution**; Fabian drinks it and is made up with a **disguise kit** as a gleaner (a
strong performance check), while [[mags|Mags]] and [[rell-aetris|Rell]] each cast **Disguise Self** to
pass as gleaners. Using **Rell's scroll of Mass Suggestion** — cast above his own level, so on an
ability check made with advantage and a reroll — Fabian projects the suggestion in four directions at
those who look like leaders: **God has brought more gifts than expected; there are shiny things on the
surface; everyone must go up at once and not stop until every shiny thing is accounted for.** He
brandishes the shining **[[the-golden-lute|golden lute]]** as evidence of the haul.

The DM resolves the crowd in four groups (Caspian spends a **Portent** 5 on the nearest). Two groups
go at once — one packing up excitedly, one simply throwing down its tools; a third wavers, unconvinced
that there can be more shiny things after yesterday's trip up; the furthest group, straining to hear,
dismisses the whole thing as a prank. A **little over half** the gleaners head for the surface,
scattering into passages and wall-gaps the party could not follow. Fabian and Mags, still disguised,
walk in on the wavering group and press the argument — first come, first served — and they too pack up
and go. The shift's most senior gleaner is judged to have gone with them; whether that was
[[tuffin|Tuffin]] is not established.

Rell crosses the now-empty **middle bridge** toward the archway; Caspian and Trevick follow under Pass
Without Trace. The last group of a dozen or so, on the far bridge, is unmoved and suspicious — "we
were told to do this, everyone's left, what are we supposed to do?" Fabian and Mags approach and
deliberately **circle north of them so that facing the party means turning their backs on the
bridge**; Mags argues (persuasion 18, aided by Fabian) that they should go and find out whether their
companions are all right, and they agree, packing their equipment away neatly first. The DM notes that
among their own the gleaners are **kind, thoughtful and courteous to one another**, whatever contempt
they showed the "godless."

The bridges are **small, rickety and intimidating** to medium-sized people, and the churn below would
crush and roll anything that fell in. Crossing, Fabian looks down (with a self-directed bardic
inspiration): most of what tumbles past is **old rock worn smooth by repeated churning**, while the
fresher, jagged stone matches the rockslide above and carries **bits of what were people's houses** —
the scrambled remains of [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]]. Every now and again, though, something
appears that **could not have come from Kethran's Fold** at all: a piece of a **column**, a piece of
**marble floor tile**. Mags, looking down, can make out nothing through the dust.

North of the canyon, Mags and Fabian find many doors — most obviously gleaner-built, gleaner-sized and
made of reclaimed material — and one thing that stands out: a **natural cleft in the rock**, wide
enough for two abreast, not a made doorway, through which they glimpse a **much more lavishly
decorated room** and a flash of something. Ignoring the question of whether looking is stealing, they
go through, and find themselves in a **[[the-golden-courtyard|courtyard]]**: archwayed porticoes on
three sides that lead nowhere (bare rock behind them), **murals** on the walls not unlike the
[[the-broken-map|map mural]] seen above, and, on a large plinth at the centre, a
**[[the-golden-dragon-statue|statue of a golden dragon]]** — perhaps eighteen feet long and ten to
twelve high, of exquisite workmanship, apparently **made of gold**, and pristine and shiny where the
rest of the room has begun to decay.

At the archway, Rell stands before the rock and it **begins to flow and shape itself**.
[[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s Arcana check identifies what is forming: **an earth elemental of some
sort** — and he knows that earth elementals can be **bound into service by magic**, so a creature that
habitually serves as a door is **probably compelled** to do so by someone or something; some, though
not all, are **sentient**. He relays this to Rell over the party's telepathic link rather than aloud,
and keeps behind him. Trevick watches the passages for returning gleaners.

The face forms: **large, humanoid, interested and curious**, faintly mocking but not hostile. Rell
drops his disguise deliberately, to be seen as an outsider with news of above — and the face, clocking
that these are not gleaners, seems **more intrigued rather than annoyed**. In a **deep, earthy voice**
it asks aloud whether they intend to walk **Grommar's Path**, in a tone the DM describes as sardonic,
amused and slightly pitying. Fabian's history check (with advantage) recalls the name: **[[grommar]]**,
a semi-legendary dwarven smith of enormous skill who sought to forge **a weapon that could kill death**
and vanished before he could do it. The party takes the face to be the **[[the-custodian|custodian]]**
the gleaners described, though it does not name itself. The session ends there.

**Retconned:** at the start of S2.33 the DM **scratched** this closing exchange — the face's question
about walking Grommar's Path is **no longer canon**, and first contact with it is replayed in
[[ch02-s33]]. The description of the face, and Fabian's recollection of the [[grommar|Grommar]] legend,
still stand.

## Key facts learned
- **The gleaners are not straightforwardly goblins** (DM, confirmed) — goblin-like, pale, and about
  **one size smaller than a human**. They are not creatures of pure darkness (they use light and
  candlelight, and go up to the surface at night), so real sunlight would be a shock to them.
- **The gate is a bound earth elemental.** The archway across the chasm is not a door but a face that
  forms out of the rock — **an earth elemental** (Caspian, Arcana). Earth elementals can be **bound
  into service by magic**; one that habitually acts as a door is **probably compelled** to it by
  someone or something. Some are sentient. Who or what binds this one is **unknown**.
- **[[grommar|Grommar]]** (spelling confirmed by the DM) is a **semi-legendary,
  semi-historical dwarven smith** of very great skill who wanted to forge **a weapon that could kill
  death**, grew **extremely paranoid** and trap-happy in his later years, and **disappeared** before
  finishing. Accounts differ as to whether this was madness or genuine occult insight into the nature
  of death. The deep beyond the gate is named for him.
- **The river of churning stone carries more than this village.** Most of what it carries is smooth,
  much-churned old rock, plus fresh rubble and house-debris that is plainly Kethran's Fold — but also,
  occasionally, **worked pieces that could not have come from Kethran's Fold**: a column fragment, a
  marble floor tile. What they came from is **not established**.
- **There is a hidden courtyard off the cavern** — reached by a natural cleft, not a door — with
  porticoes that lead nowhere, murals resembling the [[the-broken-map|map mural]] above, and a large
  **gold-appearing dragon statue** that is undecayed while its surroundings rot. Its material and
  nature are **unverified** (a casual glance says gold).
- **The gleaners' name for the crate-creatures is "sweepers"** (DM clarification; S2.31 recorded them
  as "the sleepers" — they were sleeping, but "sweepers" is what the gleaners call them).
- **Mags has no prior knowledge of a place like this, nor of this "God"** (asked directly; no check
  judged worthwhile — the answer is no). Her ecumenical shrug is about **monotheism as a form** —
  faiths that call their deity "God" in the singular — and should not be read as recognition of what
  the gleaners describe.
