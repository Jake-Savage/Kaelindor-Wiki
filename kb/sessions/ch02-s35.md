## Recap
The session opens where S2.34 ended: everyone but [[rell-aetris|Rell]] in the water, Rell on the ledge,
the staircase behind them collapsed into a ramp. The walls of the pit are slick and very hard to climb;
the carving, with its footholds, is the easy route out, and the spiders are over it. The water is
**about forty feet deep**, warm, and opaque.

**The thing in the water.** A jaw closes on [[trevick|Trevick]]'s ankle almost immediately and drags him
under. What surfaces momentarily to grab [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] gives the party its first look: a
**hydra**, pale and unhealthy-looking, with **five** long necks and lizard- or snake-like heads full of
jagged teeth. It is strong, tough, and content to take its time — it does not bite with every head at
once, because it is not in a hurry.

**Getting off the bottom.** [[mags|Mags]] casts **Water Walk**, ducking under the surface first (and
taking an opportunity attack for it) to bring Trevick into range, and the party is lifted onto the
surface of the pool — Trevick excepted, since the hydra has hold of him, though he can feel the tug
upward. Caspian gets himself out with **Spider Climb**, carrying Rell's rope up the wall.
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]], finding he has no rope of his own and failing to throw one thirty feet up
to his unseen servant, throws up **Mirror Image** instead. Rell, on the ledge, anchors a rope with his
**climber's kit** and drops twenty-five feet of it into the pool.

**Getting Trevick back.** Trevick is submerged for most of the fight and gets to within a round of death
saving throws. His firebolt is spoiled by the water; his attempt to twist loose fails. Three things
break it:

- Rell repeatedly casts **Dissonant Whispers**, which forces the hydra to use its reaction to move away
  from him — dragging Trevick with it, but breaking its position each time.
- Mags casts **Daylight** straight down into the pool, which turns forty feet of opaque water into
  something the party can see shapes in. The hydra's silhouette becomes visible, and so does Trevick's.
- Caspian places a **fireball** underwater, aimed closer to Trevick than he would normally risk, on a
  strong Arcana read of how magical fire behaves down there. It does not boil anyone; it does make the
  hydra let go.

Fabian then puts his head — and all three mirror images — under the filthy water and **Commands** the
creature to *drop*, which settles it. Trevick pops to the surface prone, courtesy of Water Walk,
summons **[[hawk|Hawk]]** to watch the water, and climbs for the rope. Mags heals him with Healing Word.
Everyone gets out. The hydra, driven off but not killed, dives and stays in the pool, active and
irritated.

**What the light showed.** With the pool lit, [[trevick|Trevick]] pieces together what he saw of the
bottom. At one side of the chamber, debris has **accumulated as though drawn toward a point** — a drain
— and among the muck around it are **one or two shiny objects glinting** and **bones**: people have died
in this pit before, wearing good things. At the opposite side is an area **considerably clearer** than
the rest of the floor, which reads as water coming in. There are no strong currents, and the water is
largely still. Trevick offers to dive for the treasure with someone keeping the hydra off; Mags agrees
to back him, then both are talked out of it, and the party lets it go for now.

**The carving is a trap.** Rell climbs the carved chimney. Two-thirds of the way up, a mechanism
triggered by his presence on the climb whirs into life and **blades emerge from the slits** cut into the
carving. Not all of them still work — the damp has had centuries at them — but enough do to make the
climb lethal, and Rell's good roll rather than any judgement is what leaves him grazed instead of
pierced. The blades' opening makes the **spiders skitter and shift, as if looking**, but they never come
for him. **Rell's theory is proven**: their sense is **magical**, and his
[[amulet-of-proof-against-detection|amulet of proof against detection]] defeats it.
He also notes, passing close, that they are **rusting**, in poor repair, and — seen properly — **very
intricate, moving naturalistically**, better than he knows constructs to be made.

**The forge.** Over the lip at the top is **[[the-ancient-forge|a room of clearly dwarven origin]]**:
trays for pouring molten metal, tools scattered everywhere, everything in disarray, and **parts of the
walls buckled inward** under outside pressure. Nothing in it is operable; almost nothing not made of
stone survives a touch. The construct parts laid out are, even at a glance, **sophisticated**. Dwarven
temples commonly hold forges, and Rell's judgement is that this is the **temple complex proper** — and
that the corridor, the pool and this room were all **originally one structure**, since crushed out of
shape. **Two doors** lead deeper; a **significant crack** in one wall opens into natural fissures.

**The regalia, identified.** Over a short rest, Caspian ritually casts **Identify** twice on
[[the-draconic-regalia|the regalia]] from S2.33:

- **[[the-handlet|The handlet]]** — the chained hand-piece — was made for a **protector or bodyguard**.
  Its wearer may take on a saving throw for someone within five feet and, succeeding, extend that
  success to them, at the cost of exposing themselves to the effect. It **requires attunement**. Mags
  takes it, on the grounds that she backs her own saves above anyone else's, and it resizes to her hand.
- **[[the-dragon-crown|The crown]]** carries **traces of enchantment**, such that in **some
  circumstances** it may enhance **charisma-based checks** — circumstantial, and nobody can say what
  triggers it. Caspian passes it to Fabian. Wearing it through the rest, Fabian finds his thoughts
  beginning to express themselves in **Draconic**, the Draconic word arriving ahead of the common one.

**What they came for.** With a decision to make about going deeper, the party restates its own brief
and corrects itself doing it. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s version — that they were chasing cultists
and there is something culty down here — is walked back by [[rell-aetris|Rell]] to the accurate one:
they intercepted the [[the-furnace-brood|Brood]]'s communications, and the instruction was to go into
the space beneath the destroyed village and find **something that should not be there**. That
instruction never named the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] itself. Rell's reading is that the
Stonehunger has swallowed things from across its range and its history, and that the Brood's object may
be one of them rather than the Stonehunger. [[mags|Mags]] counters that
[[the-golden-courtyard|the courtyard]] may already **be** the thing that should not be there. The party
commits to going deeper.

**Clearing the way up.** Caspian, having prepared it over the short rest, moves under the canopy and
directs an upcast **Shatter** upward. Old brittle stone and old brittle constructs both give: the
spiders come down, the **canopy collapses into the water**, and Caspian jumps clear of the falling
masonry. The cost is the climb — the upper carving is now **badly damaged and crumbly**, so its surface
can no longer be climbed, while the **blade mechanism is undamaged** and some slots are more exposed
than before. Rell rigs the rope to hang clear of the wall and ties **loops** into it as a ladder.
Trevick falls off it twice and takes blade damage on the wall; Fabian struggles and restarts; Mags goes
up cleanly. Everyone reaches the forge. The session ends with the party intending a **long rest** there
before going through one of the two doors. It is about **eight or nine in the evening**.

## Key facts learned
- **The thing in the pool is a five-headed hydra** — pale, unhealthy-looking, lizard- or snake-like
  heads with jagged teeth. Tough, high-constitution, grabs and drags rather than tearing. **It is still
  alive and still in the pool**, and will go for anyone who re-enters the water.
- **The pool is about forty feet deep**, with slick walls, and its floor carries a **drain** on one side
  (debris drawn toward it, with **bones and shiny objects** in the silt — people have died here) and a
  **clearer patch** opposite where water comes in. No strong currents; the water is largely still —
  **now**, which the DM was careful to say does not mean always.
- **The carved chimney is a blade trap**, triggered by climbing it. Age and rust have disabled some of
  the blades; enough still work that the climb is decided by luck, not judgement.
- **[[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s amulet theory is proven.** The spiders ignored him completely while he
  climbed and stood beneath them, reacting to the blades but never to him: their means of detection is
  **magical**, and the [[amulet-of-proof-against-detection|amulet of proof against detection]] defeats
  it. The party now has a reliable way to move past magically-sensing guardians — for one person.
- **The spiders were rusting and in poor repair**, and there were a great many of them, piled up beyond
  counting. An upcast **Shatter** destroyed them all.
- **They were made better than constructs are normally made.** Close up they were **very intricate**
  with **naturalistic** movement, to a standard Rell is not aware of constructs reaching (see
  [[constructs]]).
- **Above the pool is [[the-ancient-forge|a dwarven forge]]** — the temple complex proper. Buckled
  walls, scattered tools, **sophisticated construct parts**, nothing operable, everything very old. Two
  doors lead deeper, and a crack in one wall opens into natural fissures.
- **The corridor, the pool and the forge were originally one structure** (DM, confirmed), since crushed
  out of shape.
- **[[the-handlet|The handlet]] was made for a protector or bodyguard**: extend your own successful
  saving throw to one person within five feet, taking on the risk yourself. Requires attunement. Worn by
  Mags.
- **[[the-dragon-crown|The crown]] carries enchantment magic** that circumstantially enhances
  charisma-based checks — triggers unknown — and **turns its wearer's thoughts toward Draconic**. Worn
  by Fabian.
- **Magical fire works underwater** in a way ordinary fire does not: a fireball ignites and damages, but
  does not expand to full radius and does not boil the surrounding water (DM ruling).
- **The time:** the party went down to the landslide in the late afternoon and entered the complex in
  the early evening; it is now roughly **eight or nine at night** on 30 Dusklin.

  complex proper, wrecking the carving behind it in the process.
