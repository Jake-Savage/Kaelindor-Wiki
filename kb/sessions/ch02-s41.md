## Recap

**Holding still.** The convoy is halted on [[the-plain|the plain]], lights out, sitting tight around
[[trevick|Trevick]] while his last Pass Without Trace runs down. [[mags|Mags]], [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]
and [[rell-aetris|Rell]] are the rearguard, with [[hawk|Hawk]] aloft; everyone is still linked through
Rell's telepathy.

The thing that came out of [[dunton|Dunton]] the party now calls **the bone swarm**. It is **vast** —
even the part glimpsed through Hawk's and [[theodore|Theodore]]'s senses — and it sounds like a storm
driving sticks and debris down a street, on an enormous scale. It smells of **dry bone**, a dusty smell
rather than corpses.

The **patrol** from [[the-keep|the Keep]] is a few hundred feet from the convoy near some ruined
buildings. It carries **no torches** but gives off a faint **magical, reddish glow**. The rearguard's
plan, Rell's, is to set the patrol and the swarm on each other.

**The diversions.** Mags builds the firebomb and Hawk carries it off, under instructions over the bond,
to drop between the patrol and Rell's first flare. Rell's own attempts at more firebombs go badly —
much of the oil leaks away. Rell's first flare is **snuffed or burns out** as the swarm reaches it, and
for a time Caspian hears the swarm **stop moving forward**. Hawk drops his bomb — a fist-sized fire,
visible but small — and the swarm moves on again.

At the convoy, [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] keeps lights off with Prestidigitation and tries to buoy the
villagers. His practical advice convinces nobody, and a story of a hero leading his people out of
oppression only half works: the children listen, and others begin to think this idiot has led them into
a terrible situation. Trevick's quieter instructions — stay low and silent, he has done this before
among larger creatures — settle them.

**Something watching.** Fabian feels, suddenly, **a prickling sensation of being watched**. Mags
suspects **scrying**; the others are unconvinced. Whether anyone was watching is unknown.

**Voices.** Trevick hears **an exchange of words** from near where Hawk's bomb fell, in **a language he
does not speak** — not friendly, not confrontational. The party infers the patrol was talking to
**Lae'ethorian**.

**Hawk and the swarm.** Hawk, keeping watch near the flare, gets a close look as the swarm comes on:

- It is **crescent-shaped**, its wings **loose bones caught in a swirling wind**.
- At its centre is **a dark mass**, impenetrable even to Hawk's darkvision.
- Around the bones is **a penumbra** — gloomy and hazy — that reaches **beyond the bones themselves**.
- Near it the air turns **very cold** and tastes sharply **metallic**.

Hawk is nearly drawn into the penumbra and only just climbs clear. Had he been caught he would have been
pummelled to pieces.

**Silence.** The rearguard comes back toward the convoy, Caspian **invisible**. Mags casts **Silence**
over the villagers so they can pack and stand without noise — and because, if Fabian is being scried, it
may deafen whoever is listening. Rell circles east to scout ahead and lays another timed flare. Trevick
scouts a way north-east and finds it **choked with rocks, debris and ruined buildings**.

**The argument.** Caspian, outside the silence, hears the swarm coming **fast**, and the temperature
dropping. He tells Mags they have made a **terrible mistake**: people are going to die, and if Greyface
kills them out here **he will grow more powerful from their deaths**. His proposal is to **let him take
the villagers back**. Mags says the told-you-so has been noted. Rell argues the opposite — Greyface has
eked these people out over twelve years and will not want to **waste the whole village** in one go, so
he will come for the party and spare them. The party settles on **standing with the villagers**, and on
telling them the truth: fight with us, or run.

**The stand.** Mags drops the Silence and the noise of the bones is suddenly close. Fabian makes the
speech of his life — a monumentous decision, your place among the great men and women of this
subterranean civilisation, stand between fear and your loved ones — and in the pitch dark it falls flat;
people are **breaking, running and bursting into tears** as he speaks. Mags casts **Daylight**. The
villagers, who have not seen anything like it in years, are blinded and disoriented. In the light the
party sees **an enormous wall of bones**, **twenty to twenty-five feet high**, floating a couple of feet
off the ground, **splitting in two**: the western half, with the **dark pulsing orb** at its heart,
straight at them; the other swinging round from the south.

- **Mags** raises the symbol of [[naelos|Naelos]] — *"thou shalt not pass."* There is no fear in the
  bones, nothing of the response she knows from ordinary undead; it is a raw blow of power against a
  wall. But **some of the ancient bones crumble to dust**, and two sections of the southern swarm are
  hurt.
- **The western swarm rolls over Mags and Fabian** and the villagers around them. Mags and Fabian stay
  on their feet, with a couple of others. **Most of the villagers caught under it are killed or knocked
  senseless and drawn up into the swirl**, and it does not let them go; they become part of the swarm. A
  villager fires a crossbow at it.
- **Caspian** judges the orb a magical effect and **dispels it** — high-level magic, to his own
  astonishment. Inside, on **a platform of bones** about fifteen feet up, sits **a humanoid figure**,
  untouched by the storm around him, looking for whatever did that. Caspian ducks behind a rock.
- **Trevick** marks him and shoots; the bones defeat him.
- **Rell**, invisible, sprints in, **springs off a villager's shoulders** and dives **head first through the bones**, taking cuts, onto the platform. The figure
  is **an elf**, and it is **not the grey-faced puppet**. Rell throws himself on him and the two of them
  **tumble off the platform** — and do not hit the ground: the swarm carries them, tumbling, inside it.
- **Fabian**, inside the swarm, lies back and blasts **Thunderwave** from [[the-golden-lute|the golden
  lute]] at the elf and Rell, scattering bones into shards and opening a gap, and scrambles out. He
  drinks a potion.
- **Mags** calls up **Spirit Guardians** as great shining moths of Naelos. Inside the swarm the two
  magics clash head-on — the opposed powers Greyface demonstrated in the Ossuary — and the effect is
  volatile. She holds it. Then something happens she did not expect: when the bodies of villagers the
  swarm has taken drift into her guardians, she feels their **souls gathered in and sheltered** from
  the swarm's necromancy — the newly dead, whose souls have not yet passed on. She gives up chasing the
  elf to gather them, and **shelters seven**.
- **The elf turns on Rell.** He makes no effort to break free; he **clings to Rell** and pours
  **necromantic power** into him, indiscriminately — not like being wounded, but **profound cold and
  profound isolation**, and Rell senses the damage will be **very hard to undo** if it goes on. Rell is
  brought to the edge of death but held conscious by the link. The elf regains command of the swarm,
  thickens the bones around himself, and is **furious — with an air of righteousness**.
- **Caspian's Magic Missile** does not break his hold.
- **Trevick's arrow does**, through three-quarters cover of bone. The elf throws up a magical shield;
  the arrow gets through anyway, and **the flow into Rell is cut**. Rell, falling unconscious, locks his
  grip tighter, and a bone takes him in the neck.
- **The southern swarm comes in** and runs into Mags's guardians. This time she **cannot hold** them;
  they buckle and fail. But the souls she sheltered **flow into her** — nothing visible — and she knows
  she is **carrying them within herself**, for as long as she stays whole, conscious and faithful. That
  swarm takes **three more villagers**.
- **Fabian** shepherds the children behind the big rock and declares to the elf that by turning on the
  people he swore to protect he has become **cursed**; the curse does not take. He inspires Trevick.
- **Mags** tries to **hold him** in the name of Naelos, the god of order; he resists.

**He leaves.** The elf — **Lae'ethorian**, and the party knows him for it — weighs what he came for against what has
happened, and **withdraws**. The swarm **reverses** and sweeps back across the plain. As it goes,
Caspian dispels a clean patch of it **beneath Rell**, who drops out onto the stone. Mags stabilises him
and heals him fully.

**The cost.** **Twenty of the thirty-four villagers are alive**, and the dog; **fourteen are dead**.
**Every child survives**, though six of them lost a parent and Daisy Thorngage both. Of the two
long-term survivors, Mothrigg Tosscobble lives and Oshin Half-toes does not. **[[varen|Varen]]** the
innkeeper is dead, and [[kithri|Kithri]]'s son Ned. **[[the-provost|The Provost]] was consumed** — and with her, the knowledge of where
the tunnels are. Whether she is among the seven Mags sheltered, Mags cannot tell. Fabian gathers the
survivors. Many of them, the party learns, think **Rell is a hero**. **The old dog survives.** The patrol is still out there; the
swarm is still audible, moving away.

## Key facts learned
- **Greyface came himself.** The bone swarm is directed by **an elf** sitting on a throne of bones
  inside it — **Lae'ethorian** in his own body, not the puppet.
- **The swarm:** a crescent of loose bones in a swirling wind, 20–25 ft high, floating; a **magical
  darkness** at its heart that darkvision cannot pierce; a cold, metallic, gloomy **penumbra** beyond
  the bones; it can **split into two**; it **holds** those inside it, takes the dead and the senseless
  up into itself, and **cannot be escaped without a struggle**.
- **Turning does not frighten it**, but the power of Naelos still **destroys** some of its bones.
- **Naelos's guardians and the swarm's magic are opposed**, and meeting head-on is volatile.
- **Spirit Guardians can shelter the newly dead** from necromancy; **Mags now carries seven souls**.
- **His necromancy drains in a particular way** — cold, isolating, and lasting.
- **He has a magical shield**, and the darkness in the swarm was **high-level magic**.
- **Daylight does not trouble him.**
- **The patrol's light is a faint reddish magical glow**, and its members speak a language Trevick
  does not know.
- **Fourteen villagers are dead**, the Provost and Varen among them; **twenty survive**, all seven
  children among them, with the dog. The full roll is on [[dunton]].
