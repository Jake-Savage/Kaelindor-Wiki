## Recap

**Breakfast with the Provost.** The party sleeps late, comes down into
[[ketters-rest|Ketter's Rest]], and finds **[[the-provost|the Provost]]** waiting with half a dozen
villagers. She is **sixty-five or so, severe**, and wearing **battered but well-kept armour**. She is
not delighted they are here — not angry, but plainly reading them as **trouble she does not need**.

[[mags|Mags]] lays out the party's rations as a gesture of welcome; the Provost asks whether they mean
to **contribute** them, sweeps up **all but a mouthful** for the common store, and ceremonially tops up
what is left so everyone present gets a bite and the courtesy is satisfied. [[dunton|Dunton]] runs on
**strict redistribution by need**, and its people are poor but not hungry.

She gives her name once — *"my name was, in the world above, **Yafet Em**, but that has not been
relevant for many years. You may call me Provost"* — and then lays out the place:

- **They are [[black-barrow|Black Barrow]]'s people**, a Mirrowen village the Stonehunger took **twelve
  years ago**. **Forty-two** came down with [[the-high-priest|Greyface]]; **nineteen** have died since,
  mostly out beyond the palisade.
- **They were not first.** They found **four people already here**, underground **fifteen or sixteen
  years** before them and *"three quarters mad, at least."* **Two are still alive**, incoherent, after
  something close to a **quarter of a century** below.
- **Until this party, no survivors had ever come through.**
- Beyond the palisade is **[[the-plain|the plain]]** — dark, scattered with deposited masonry, its
  features **never quite in the same place twice** — and beyond that **[[the-keep|the Keep]]**, which
  **stands above and guards the lower levels** she has never seen and does not wish to.
- **"This is a clearinghouse. A processing centre."** These are *"things not yet fully absorbed, or
  things that cannot be fully absorbed."* There are **other places intact, protected**.
- **Large insect- or crab-like creatures** roam the plain in packs: magical but not constructs, able to
  see in the dark, avoidable with care, edible at a pinch, and the main cause of death here.
- **No contact with [[the-gleaners|the gleaners]]**, who are hostile and numerous enough to destroy
  Dunton outright.

**On Greyface.** Her lip gives a **wry twist** at the title. She does not take "High Priest" entirely
at face value, and is **respectful up to a point** and no further.

- He came to Black Barrow **as a traveller with a guide**, said he had **business further south**, and
  stayed a few nights. Then the village was taken. *"It was a strange coincidence."*
- A young man who was there fills it in: a great noise at dawn, most of the village buried at once,
  and **Greyface in the street** telling the survivors to go **in rather than run** — into the lee of
  the slip. He worked magic that sheltered them and then **cut a way through**, and a little way down
  it opened into a great cavern.
- **He was not dead then.** *"No, he wasn't dead — although by all accounts he was an unusual man even
  then."*
- He has made **"a sort of accommodation, one that buys us peace"**; without it they would not have
  lasted. *"He does have requests. But he has moderated—"*
- He **heals**, **conjures food out of rock and bone**, lit the village with his enchantments, and
  **keeps folk busy** with **projects — "study, he calls it, although what he's studying I have never
  quite had the inclination to ask."**
- **"He is not the only priest. In the levels below, there are very, very many of them. We see them
  upon the plain sometimes."**
- **There is a spirit of the stone here and he commands it** — though a villager remembers that *"that
  wasn't his servant always… he didn't always have such command of it."*

She **declines** a commemoration of Dunton's dead in [[naelos|Naelos]]'s name, offered by
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] on [[mags|Mags]]'s behalf: *"such a thing could be awkward."* The
inhabitants of the lower levels **regard their Godhead as all around them**, and Greyface has told her
it would be **imprudent to obviously contradict that**. *"I maintain a neutrality."*

Asked by [[rell-aetris|Rell]] why surface survivors of these collapses come out so **changed**, she
confirms the few who escaped Black Barrow were shaken — and volunteers something about herself. She was
away from home; she went back to look for her dead; *"but I don't know that I would have so eagerly
walked into the bowels of the earth as I did, there not being some sort of an influence. **There is a
strangeness.**"*

**[[kithri|Kithri]]'s house.** Following up a remark about villagers making things for Greyface, the
party visits the needleworker. Her house is full of **extraordinarily opulent cloth** — nobleman's
stuff, nothing like it in Black Barrow or, she thinks, [[greywatch|Greywatch]] — worked to **detailed
patterns he supplies**. [[mags|Mags]] finds **no evidence anything like it could be made down here**.

She is a few weeks into **a manticore banner**, in the same style as **the bull banners the party saw
in [[the-buried-arena|the arena]]** — *"we finished those a little while back; for a while he was mad
about bulls"* — and **others have been working scorpion tails**, a design he currently favours. Banners
run to monsters; robes and cloaks to trees and nature.

[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] draws her a plausible-looking fake glyph. She does not do such work on
cloth — *"it needs to be stone for this sort of work"* — but she **recognises it**: *"they're part of a
boundary."* **Beyond the palisade there are two lines of them, great half circles**, and they **were
there before her people arrived**.

Then [[mags|Mags]] asks whether the robes are always the same size, observes that these are **not robes
for one person**, and — establishing that nobody has told Kithri to keep quiet — gets the answer the
whole session turns on, offered as though everyone knew it:

> *"Young Roscoe. You've met Greyface. **The body he is using at present.**"*

- The bodies **deteriorate**; when there is an accident he **"takes the opportunity to renew things."**
- **[[roscoe|Roscoe]]** was a villager killed **less than a year ago** by one of the plain's creatures —
  stabbed through the lung, carried home by his friends, dead before anything could be done. The body
  before him lasted **six or seven years**.
- *"No one begrudges him. But still, it's strange. It feels strange."*

Kithri also gives the fullest account of the **first weeks underground**: Greyface **shut himself in
[[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]** and told them to stay behind the ward signs; **people went in and did not
come back**; and then **the Provost arrived six weeks late, alone and bloodied, and walked straight in
after them.** *"I think she told him what was what. Because after that, things got different."* No more
disappearances, and an **understanding — an exchange**. From then on, **when someone died, they took
them to the Ossuary — but not before that.**

**What the party knows about elves and bodies.** [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]], [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]
and [[rell-aetris|Rell]] all cast back. Caspian and Rell have heard the **commonplace rumour that
Elvish armies practise necromancy**. Fabian, through military friends and relatives, has the real
version: the **Erathian League** and the **Elvish Empire** are largely frozen in direct contact, but
there are still engagements — and there is **serious concern about bodies left behind**, with a
standing expectation that armies **burn their own dead** rather than leave them to be recovered. The
fear is **not primarily infiltration**: it is that a recovered body yields **information**, and that
the magic worked on it might be used to **harm or influence the people that person was close to**.
(See [[elvish-necromancy]].)

Fabian shows Kithri **[[the-furnace-brood|Brood]] iconography**. She finds all of it unpleasant and
**recognises none of it**.

**[[the-ossuary|The Ossuary]].** Word comes that Greyface wants to see them, which suits the party. The
villagers walk them to the passage and leave them to go in alone.

It is **obviously a temple** — grand, large, altars for offerings — but its symbols are **recognised by
nobody**, and it resembles nothing else on the way down. **One route through is lit with candles.** The
bones are **all broadly humanoid but very diverse** in size, skull shape and thickness, **sorted by
type** rather than assembled into skeletons. They are **very old and visibly held together by magic**;
**none are new**, and none show obvious violence. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] judges them placed with
**great intentionality**, and the structure **tuned to promote certain kinds of magic** the way a
[[calvyr|Calvyr]] temple is.

At the end is a chamber with **a great chair of bones on a dais**, unoccupied, and — added later, off
to one side — a **human-scale** set of practical bone chairs with **cloths on the walls**. Greyface
stands to greet them from one of them.

**Greyface talks.** He is markedly warmer than the night before. [[mags|Mags]] finds **no obvious
deception**, but reads him as a man who **always has projects**.

- The Ossuary was **not built down here** — *"certainly wrought down a long time ago"* — and survived
  because *"like any place of worship and spiritual power, [it is] not possible to be entirely absorbed
  or ingested."* He **did not know it was here**: *"a very lucky find. It has served me extremely
  well."*
- **He had no plan.** *"I honestly didn't have too much of a plan when I first came down here. I was a
  little desperate, if I'm perfectly honest."* It took time to understand what was happening — and,
  plucking at his robe, **"to understand the proper attitude that I would need to adopt in order to
  make myself acceptable."**
- **He is an exile, lying low, and not free to come and go.** *"It is lonely."*
- **He was educated in the principles of his craft from a very young age** — and will not say by whom.
- **He confirms the surrogates.** *"The use of surrogates, such as this, protect me from some of the
  worst side effects of this art,"* and *"yes, I employ surrogates such as this to distance myself a
  little."* To the party the magic reads as **clearly powerful, and more than that, very weird** —
  unlike anything any of them has heard of anyone doing.
- **He takes [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s arm**, pushes the sleeve up and touches the **bare skin of his
  forearm and wrist** with a cold dead hand, holds it a few seconds, and says: *"Ah, yes. That makes
  sense. That makes sense."* And then: **"It seems we have a little more in common than I had
  suspected."** [[mags|Mags]] looks for kinship in it and finds that is not the feeling.
- **He weighs them openly.** *"The temptation to keep you here is quite strong"* — then, on being
  refused: *"Last night, I could have killed you quite easily. Today… at your full strength, I think
  you might give me trouble. And even if I tried to keep one or two of you and send the others away —
  well, **my position here is probably weaker than it looks**. I have no desire to make trouble for
  either of us. That is part of the reason I think that you should leave."*
- **There is a clock.** The place will **submerge again — "days, a week perhaps"** — which he reads off
  **the timing of the tremors** and **how the gleaners are behaving**. It has moved and submerged in
  the twelve years since Black Barrow, but **this is the first time in that span it has surfaced**.
- **He has never seen a [[korrathian|Korrathian]].** *"Of all the troubles I have considered,
  Karathians were not amongst them… I have been visited by none of the servants of Karath."* It would
  be **a departure for them**, he thinks — though if they learned of this place they might take an
  interest, *"because magic is more complicated than most of you think that it is."* There are **at
  least three or four levels below this one**, and possibly whole areas he has never considered.

**What the Stonehunger is.** The largest single account the party has been given:

> *"God is a living thing. It's very old. **It's very damaged.** I think in its prime, it would have
> taken all that it ingested and it would have made them into something strange and extraordinary and
> terrible. There is a great hunger in it — a great frustration that it is no longer able to do
> anything of what it once did."*

- **It can be communicated with**, and he does. *"It has a will. It has a sense of purpose. It has not
  too much more than that now — but I think it was perhaps not fully sentient, but possessed of
  powerful instincts that may once have made it something close to reasoning."*
- **"It was imprisoned a long, long time ago, along with its fellows, and it broke out."**
- It is **full of rage, spite and malevolence** and would eat anything — *"but it's far too weak to do
  more than consume the odd village. It can't break out of [[the-mirrowen|the Mirrowen]], perhaps not
  even quite a small area within it."*
- **It takes him for one of its own** — part of its cult. *"If it understood what I am, what I'm doing
  here, I don't think I would be in a position."* Could he stand against it? *"Perhaps. I would not
  like to test it."*
- **It senses, but not everywhere and not always.** He can usually feel when its awareness is near,
  *"although I've been wrong a time or two."*
- **"It has certain spirits of the earth which it uses as its eyes and ears. The one that had custody
  of this area I have made tame."**
- **It cannot digest holy ground, and he does not know why.** Which is why the Ossuary stands — and why
  [[the-ancient-forge|the forge shrine]] stood until, as he tells Caspian to his face, *"you rather
  **carelessly undid that protection**."* He is not sorry: *"it gave me the opportunity to conduct my
  little experiment. You might have been rather more resistant to persuasion otherwise."* His advice
  now: **"if you find holy places of any sort, I would let them be. They are sanctuaries in this, in a
  very real sense here."**
- **Who imprisoned it?** *"An interesting question, and not one I have been able to discover the answer
  to."* He dates it, patting the temple wall, to **around the time this place was built**.

**[[the-balance|The balance]].** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] asks about the sliding scale from radiant to
necrotic and Greyface corrects him, then cannot stop himself:

> *"Magic is not a scale from good to evil. **It is a balance.** On the one hand, vitality, life,
> energy of a generative sort. On the other, unlife, death — but not death as the priests of
> [[naelos|Naelos]] would have it. Destruction, stifling, iron control. And between them,
> **[[itharis|Itharis]]**, the pivot point, the balance."*

He catches himself — *"I should not be saying any of this, and certainly not to someone who is going to
go back to Crownspire"* — and says it anyway: **"your understanding is woefully out of balance. And so
is ours. And that is going to be a problem for us all."** Asked how, he answers that without an
understanding of **both sides of the scale** there will be **no opportunity to even try to change
anything, to fix anything** — and then collapses out of it entirely: *"But I made my peace with this a
long time ago, and there's no point in any of this."* He goes **flatly fatalist**.

[[mags|Mags]]'s religion check half-places the doctrine: she has heard **Itharis called the axis or
pivot point** in Itharian contexts, but either does not know, cannot remember, or was never told **what
the goddess is the pivot between**.

[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] argues him out of it — that as a wizard he of all people should know **anything
is capable of change; that that is the point, and the fun of it** — and the figure visibly **pulls
himself together**, called back to something he clearly once believed.

**The session ends there, before his reply.**

## Key facts learned
- **[[the-provost|The Provost]] is Yafet Em**, a Black Barrow survivor who came down **six weeks after
  the others**, alone and fighting, and **ended the Ossuary disappearances by walking in after them**.
- **[[dunton|Dunton]]: forty-two came down twelve years ago; nineteen have died.** Four earlier
  survivors were found already here; **two are still alive** after nearly a quarter of a century.
- **Dunton's dead go to the Ossuary.** That is the exchange that followed the Provost's intervention.
- **Greyface is wearing [[roscoe|Roscoe]]**, a villager killed under a year ago. **The whole village
  knows**, and nobody begrudges it. Bodies deteriorate; the previous one lasted six or seven years.
- **He was a living human when he came to [[black-barrow|Black Barrow]]** as a traveller, days before
  it fell, and **his face became elf-like during the disaster** when he began working serious magic.
- **He confirms the surrogates himself**, and says they **protect him from the worst side effects of
  the art**.
- **He is an exile**, lying low, not free to come and go, **educated in his craft from a very young
  age**, and will not say where from.
- **The [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] is a damaged living thing** — once able to transform everything
  it swallowed, now too weak to do more than eat the odd village — **imprisoned long ago along with its
  fellows, and escaped**. Nobody knows by whom.
- **It can be communicated with**; it has **will and purpose** but is not fully sentient.
- **Its "eyes of God" are literally its sense organs** — earth spirits it uses to see and hear. The one
  at [[the-buried-arena|the arena]] has been **tamed** by Greyface.
- **It cannot digest concentrations of holy power.** That is why [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] and
  [[the-ancient-forge|the forge shrine]] survived — and the party **undid the forge's protection**,
  which Greyface says plainly and finds convenient.
- **The clock: it will submerge again within days or a week.** This is the first time it has surfaced
  in the twelve years since Black Barrow.
- **[[the-plain|The plain]]** is dark, shifting, scattered with deposited masonry and roamed by large
  magical insect-like creatures; **[[the-keep|the Keep]]** lies across it and guards the **lower
  levels**, of which there are at least three or four.
- **Two great half-circle ward-lines lie beyond the palisade** and **predate Dunton's arrival**.
- **[[kithri|Kithri]] and others make Greyface's banners and robes** from opulent cloth that could not
  be produced down here — **the arena's bulls were theirs**, a **manticore** is in progress and
  **scorpion tails** are the current favourite.
- **No Korrathian has ever been down here**, per Greyface. The party's own brief never claimed
  otherwise — they are the Brood's agents, so any sniffing around is theirs.
- **[[the-balance|Greyface's theory of magic]]**: not a moral scale but a balance of generative life
  against unlife and iron control, with **[[itharis|Itharis]] as the pivot** — and everyone's
  understanding of it, **his own side's included**, badly out of true.
- **Elvish armies are rumoured to practise battlefield necromancy**, and the Erathian League expects its
  armies to **burn their own dead** rather than let bodies be recovered.
