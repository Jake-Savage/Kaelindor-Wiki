## Recap

**The forge, before the rest.** The party is in the rooms above the shattered carvings, in
[[the-ancient-forge|the ancient forge]]. Looking around, they find that **other people have camped
here** — fires lit on the floor, a little low-value debris — not in the ancient past but within
**months and years**. There is no sign of a struggle and no blood; whoever they were, they packed up
and went. The complex here is **three large rooms**: the **western** one the party climbed into, the
**southern** one holding an archway onto a spiral staircase down, and the **northern** one with a
hatch in its ceiling. Other original exits are visible in the walls, each **filled with rock pressed
forward into the doorway**.

[[trevick|Trevick]] searches what the campers left and finds nothing, but does find the **hatch** —
concealed once, no longer — and the **upper chamber** above it. It is the smallest room and much the
most ornate: **[[calvyr|Calvyran]] devotional reliefs** in a traditional dwarven style, the stone
beneath them stained by **incense** burnt there over a long time. These were working sites of prayer.
Something has been **taken out of it recently** — straight, relatively fresh scrape marks where things
were pulled from the walls, and a few **scraps of paper** where written material was stored. Trevick
gathers the scraps for [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]. **They are blank.** Either they are magical and have
survived the age of the place, or they are much more recent; nobody tests which.

**Making camp.** The party beds down in the upper chamber, on [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s point that it has
only one entrance. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] casts **Leomund's Tiny Hut**. [[mags|Mags]] inscribes a
**Glyph of Warding** on the hut's floor — negotiating the trigger from "anyone who hurts a member of
the party" down to a workable definition of harm, and loading it with a **Guiding Bolt** — then
declares herself too old for watches and sleeps. Rell rigs **bells and string** in the room below.
[[trevick|Trevick]] proposes pulling his three remaining
**[[nameweave-crackers|crackers]]** and is talked out of it. Watches are set: Caspian, Trevick, Rell,
Fabian.

**First watch.** Caspian, by his own admission so confident in the hut that he barely looks up from his
notes, sees nothing.

**Second watch.** Gravel begins pattering down from the ceiling, and Rell's bells start to chime —
not as though anyone has tripped them, but softly and repeatedly. Looking down through the hatch,
Trevick sees a **fine tracery of cracks** across the floor of the northern room, which was pristine
when they came up, and hears heavier grinding from the **western** room. He sends **[[hawk|Hawk]]**
ahead on a prearranged signal — two squawks for a physical entity — and gets two. He then creeps down
past Rell's strings himself and looks.

The **western room's wall is moving**, and the cracks are spreading out from that movement. At the
broken lip of the carvings, under a crust of stone, is **something whitish and almost gelatinous**,
alive, pushing the stone out of its way. It is **not a creature inside the rock**: the impression is
that **the stone is its skin**, cracking open as the thing shifts beneath. It is squeezing the chamber
inward, slowly, but fast enough to watch.

Trevick wakes **Rell** rather than the camp. They agree a threshold — move when it reaches the rooms
above, before their options are cut off — and Trevick sits out the rest of his watch caught between not
wanting to alert it and wanting to touch it.

**Third watch.** Rell takes his watch down in the forge itself with a **small fire** lit at the edge of
the chamber, on the reasoning that animals fear fire, and takes down his bells. Less than half an hour
in, **one wall of the western room collapses**, loudly enough to wake everyone, briefly exposing a
large patch of the white substance before the stone crusts back over it.

The party argues it out. Rell reports what it is — *"I would assume this is the Stonehunger... well,
its physical form"* — and nobody disputes him, though it remains **his inference**. Caspian presses
hardest for leaving: the room may be safe, but a safe room is worth nothing once every exit is gone.
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] raises the one attempt at an explanation anyone offers — that they may be
**protected** in the shrine chamber, as the warded [[the-golden-dragon-statue|dragon statue]] in
[[the-golden-courtyard|the courtyard]] was protected. The upper chamber has taken **no crack, no dust
and no movement** all night, and the DM confirms its architecture is otherwise no different from the
rest.

They settle on carrying on a little longer and reassessing at the change of watch. Shortly after, the
**other side of the western room goes too**, in a cloud of dust — closing the route back down to
[[the-foul-pool|the pool]] for good — and the **northern room** begins the same process, its floor
tiles buckling upward from below. Rell raises the alarm and the party moves.

**Studying it.** Before leaving, Rell examines an exposed patch in the southern room. The stony skin is
**dry**; the exposed moving part is **warm and sticky**, and traces come away on his fingers. He takes
a **smear onto leather and seals it in an empty potion vial**. Bringing a brand **close** produces no
reaction; bringing it into **contact** makes it fizzle, quickens its movement, and the substance
**spreads in a thin layer across the torch and puts it out**. [[mags|Mags]] reads that as
**intentionality**; Rell answers that it **could be purely reactive**. Neither is settled, and the
noise from that direction increases afterwards.

Rell then casts **Detect Thoughts** on it, looking only for whether there is intelligence there. He
gets a brief brush and pulls back at once. There **is** something that could be described as a mind;
it is **extremely strange**, and **vast and old and not like anything else he has encountered**; he can read no detail
from it, and is left in no doubt that a deeper connection would be a bad idea.

**Down the staircase.** The party takes a **short rest** — perhaps five hours' sleep for those who
slept through, and no long rest — and goes down the spiral stair from the southern room, with the
sounds of destruction continuing behind them. The staircase is **not original**: plainer, workmanlike
masonry, taken from a building and positioned here rather than cut from the rock, and on the way down
its style changes at points — **several staircases pitched together**.

It ends at a **dead end of raw stone**. No collapse, no writing, no pressure pads — but Caspian's
investigation turns up **faint ripple patterns in the rock** of exactly the kind
[[the-custodian|the elemental at the gate to Grommar's Path]] left when it shaped and reshaped its own
entryway hours earlier. Something has worked this stone the same way.

**The voice.** Mags knocks and calls out. **[[the-high-priest|A voice]]** answers — **clear and bright,
seeming to come from the air** above them rather than from within or behind the stone, and **not** the
custodian's voice. *"Well, well, visitors, you are most welcome. Give me a second."*

Minutes pass. Fabian raps a rhythm on the wall and observes that where he comes from one does not leave
visitors waiting at the door; the party adds that they bring news. Then the stone begins to move, and
the voice speaks again, less declamatory: *"That wasn't so hard. I knew you could do it with the proper
incentive."* The stone parts **slowly and with evident effort** into a plain gateway. The voice comes
more sharply — *"Come now, you can do better than that"* — and the gateway widens and takes on
something of the **grandeur** of the arch onto [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]], simulated columns and
an arched top.

**[[the-buried-arena|The arena]].** Beyond is the first **lit** place the party has found down here:
torches burning in braziers around an **amphitheatre**. It is patched together — roughly **70% one
structure**, the rest odds and ends added on to make it grander, with statuary crammed in and seating
that does not match. **Banners** hang around the edges and are **not old or threadbare**. A **bull
mosaic** is set into the floor, and nobody recognises the iconography. A **pit** opens in the middle,
its old covering **grill** mostly rusted away. Above the walls, the rock **bulges over the top as
though it had flowed across and settled around the place** — with no sign of movement now.

The **stone door shuts behind them**. Fabian thanks the voice aloud and asks whether there is anyone to
talk to; **no reply comes**.

The floor is muddy and wet, with an unusual **oily sheen**. Caspian reads it as **more biological than
geological** — something lurking below the mud — and warns everyone off it. Mags walks an illusory
rabbit across it with **Minor Illusion** and nothing reacts; Caspian throws a stone, and Mags, watching
closely, catches the mud **rising to meet it** and engulfing it rather than letting it sink. Testing
the edges, they find an **invisible barrier**: smooth to the touch, wholly transparent, stopping
movement and splashing spells off it like a wall, and enclosing the **whole arena including the far
staircase**. There is no way on but through the mud.

**The minotaur.** Sounds rise from the pit. Caspian casts **Haste** on Trevick; Rell uses a **scroll of
Blink**; Fabian puts on **[[the-dragon-crown|the crown]]**; Mags calls down the hole with **Speak with
Animals** and gets nothing.

What climbs out is a **very large undead minotaur**, ten to twelve feet tall, wielding no weapon but
possessed of unusually long horns and claws. Rell judges it **essentially mindless** from the state of
its decay. Buried in the matted fur of its chest, with the undead skin **grown closed around it**, is
**[[the-embedded-axe|a very large dwarven axe]]** — which Mags reads as a **holy thing**, and
**Calvyran**, two-handed and made for a strong warrior. Asked whether it is hurting the creature, she
is told it looks **angry and in pain** — with the caveat that the party has been hitting it and the
causality cannot be read.

The fight is slow going. Fabian opens with **Phantasmal Force**, an illusory **griffin**, on the
knowledge that griffins are the minotaur's infamous natural rival — it does the creature no harm, since
it is **immune to psychic damage**, but it draws a claw and he works it for the rest of the fight. Mags
takes the first charge: the gore bounces off her, the follow-up claw lands for 16. Her **first Turn
Undead** does nothing at all. Rell's sorcerous burst, Trevick's hasted arrows, Hawk's flybys, Caspian's
**Shatter** and **magic missile** and Fabian's sword between them leave it hurt only "a bit," and not
bloodied. Mags's **Thorn Whip**, spent trying to wrench the axe out of its chest, fails.

Rell steps into the mud and is **restrained** by something beneath it. Caspian's Shatter drives several
more of them briefly to the surface: they are a **form of slime**, flat and featureless, without faces,
and **not undead**. They burrow back. The one holding Rell pulls him **prone** and works to smother
him; he kills it with a **Shadow Blade** and takes its **acid death burst** as it dies, which frees
him. Throughout, **the minotaur walks through the mud unmolested** — it ignores the slimes and they
ignore it.

Mags's **second** attempt lands: 20 radiant damage, and the creature is **frightened and incapacitated
for a minute**, running for the pit. Trevick, working out for himself that an arrow would break the
effect, **holds his action**. Mags argues for letting it go — her read being that beating the thing is
what will earn them a conversation with whoever spoke at the door, and that the creature will show them
a way out one way or another. The party lets it flee back down the hole.

The session ends there, mid-combat, with about a minute of the turning left to run and Caspian saying
he has a plan he does not state.

## Key facts learned
- **The Stonehunger has a body, and the party has now seen it.** Beneath a crust of stone is a
  **whitish, faintly pearlescent, gelatinous substance** that moves, pushes masonry out of place and
  buckles floors from below. **The stone is its skin, not a shell around it** — it cracks open as the
  thing moves and re-forms over the exposed surface within a minute or two.
- **Identifying it as the Stonehunger is [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s inference**, made on the spot and
  unchallenged. The fiction has not named the substance.
- **It reacts to fire.** No reaction to heat brought near; on contact it fizzles, speeds up, and
  **spreads a thin layer over the flame to smother it**. [[mags|Mags]] calls that intentionality; Rell
  says it may be reflex.
- **There is a mind in it.** Rell's Detect Thoughts brushes something that **can be called a mind** —
  **extremely strange**, **vast**, **old**, **unlike anything he has encountered** — with no detail readable and a
  clear sense that pressing further would be dangerous. He withdrew immediately.
- **It is slow.** Most of a night to bring down two rooms. It never attacked anyone; it crushed the
  space.
- **[[the-ancient-forge|The forge]] is three rooms** — western (the way they came up), southern (the
  staircase down), northern (the hatch up) — with the original exits filled by rock **pressed forward
  into the doorways**. The western room collapsed in two stages during the night, closing the route
  back to [[the-foul-pool|the pool]]; the northern room was going the same way when they left.
- **The upper chamber is a [[calvyr|Calvyran]] shrine** — devotional reliefs in a traditional dwarven
  style, incense staining beneath them from long use as sites of prayer — and it was the **only room
  the Stonehunger did not touch all night**. Whether that is protection is a guess
  ([[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s); the DM confirms its architecture is otherwise the same dressed stone.
- **The shrine was cleared out recently.** Fresh scrape marks where things were dragged from the walls,
  and blank paper scraps where written material was stored. The scraps are either **magical and
  ancient** or **much more recent**; the party can test which and has not.
- **Other groups have camped in the forge**, within **months and years**, with no sign of violence.
- **The spiral staircase is not original to the complex** — plainer, later work, made of **several
  different staircases pitched together** and positioned rather than cut.
- **The custodian's kind of stone-shaping reaches this far down.** The same faint **ripple marks** are
  in the raw stone at the dead end below. Whoever opened it worked **slowly and with visible effort**.
- **[[the-high-priest|The voice]] is not the custodian** and does not come from the stone. It welcomed
  the party, then spoke twice as though **pressing someone else** to do the opening. It has said
  nothing since the door shut.
- **[[the-buried-arena|The arena]] is assembled out of several buildings** (~70/30), lit by torches,
  hung with **banners that are not old**, floored in **oily mud that hides living things**, and
  **sealed by an invisible barrier** that encloses every exit. Its **bull mosaic** means nothing to
  anyone present. The **rock appears to have flowed over and around it** rather than through it.
- **The arena slimes** are flat, featureless, live under the mud, restrain and try to smother, are
  vulnerable to psychic damage, and **burst in acid** when killed. They are **not undead**, and they
  and the minotaur take no interest in each other.
- **[[the-undead-minotaur|The minotaur]]** is very large (ten to twelve feet), clawed and horned rather
  than armed, essentially **mindless**, **immune to psychic damage**, and carries
  **[[the-embedded-axe|a Calvyran holy dwarven axe]]** lodged in its chest with the flesh grown closed
  around it. Minotaurs are uncommon but known to exist. It is **not dead** and is **down the pit**.
- **The party is out of resources.** A short rest and no long rest since before the pool fight; Mags is
  down to roughly one second-level slot.

## Threads opened / advanced
- **[[the-stonehunger-below]]** — the party loses the forge, gains its first physical sighting of the
  Stonehunger and a sample of it, and is sealed into the arena a staircase below.
- New: **who [[the-high-priest|the voice]] is and who it was talking to**; **what
  [[the-buried-arena|the arena]] is for**, who lights it and who hangs its banners; **whose bull** that
  is; **why the arena was spared** when the forge a staircase above was crushed; **who buried
  [[the-embedded-axe|a Calvyran holy axe]] in the minotaur** and whether that is why it still moves;
  **whether the shrine chamber was protected**; and **who cleared the shrine out, and when**.
- Advanced: **what the Stonehunger is** — now with a body, a reaction to stimulus, and something that
  can be brushed as a mind, none of which settles the question.
