## Recap

**The patrol.** Some ten minutes after [[the-bone-swarm|the bone swarm]] has gone, with [[mags|Mags]]'s
Daylight still burning over the convoy on [[the-plain|the plain]], the party sees **[[the-keep|the Keep]]'s
patrol** — the one it has been avoiding since S2.40 — coming at them from the north-west, fanning out as it comes. There are a couple of minutes to get ready. The
children go into **a crevice behind two big rocks** to the south, with Marianne Swithun and her baby
Caelum, and Daisy Thorngage, who has no one left, put in with them. Old Mothrigg Tosscobble stands over
them with a club. Max the dog is set to watch the other flank. Most of the adults take cover; a few
take up bows. **[[kithri|Kithri Munn]]**, whose son died under the swarm, comes forward to fight.

[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] calls Mags to be heroic for these people and lays **Heroism** on her, and
tells [[rell-aetris|Rell]] to do that cool thing again. Rell already has **Mirror Image** about him.

**What they are.** There are **seven**, the last of them their **commander**. Some carry only weapons
(heavy crossbows, maces; they also carry nets), some weapons and **the marks of magic**, some only the
magic. One raises a **gauntleted left hand** and looses a bolt of death; another wreathes his crossbow
in **dark, black flames**. **Their magic is fire, lightning and worse**: one casts **Lightning Bolt**
down the line, and the commander casts **Fireball** and **Slow**. Others throw **Chill Touch**,
**Prismatic** blasts and **Shatter**.

They are **undead**, every one. **Mags's turning takes hold of them** — though they resist it hard — and
**necrotic magic does nothing to them**, which Mags knows well enough not to try. Whether they were
*made*, as constructs are, is not quite the question: in a sense, but not in the relevant one.

**The opening.** The bolt of death misses Fabian. Mags lays **Bane** on three of them, two of whom it
takes, and tries to goad them into coming for her instead of the villagers. Kithri looses an arrow and
**draws blood**. The **lightning bolt** runs straight through the party's line. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]
holds his own fire until five of them are bunched together and then drops **a Fireball** among them,
catching every one. Then **the commander answers in kind**: a second **Fireball** catches Caspian, Mags,
Fabian, Rell and Kithri. **Mags, Fabian and Kithri go down.** A patrolman puts a crossbow bolt into
Kithri where she lies, and **she dies** there.

**Holding on.** [[trevick|Trevick]] is struck hard twice and keeps shooting; Caspian gets past a
patrolman and pours **a healing potion** down Mags's throat; Rell gets to his feet and goes in with his
blades. The commander's **Slow** catches Caspian and Trevick. A mace drops Mags again. Then **Fabian
opens his eyes**, of his own accord, eats **a fruit cake** that works like a potion of greater healing,
and brings Mags back with **Healing Word**. Mags raises the symbol of [[naelos|Naelos]] and pours
healing into Caspian, Fabian and Rell, and heals herself.

**The villagers fight.** One of the casters wades in among the villagers. **Maddie Pruett** —
[[roscoe|Roscoe]]'s mother — shoots him, then throws herself at him to protect the children. **Finnan
Highcroft** shoots him; **Rosie Tryn**, eleven, slips round behind and **stabs him** with her dagger.
**Del Zoric**, who has cowered for most of the fight, gathers himself and shoots again and again,
missing every time. **Olo Halfkettle** swings at the caster and misses; **old Bohdan Tarek** clubs him,
and **Max** bites him and worries at the hem of his robes. The caster answers with **Shatter** on
himself in the middle of them: Rosie is badly hurt, Bohdan, Maddie and others less so; **nobody dies of
it**. **Trevick's arrow kills him** — with Max convinced the robes did it.

**Turning the fight.** Fabian, from [[the-golden-lute|the golden lute]], mocks the patrol — *experts of
the deep, who cannot hit a dandy like him, while a little girl stabs them* — and **two of them collapse
laughing, the commander among them**, so his Slow fails. Mags **turns** two more, and the power of
Naelos burns them as they go; one of them **gets away** across the plain. Rell **cuts down** patrolmen one after another, Trevick shoots, and Fabian
mocks the last of them into misery. Caspian is beaten unconscious by a mace late on, and Mags brings him
back.

**What the last one wants.** Fabian reads the last patrolman standing: **he would very much like to
leave**, but he keeps looking to his commander, who is rolling on the ground laughing and paying him no
attention, and **he does not have the authority to break off**. He clearly believes the commander would,
if he could. Trevick finishes him.

**The prisoner.** That leaves the commander, still laughing. Rell **binds his fingers and gags him**, so
that he can neither shape nor speak a spell. The other patrolmen had looked to him for instruction when
they wanted to leave. Of the rest of the patrol, all are destroyed but **the one that fled**, and
Rell argues against stopping to rest here for that reason.

**After.** **Kithri is the only villager lost.** The rest are sad for her but steadier than they were
after the swarm; Fabian sees to them. The party means to **question the prisoner** briefly and then go
on across the plain in the same direction. Rell will do the asking **telepathically**, so that the man
need never speak. **He speaks a variant of Draconic** — not the Draconic of the modern world, but close
enough to be understood on practical matters — and when Fabian puts on **[[the-dragon-crown|the dragon
crown]]**, even half-recovered, the prisoner gives him **his full attention**.

## Key facts learned
- **The Keep's patrols are undead**, their commander too. They can be turned, though they resist it strongly,
  and they are **immune to necrotic** magic.
- **They are casters as well as soldiers**: bolts of death from a gauntleted hand, dark-flamed
  crossbows, Lightning Bolt, Fireball, Slow, Chill Touch, Shatter. They carry **nets**.
- **They have a chain of command.** The patrolmen look to their commander and cannot break off without
  him.
- **One patrolman got away**, fleeing Mags's turning.
- **The commander speaks an old variant of Draconic.** The dragon crown on Fabian's head commands his
  attention.
- **Kithri Munn is dead.** Nineteen villagers survive, with the dog.
