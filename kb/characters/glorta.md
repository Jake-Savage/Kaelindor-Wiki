## Overview
Glorta (name uncertain — also heard "Lortos") is the **more timid** of the two [[the-gleaners|gleaners]]
the party finds beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] (S2.31). He defers in everything to his
companion [[turley|Turley]], looking to her before he acts. Like her he carries spear, bow, and a
grapnel-pole, and comes up the stairs under [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s **Suggestion**.

## Appearances
- S2.31: Beckoned close by [[rell-aetris|Rell]], who whispers the trigger word "**sleep**"; Glorta
  obediently lays out a makeshift pillow and falls asleep. [[turley|Turley]] takes it for the boy
  simply dozing off. He is later left sleeping as the party moves deeper.
