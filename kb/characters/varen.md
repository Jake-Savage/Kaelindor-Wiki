## Overview
The landlord of **[[ketters-rest|Ketter's Rest]]** in [[dunton|Dunton]]. Middle-aged, human, and
bleary-eyed when the party wakes him in the small hours of the morning. He comes down the stairs and
looks at them in plain **astonishment** — visitors are evidently not a thing that happens here.

*"This is my place."*

## Manner
When the party says it was sent by [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]], Varen's reaction is **nervous**
— there is fear in it — but he looks at them **more with pity than with fear**. Told that God has
decreed their sacrifice can wait, he holds up his hands in a gesture of helplessness and starts to say
that sacrificing guests is not something he would be party to.

He will not promise to hide them: **"I can't hide you from the Provost. Do you understand that? She
will have to be told."**

Two things he says in passing:

- He begins to observe that they are all here in one piece, then **catches himself and asks, stricken,
  whether they left anyone**. Told nobody, he nods and smiles with **obvious relief** — *"then things
  are well enough."*
- On healing: **"there was a time when a healer in these parts would have changed everything — but
  this High Priest came. We've not too much lack in that regard, which is a blessing, of course."**

He is otherwise **courteous and thoughtful**, pours what he has, shows them upstairs, and will fetch
anything he can — he empties his house of pillows on request.

His name tells the party nothing: it is a name they could have met in [[greywatch|Greywatch]].

## DM notes / secrets
- **He will go to [[the-provost|the Provost]] at the earliest possible opportunity** — confirmed. He
  wants no part of whatever is occurring, and that is the whole of his motive.
- What the pity is for — what he expects to happen to people the High Priest sends down — is
  unestablished, as is what "sacrifice" actually means in [[dunton|Dunton]].
