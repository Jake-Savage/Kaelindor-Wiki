## Overview
Theodore is the rat familiar of [[caspian-talon]] — a frequently-used scout and spy who can be sent invisibly to overhear plans and gather intelligence. Caspian relies on him repeatedly (notably eavesdropping on the [[the-eldryn]] camp's warding plans, S2.19) and is wryly fond of him despite his "I hate to ask Theo to offer his services" grumbling.

## Knowledge & motivations
As a familiar, Theodore acts on Caspian's direction. Nameweave is noted (in Caspian's diary) as "Theo's favourite holiday."

## Appearances
- S1.01–05: Caspian's familiar through the early arc.
- S2.03: Killed by [[bartholomew-crane]] when investigating the Brass Tower clocktower; later resummoned.
- S2.04: Returns terrified bearing Caspian's letter after Caspian's secret meeting with [[lucien]].
- S2.19–20: Scouts the Eldryn camp and the burnt zone invisibly alongside [[hawk]].
- S2.29: Sent into [[jorrith-potter|Jorrith]]'s [[the-three-copper-hatch|house]] in [[greywatch]] to scout the interior (and later to watch Rell's meeting from inside) — finds it tidy and spartan, with no shrine, sigils, or hidden doors, only a framed drawing of [[meera]].
- S2.31: The party's eyes underground beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]]. Perches on the
  gargoyle golem to guard its key during the lock-picking, then scouts the [[the-gleaners|gleaners']]
  complex ahead of the party — the dwarvish machine-room, the two gleaners (their soft shoes, their
  grapnel-poles), the stinking crate-room, the climbable pipes — and finally pokes his head into the
  chasm cavern to reveal the **web of bridges over the river of churning stone** and the gleaners
  crowding it.
- S2.34: Scouted [[the-foul-pool|the pool]] twice for [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]. The first trip, down
  the stair and along the ledge, produced everything the party knew before they descended: the size of
  the pit, the filthy warm water, the damaged ledge, the carved chimney and the dark shapes roosting
  under its canopy. The second trip, under the canopy itself, produced the **construct spiders** — one
  came at him almost at once, metal-clattering and fast — and he was dismissed out of existence a moment
  before it reached him.
