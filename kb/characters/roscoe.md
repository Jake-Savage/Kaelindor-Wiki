## Overview
A villager of **[[dunton|Dunton]]**, almost certainly of the **[[black-barrow|Black Barrow]]** stock,
who **died less than a year ago** — and whose body **[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] has worn ever
since**.

The party met him without knowing it: the figure in **red and gold robes** at
[[the-buried-arena|the arena]] and in [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] is Roscoe's corpse. The **human face
altered to look more elvish** is his.

## How he died
[[kithri|Kithri]] tells it plainly:

- He was out beyond the palisade when **one of the great insect things** of [[the-plain|the plain]]
  got him — **stabbed, taken in the lung**.
- **His mates carried him back** to the village, but he **died before anyone could do much to help
  him**, and before Greyface could reach him — *"and even if he had been able to save him, which I'm
  not sure he could; he's not a miracle worker."*
- Afterwards Greyface *"must have patched him up a bit, I suppose, but that's not something we'd see."*

This is the standing arrangement in Dunton: **when someone dies they are taken to the Ossuary**, and
when a surrogate wears out or an opportunity arises, Greyface **renews**. The previous body had lasted
**six or seven years**.

## DM notes / secrets
- **Nobody in Dunton objects.** *"No one begrudges him. But still, it's strange. It feels strange."*
  For many it is **still difficult to look at**, because it is a man they all knew.
- **Whether anything of Roscoe remains** is not addressed. On what the party knows of
  [[undead-puppetry|the magic]], the puppet's **mental attributes are the puppeteer's** and the
  perception is the puppeteer's magical sensing, not the corpse's senses — but nobody has asked, and
  nobody in Dunton has raised it.
- **How Roscoe came to be the one** — whether his death was simply the next accident, or anything else
  — is unestablished. His death was witnessed and reported by his own companions.
