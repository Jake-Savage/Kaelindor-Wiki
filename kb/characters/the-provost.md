## Overview
**Not yet met.** The Provost is named twice in S2.37 and is clearly the authority in
[[dunton|Dunton]].

What is known:

- **She is a woman.** [[varen|Varen]] refers to her as "she."
- **She will be told about the party.** Varen says plainly that he **cannot hide them from her** and
  that she **will have to be told** — and he intends to go to her at the earliest opportunity.
- **She can direct people to [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]].** [[the-high-priest|The High Priest]] says
  so, adding that he doubts she would be keen to accompany them there.

The High Priest's general estimate of Dunton — that even tired, the party would be more than a match
for any of them, and that they are *"a rather sorry bunch"* — is **his** assessment, offered to
reassure the party that his name would be believed.

## DM notes / secrets
- Her title, her authority's basis, her relationship to [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]], and what
  she does about outsiders are all **unestablished**. The party's stated intention is to meet her.
