## Overview
A woman of **sixty-five or so**, **severe-looking**, unmistakably in charge. She wears **actual
armour** — **battered, but well cared for**: somebody has made an effort with it despite the knocks
it has plainly taken over the years.

She gave the party her name **once**, with a shrug:

> *"It's so long since anyone's called me anything but Provost. Well — my name was, in the world above,
> **Yafet Em**. But that has not been relevant for many years. You may call me Provost."*

Asked what a Provost does here: *"I do my best to keep these people alive, and with some hope."*

## Manner
She is **not delighted** the party has arrived. Not angry — but she plainly **would rather this had not
happened**, and reads it as **trouble she does not need**. She remains **guarded** throughout, and
**as welcoming as she might reasonably be in the circumstances**.

She is **not deceptive** under scrutiny, but she is **careful**. Asked about "your God" she answers
**cautiously, not worshipfully**. Asked about [[the-high-priest|Greyface]] her lip gives a **wry
twist**: she does not take the "High Priest" title entirely at face value, feels **ambivalent** about
him, and is **respectful up to a point**. The two young soldiers with her are **completely focused on
her** and show no trace of disagreement with anything she says.

## How Dunton is run
- **Strict redistribution.** When the party lays out its rations as a gesture, she asks whether they
  intend to **contribute them to the village**, and then sweeps up **all but a mouthful** for the
  **collective stores**, to be distributed in the normal course of things. She makes clear it is **not
  compulsory** and that they are guests, but that it is appreciated — and ceremonially tops up what is
  left so that **everyone present gets a bite** and the courtesy is satisfied.
- The principle is explicit: resources go **not to whoever gathered them but to whoever needs them**.
  Dunton has **old people and very young children**, and she judges they will not survive otherwise.
- The villagers are **poor but not malnourished**.

## What she told the party
- **Most of them came from [[black-barrow|Black Barrow]]**, taken **twelve years ago**. They found a
  **few people already down here** who had been here much longer. She herself **came later**.
- **Forty-two folk came down with Greyface.** They have **lost nineteen** since. There have been
  additions as well as losses — *"yes, we are fewer than we were."*
- **Until the party arrived, no survivors had ever come through.**
- She had **already guessed the Stonehunger had surfaced**, from fresh things appearing out on
  **[[the-plain|the plain]]** some weeks ago.
- **Beyond the palisade: the plain, and then [[the-keep|the Keep]]**, which stands above and guards
  **the lower levels** — which she **has never seen and does not wish to**.
- **"This is a clearinghouse. A processing centre."** These are *"things not yet fully absorbed, or
  things that cannot be fully absorbed — within [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]], for instance. There are
  other places intact, protected."*
- **[[the-gleaners|The gleaners]]:** Dunton knows of them and sometimes sees them on the plain, but has
  **no relationship** with them. The gleaners are **hostile**, and Dunton is **afraid** — they believe
  the gleaners have the numbers to wipe them out entirely, which on what the party has seen is **true**.
- **Greyface is "a cleric of sorts."** She believes **prayers are efficacious** and has seen his powers:
  **he can heal**, and **conjure nourishment where there was only rock and bone**.
- **"He is not the only priest. In the levels below, there are very, very many of them. We see them
  upon the plain sometimes."**
- **His accommodation.** *"He, I think, has made a sort of accommodation — one that buys us peace.
  Without it, we certainly would not have survived as long as we have."* And, when needled about his
  selflessness, her face hardens: *"He does have requests. But he has moderated—"*
- He has **little need of food** and little interest in anything they can offer; *"indeed, the commerce
  rather goes the other way when times are hard."* He **helps when folk are sick**, **feeds them when
  food is scarce**, and **keeps folk busy** with **projects — "study, he calls it, although what he's
  studying I have never quite had the inclination to ask."**

## Her neutrality
She **declines** [[mags|Mags]]'s offer to commemorate Dunton's nineteen dead with
[[naelos|Naelos]]'s rites — *"a kind offer, and kindly received. But such a thing could be awkward. We
have to be a little careful here."* The reason: the other inhabitants of this place **regard their
Godhead as being all around them**, and **Greyface has told her it would be imprudent to obviously
contradict that interpretation**.

> *"I maintain a neutrality. Greyface, perhaps, prays for all of us. Perhaps so that we might not have
> to."*

## DM notes / secrets
- **She faced him down.** Per [[kithri|Kithri]], the Provost arrived **nearly six weeks after the
  disaster**, having **fought her way down alone**, arriving bloody and embattled — and **went straight
  into the Ossuary** while people were still disappearing into it. *"I think she told him what was
  what. Because after that, things got different."* No more disappearances; an **understanding, an
  exchange**. The party has not heard her own version.
- **Something drew her down.** She was away from home when Black Barrow fell; she went back **to search
  for those she had lost** — *"but I don't know that I would have so eagerly walked into the bowels of
  the earth as I did, there not being some sort of an influence. **There is a strangeness.**"* She
  volunteers this herself, unprompted, and does not elaborate. See [[the-stonehunger]].
- She offers the party **a place in Dunton** if they wish to stay.
