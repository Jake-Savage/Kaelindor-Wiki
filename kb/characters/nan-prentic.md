## Overview
Nan Prentic is an older human woman — hard to age, perhaps 55–60 — thin, wiry, and clearly
seasoned in the outdoors, with a dour, world-weary manner. She is one of two **unofficial
(unlicensed) guides** the party hires in [[greywatch|Greywatch]] to reach [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's
Fold]] (S2.30), the lead among the pair. "Nan" is most likely a **title rather than her given
name** — most people call her Nan, and it is not clear to the party that it is her actual name.
She travels with her donkey [[lisa|Lisa]] and her companion [[cressan-weller|Cressan Weller]].

Because she is not part of the licensed guild, she has **no access to the guild's updated maps or
the knowledge returning guides report in** — she navigates from older knowledge she learned by
rote in her training, plus her own memorised observation of unchanging landmarks (rock shapes,
turn angles, distant peaks lined up as waypoints). She last passed toward Kethran's Fold about
**two and a half years ago**, and is uncomfortably conscious that her information is not
up to date.

## Knowledge & motivations
Nan is careful, practical, and unsentimental. She took the job for the money — party gold (largely
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s), plus a little pressure from [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s shadier Greywatch
contacts — and says plainly it means fuel and a roof for the winter. She thinks going to Kethran's
Fold is a fool's errand: the village is "gone," "the land's taken it," and any survivors would have
been found weeks ago. She agrees to wait a day or two at the site while the party does its business,
then bring them back.

She is a sceptic about the country's darker folklore. She dismisses the **[[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]]**
her companion Cressan invokes as superstition — it was "a landslip," these parts are prone to such
things, and the frightening tales are "made up to frighten people." Her stated view of the Mirrowen
is narrower: "there's too much magic here," so one steps careful and stays watchful. She is protective
of Cressan, stepping in verbally when others might mock him, and gives the sense of someone who has
had to defend him before.

## Appearances
- S2.30: Hired in Greywatch as the party's lead unofficial guide to Kethran's Fold. Leads them south
  over two days, navigating by memorised landmarks; grows tenser as the broken terrain near the fold
  defeats her long-range bearings. At the site she is disinterested and eager to leave, dismisses the
  Stonehunger and the "singing" stories as nonsense, and sets up camp to wait while the party descends
  into the buried village.
