## Overview
A survivor of **[[black-barrow|Black Barrow]]** and the mother of one of the young men who guards
[[dunton|Dunton]]'s palisade. Her house is full of **sewing and embroidery things**, and she works
**to detailed patterns** supplied by [[the-high-priest|Greyface]].

She is not nimble or strong enough to go out beyond the palisade, so this is **how she is useful**:
*"my priest asks us to contribute with these trifles, and it's a skill I have. It gives me something to
fill my days."*

## The work
- **The cloth is extraordinary.** Rich, fancy, **opulent** — in total contrast to everything else in
  Dunton, which is basic. She calls it **nobleman's stuff**: *"even back in the burrow, I never saw
  anything like this. I don't suppose even in [[greywatch|Greywatch]] they have much like this."*
  Touching it is one of the things she likes about the job.
- **Greyface fetches it from somewhere.** She does not have an unlimited supply — one piece at a time —
  and *"he's always excited to have something new. He travels more widely than we do. I can't imagine
  where it comes from."* [[mags|Mags]] examines the fabric and finds **no evidence anything like it
  could be produced down here**; it seems improbable, though magic makes little impossible.
- **Banners are usually monsters.** She is currently a few weeks into **a manticore**, in the same style
  as the bull banners.
- **The bulls were hers and her neighbours'.** *"Yes, **we** finished those a little while back — but
  yes, for a while he was mad about bulls."* The party had seen them hanging, fresh and not threadbare,
  in [[the-buried-arena|the arena]].
- **Others have been making related things these past few weeks**, and *"the scorpion tail in particular
  seems to be a design he's favouring."*
- **Robes and cloaks** are harder, and tend toward **trees and nature** rather than monsters. She
  prefers those. *"He likes to look the part."* The red robes are **not all he has**.
- **She does not do glyph work on cloth** — but she **recognises the marks** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]
  draws for her. *"They're part of a boundary. I don't suppose cloth would keep still enough. It needs
  to be stone for this sort of work."* She has seen such work: **beyond the palisade, two lines of
  them, great half circles** (see [[the-plain]]). **They were there before her people arrived**, and
  she has never had cause to draw them.
- Asked whether the robes are always the same size, she says **yes, near enough** — and when
  [[mags|Mags]] observes that these are not robes for one person, she gives an *"it's complicated and
  I'm not sure you'd understand"* look and declines to go into it. She is **not deceptive and not
  nervous**, just unwilling.
- [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] quietly casts **Detect Magic** over her materials while she talks and finds
  **nothing** in them.

## What she says about Greyface
Pressed gently on why the subject is awkward — and told plainly that nobody has instructed her to keep
quiet — she gives the party the session's single biggest revelation, assuming they already knew it:

> *"Young Roscoe. You've met Greyface. The body he is using at present."*

- **The villagers know.** *"It wasn't so long ago. It's not easy for folk. And no one begrudges him. But
  still, it's strange. It feels strange."*
- **The bodies do not last forever**, and they **deteriorate** over time. When there is an accident or
  someone dies, he **takes the opportunity to renew things**.
- **[[roscoe|Roscoe]] died less than a year ago.** Before that it had been **six or seven years**.
- Of what he does with them: *"I can't rightly say. I don't know how the magic works, but as far as one
  can see, I suppose sometimes they must need patching up a bit."*
- She also gives the fullest account the party has of **[[black-barrow|the disaster]]** and the
  **first weeks underground**:
  - Greyface **shut himself up in [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]** and told the survivors to stay back
    behind the ward signs and make what they could of the place.
  - They found **folk already here**, not many, who had been making do a long time and showed them how.
  - **In those first weeks, people went into the Ossuary and did not come back.** Fear of Greyface grew
    and things were bad, and they had no real choice but to stay.
  - **Then the Provost came**, nearly six weeks after the disaster, having fought her way down alone
    and arriving bloody and embattled. **She went straight into the Ossuary**, and Kithri thinks
    *"she told him what was what. Because after that, things got different."* No more disappearances.
    An **understanding — an exchange**: he started helping them and they started helping him.
  - **From then on: when someone died, they took them to the Ossuary — but not before that.**
  - *"I don't suppose it's ideal. I don't know what he does up there. I don't suppose I want to know.
    But most of us who might have expected to live to this year anyway have done. And there are folk
    who've made families down here. Well — things could have been different."*

## DM notes / secrets
- **The spelling of her name is uncertain** — the recording gives "Kithri"; her son calls her "Ma."
- **She is the first person to tell the party outright what Greyface is.** She assumed they knew, and
  there is no secrecy about it in Dunton at all.
- **Her son is unnamed** in the recording.
- **The manticore and the scorpion tails are being made now.** The bulls preceded the minotaur. Nobody
  at the table said the obvious thing out loud, and the fiction has not confirmed it.
