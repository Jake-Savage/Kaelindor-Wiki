## Overview
Turley (name uncertain — also heard "Terri"/"Telly") is a **gleaner** (see [[the-gleaners]]), the
**more assertive** of the two goblins the party finds at rest in the dwarvish machine-room beneath
[[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] (S2.31). Her timid companion [[glorta|Glorta]] looks to her for
every decision. She carries a spear and the gleaners' grapnel-pole, and speaks Common.

She meets the party with **supreme, patronising confidence** — leveling her spear, naming them
"godless," and offering, as though doing them an enormous favour, to let them hand over all their
"shiny things" in exchange for being shown the way out. Even under [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s
**Suggestion** she does not realise she is charmed; she simply finds herself, to her own confusion,
**interested** in these strange godless ("something about you that makes me... interested").

## Knowledge & motivations
Under Suggestion, Turley answers the party's questions (see [[the-stonehunger]] and
[[the-gleaners]] for the substance): the nature of "God," the gleaners' place in the world, the
route deeper (past the **sleepers**, over the **bridges** across the chasm, to the
[[the-custodian|custodian]]'s gate), the numbers below (a "dozen or two" at the river, "forty,
fifty" near [[tuffin|Tuffin]]), the "**glass smoke**" in the pillars (see [[the-glass-smoke]]), and
the myth of **Dunton**, a place deeper where godless who "found a place" are said to live.

- **Her guilt.** Pressed by [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] ("You sound guilty!"), she admits the
  gleaners kept a small catch for themselves — "should have shared what [Blotter] caught when we
  was last out... but it was only small and we was hungry." The party reads a persistent
  **disingenuity** in her manner, as though she is hiding or scheming something.
- She is tempted by the offer of **cooking/preparation techniques** ("cannot be taken unless
  willingly given") — what comes up from below "gets a bit samey" — but doubts [[tuffin|Tuffin]]
  and the others would permit escorting the party across the bridge with all their loot.

## Appearances
- S2.31: Interrogated under Suggestion; leads the exchange with the party. Her companion
  [[glorta|Glorta]] is put to sleep by Rell's trigger word; Turley remains, oddly cooperative, and
  the party ultimately puts her to sleep as well to slip past the gleaners deeper in.

## DM notes / secrets
- The party's read that Turley is **"up to something"** is a grounded suspicion — an **Insight read**
  of her behaviour and body language, backed by her admitted hoarding — **not** a confirmed scheme.
  Keep it as suspicion: something concealed or in the works, contents unknown.
