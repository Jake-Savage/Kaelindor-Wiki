## Overview
The last of the seven in [[the-keep|the Keep]]'s patrol — the one the party had been avoiding since
S2.40 — that came at the convoy on [[the-plain|the plain]] minutes after [[the-bone-swarm|the bone
swarm]] withdrew. He is **undead**, like all his men.

## In the fight (S2.42)
- **He came in late** and answered [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s Fireball with **one of his own**, which
  dropped [[mags|Mags]], [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] and [[kithri|Kithri]] — Kithri fatally, once a
  crossbow bolt followed.
- **He cast Slow** over the party, catching Caspian and [[trevick|Trevick]].
- **Fabian's mockery set him laughing** so hard he fell, and he lost the Slow. He did not recover
  before the fight ended.
- **His men looked to him.** When things went badly they wanted to leave, and looked to him for
  instruction; the last of them **would not break off without his order**, and plainly believed he
  would have given it.

## Taken
[[rell-aetris|Rell]] **bound his fingers and gagged him** while he lay laughing, so he can neither
shape nor speak a spell.

- **He speaks a variant of Draconic** — not the Draconic of the modern world, but close enough for
  practical questions; finer matters may drift. He was muttering away in it during the fight.
- **When Fabian puts on [[the-dragon-crown|the dragon crown]], he has the man's full attention**, even
  before he has fully recovered.
- The party means to **question him briefly** and then go on. Rell will put the questions
  **telepathically**, so the man need not speak; Fabian will relay through Rell.

## DM notes / secrets
- **He is undead**, like the rest of his patrol (DM, after S2.42). He was not turned in the fight.
- **One of his men got away**, fleeing Mags's turning — so the Keep may soon know what became of him.
- **The party's questions, so far:** how to get out; whether there is a secret exit; whether others
  will come after them.
- **Why the crown holds his eye** is unknown. The courtyard's inscription in [[the-golden-courtyard]]
  was also in an archaic Draconic; whether that bears on him is an open question.
