## Overview
At the foot of the spiral staircase down from [[the-ancient-forge|the ancient forge]], the party finds
a **dead end of raw stone**. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] finds no pressure pads or writing, but does find
**faint ripple patterns in the rock** of the same kind [[the-custodian|the stone elemental at the gate
to Grommar's Path]] left when it shaped and reshaped the entryway — so something has worked this stone
the same way, at some point.

[[mags|Mags]] knocks and calls out. A **voice** answers.

## What is known of it
- **It is not the stone, and it is not the Custodian.** The voice does not sound like
  [[the-custodian|the elemental]] the party met hours earlier, and it does not sound as though it comes
  from within or behind the rock. It sounds **clear and bright, as though in the air** — above them,
  perhaps a little further back up the stairs. Nothing is visible.
- **It welcomes them.** *"Well, well, visitors, you are most welcome. Give me a second."*
- **It does not open the gate itself.** Several minutes pass. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] raps on the
  wall in a rhythm and observes that where he comes from one does not leave visitors waiting at the
  door. Then the stone begins to move, and the voice speaks again — **less declamatory this time** —
  *"That wasn't so hard. I knew you could do it with the proper incentive."* The stone parts slowly and
  with **evident effort** into a plain gateway. The voice comes **more sharply**: *"Come now, you can do
  better than that."* The gateway then widens and takes on something of the **grandeur** of the arch
  that led onto [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]] — simulated columns to each side, an arched top.
- **It has said nothing since.** The stone door shuts behind the party. Inside
  [[the-buried-arena|the arena]] Fabian thanks it aloud, addresses it as "Mr. Voice in the Wall," and
  asks whether there is anyone to talk to. **No reply comes.** Shortly afterwards
  [[the-undead-minotaur|the minotaur]] begins climbing out of the pit.

## Open questions
- **Who was it talking to?** Its two remarks — about incentive, and about doing better — are addressed
  to someone, and the party has no way to tell whether that someone is them or whatever is actually
  shifting the stone. Nobody at the table settled it.
- **Is it present?** [[mags|Mags]] speculates aloud, mid-fight, that the minotaur might headbutt "an
  invisible bastard with a voice who had been taunting us." That is her guess, not a finding.
- **Does the minotaur belong to it?** The party has not established that the voice put the minotaur in
  the pit, or that it is watching the fight. Mags's working assumption is that **winning earns them a
  conversation** — again, her reading.

## DM notes / secrets
- Nothing about this being has been established beyond the lines above: not its nature, its location,
  its intentions, or its relationship to [[the-custodian|the eyes of God]], whose ripple-marks are on
  the stone it opened. Record the evidence; it is for the DM to say whether they are the same kind of
  thing.
