## Overview
Tuffin (spelling uncertain — also heard "Tuffpin") is the **local boss** of the
[[the-gleaners|gleaners]] beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]], named by [[turley|Turley]]
(S2.31) as the "gov'nor" she answers to. He is stationed **down by the chasm/river** with the main
body of workers — "forty, fifty maybe" gleaners in his vicinity, a "dozen or two" working the river
itself.

Turley doubts Tuffin would allow the gleaners to escort "godless" across the bridge, which is why
crossing to the [[the-custodian|custodian]] openly is not straightforward. He has **not been seen**;
nothing else about him is established.
