## Overview
The **Custodian** is the being said (by [[turley|Turley]], S2.31) to guard the **gate to the deep**
beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] — the way onto **[[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]]**, where
"the bigger folk" and the priests closer to "God" dwell. Per Turley he puts an **enormous face — "the
size of three houses"** — in the middle of the gate "for the drama of it," does not always come when
called, is **bored** and **curious**, and is fond of **news of "above and beyond."** His duty is to
keep folk out, but he is a "**soft old fellow**" who might well let people through on the reasoning
that Grommar's Path will kill them anyway.

The party met him in **S2.33** and found Turley's read broadly accurate in manner, though considerably
sharper in substance.

## What he is
Across [[the-stone-river|the stone river]], on the western rock face, is a **large carved archway**
with no visible door. Approached, the **stone flows and shapes itself** into a face.

- **An earth elemental.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s Arcana check (S2.32) identifies the shaping of the
  stone and the craggy planes of the face as an **earth elemental of some sort**. Earth elementals can
  be **bound into service by magic**: one that habitually acts as a door is **probably compelled** to it
  by someone or something. They are not all sentient, but can be — this one plainly is.
- **"The eyes of God in this part."** So he describes himself (S2.33). He speaks of **"the eyes of God
  below"** as his **brothers**, and doubts they would pass news of the party back up to him — implying
  a series of such watchers stationed through the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]]. This is **his own
  account of himself**; nothing independently confirms it.
- **He watches.** He saw the party's **Mass Suggestion** empty the cavern in S2.32 and opened the
  conversation with it: the trick with the "little scavengers" **amused** him — *"the way you sent them
  forth; I haven't seen someone do that before."* He holds that being amused is proper to his office.
- **Manner.** The formed face is **large and humanoid**: interested, amused, faintly mocking,
  **unsympathetic**, but not hostile. It speaks **aloud** in a **deep, earthy voice**.

## The bargain (S2.33)
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] — still shrunk and painted as a gleaner — asks outright for access to
Grommar's Path.

- **He refuses at first, on grounds of waste.** The party will very likely not survive; and if they do,
  his brothers below will not share news of them with him. He says they will **not return this way**.
  He would rather keep them and watch them explain themselves to the gleaners when they come back — he
  notes it is remarkable in itself that anyone came down **so long after the surfacing**.
- **Fabian buys interest with a story** — his festival mask and the pilgrims of
  [[shadows-meet|Shadows Meet]] in [[greywatch|Greywatch]], embellished (performance 25). The face grows
  visibly animated: *"I had not imagined the petty people of this age were capable of such
  elaboration."* But he dismisses the surrounding country as **paltry fare**, and says the party is
  **not of this place**: *"you are too late and too interesting… travellers from somewhere far more
  promising, such as I had begun to suspect were no longer in the world."*
- **Rell's wager.** [[rell-aetris|Rell]] offers: let them pass; if they fail on Grommar's Path they will
  leave **a thread he can pull** — a means of word reaching him; if they succeed, he is to accept it as
  their victory and grant his **acknowledgement** for the descent. He accepts, naming what he actually
  wants — *"I would dearly like to pull those threads that would lead us back to lands more worthy of
  God's sight"* — and grants his **acknowledgement and blessing** to carry below. On a formal
  acknowledgement from Rell, the stone **ripples aside** and the passage opens; it **closes once the
  party is inside**.

## DM notes / secrets
- **He cannot deliver.** [[mags|Mags]]'s **natural 20 Insight (27)** reads that the creature has the
  power to grant **neither the prize nor the reward** of the wager it agreed to, and **will not admit
  it**. It let them through regardless. What his "blessing" is actually worth below is an open question.
- **He believes his kind's reach is shrinking.** When Rell remarked that there is a limit to the lands
  he may now reach, an involuntary **spasm of frustration** crossed the face before he mastered it.
  Mags's Insight: he **genuinely believes** there is now a limit to the lands they can **claim**, is
  **baffled** by it, and feels something close to **shame** — the consciousness of a **diminished
  power**. Then a flash of **cunning**. What causes the limit is unexplained; do **not** fuse it with the
  [[the-mirrowen|Mirrowen]]'s fragmentation or any other thread.
- **Retcon (S2.33):** the DM **scratched** the S2.32 closing exchange at the archway — the face's
  question about walking Grommar's Path is no longer canon, and first contact is the S2.33 meeting.
  What survives the scratch and remains canon: the **description of the face**,
  [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s identification of it as a bound **earth elemental**, and
  [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s recollection of the [[grommar|Grommar]] legend.
- Nature and true size still unconfirmed — Turley's "three houses" has not been matched by what the
  party has seen, and who binds him (if anyone) remains unknown.
- **S2.36 — his marks are further down.** At the raw-stone dead end at the bottom of the spiral stair
  below [[the-ancient-forge|the forge]], [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s investigation finds the **same
  subtle ripple patterns** in the rock that the custodian left when it shaped and reshaped its own
  gateway hours earlier. So something has worked that stone **the same way, at some point**. The
  [[the-arena-voice|voice]] that answers there does **not** sound like him and does not seem to come
  from the stone at all — and whoever actually opened the gate did it slowly and with visible effort.
  Whether one of his "brothers below" is behind that wall is **not established**.
