## Overview
The **Custodian** is the being said (by [[turley|Turley]], S2.31) to guard the **gate to the deep**
beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] — the way onto **Grommer's Path** (spelling uncertain), the
deadly deeper reaches where "the bigger folk" and the priests closer to "God" dwell. He has **not
been seen**; everything known is Turley's account.

Per Turley: there is a **great doorway or gate** deeper in, and the Custodian likes to put his
**enormous face — "the size of three houses"** — right in the middle of it "for the drama of it,"
though he could show a regular-sized face if he chose. He does not always come when called, but is
**curious** and likely will. His **duty is to keep folk out**, but he is a "**soft old fellow**,"
**bored**, and fond of **hearing news of "above and beyond"** — so the gleaners reckon that
bringing him news would likely persuade him to let the party through (he half-reasons they will die
on Grommer's Path anyway).

## DM notes
- Nature and size unknown — a face "the size of three houses" implies something very large (a
  construct, a giant, or something stranger), but this is Turley's telling, not confirmed sight.
- He is the party's intended next objective: reach the Custodian's gate and talk their way past.
