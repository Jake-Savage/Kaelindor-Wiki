## Overview
The **Custodian** is the being said (by [[turley|Turley]], S2.31) to guard the **gate to the deep**
beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] — the way onto **[[grommar|Grommar's Path]]**, the deadly
deeper reaches where "the bigger folk" and the priests closer to "God" dwell.

Per Turley: there is a **great doorway or gate** deeper in, and the Custodian likes to put his
**enormous face — "the size of three houses"** — right in the middle of it "for the drama of it,"
though he could show a regular-sized face if he chose. He does not always come when called, but is
**curious** and likely will. His **duty is to keep folk out**, but he is a "**soft old fellow**,"
**bored**, and fond of **hearing news of "above and beyond"** — so the gleaners reckon that bringing
him news would likely persuade him to let the party through (he half-reasons they will die on
Grommar's Path anyway).

## First contact (S2.32)
Across [[the-stone-river|the stone river]], on the far rock face, is a **large carved archway** with
no visible door in it. When [[rell-aetris|Rell]] stands before it, the **stone begins to flow and
shape itself** into a face.

- **What it is.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s Arcana check identifies the shaping of the stone and the
  craggy planes of the face as **an earth elemental of some sort**. Caspian also knows that earth
  elementals **can be bound into service by magic**: this one may be here of its own accord, but if it
  **habitually** acts as a door, it is **probably compelled** to do so by someone or something. Earth
  elementals are not all sentient, but **can** be.
- **Manner.** The formed face is **large and humanoid**. Its expression is **not hostile** — interested
  and curious, perhaps a little **mocking**. It **fully clocks** that these are not gleaners (Rell
  drops his Disguise Self deliberately) and seems, if anything, **more intrigued** by that.
- **Its question.** It speaks **aloud**, in a **deep, earthy voice** (not mind-to-mind), and asks
  whether they intend to **walk Grommar's Path** — sardonic, amused, and slightly **pitying** rather
  than threatening.

The archway does **not** currently look like a door of any kind, though it has the potential to be
one. The exchange is where the session ends.

## DM notes / secrets
- **The identification with Turley's "custodian" is the party's working assumption, not stated.** The
  being does not name itself, and the face is described as large rather than "the size of three
  houses." The location (the gate beyond the bridges), the function (a face in a doorway that
  interrogates arrivals), and the subject (Grommar's Path) all fit Turley's account, so the reading is
  well-grounded — but treat it as an identification, not a confirmation.
- **Who binds it, if anyone, is unknown**, as is whether it is sentient in its own right or performing
  a compelled routine. Caspian's knowledge gives the possibility, not the fact.
- The gleaners' angle — that news of the world above is the price of passage — has not yet been
  tested. Rell revealed himself as an outsider **on purpose**, to trade on exactly that.
