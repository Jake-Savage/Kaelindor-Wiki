## Overview
Cressan Weller is a quiet, twitchy man of about thirty, the junior of the two unofficial guides the
party hires in [[greywatch|Greywatch]] (S2.30). He lets [[nan-prentic|Nan]] do all the talking and
gives the impression of someone who spends little time in town. He does not inspire confidence — the
DM's framing is that he seems a little unpredictable — but Nan knows and trusts him, and he was
willing to make a journey most other guides refused.

Per [[lisa|Lisa]] the donkey (via [[trevick|Trevick]]'s Speak with Animals), Cressan's **mother once
travelled with Nan**; after she died, Nan — feeling sorry for him — took Cressan in a few years
later, and he has travelled with them most of the time since.

## Knowledge & motivations
Cressan is the one who names the country's collapses as the **[[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]]** — a
thing that lives underground, comes and takes a village or a house, and leaves the land "still
hungry" after. He warns that people pulled from beneath the stone "aren't right," and repeats stories
of survivors who went back under the rubble, of singing and knocking and voices heard days or weeks
after a place is taken. Nan cuts these tales off as nonsense; Cressan holds to them. On seeing the
Kethran's Fold site he insists the Stonehunger "has to be below," because there is no space for it above.

He is wary of the mimic-danger the Mirrowen is known for: he tells the party that if anyone returns to
camp alone, "I know what to do," resting a hand on his crossbow.

## Appearances
- S2.30: Serves as the party's junior guide to Kethran's Fold. Keeps a continual watch on the route
  alongside Nan; voices his belief in the Stonehunger and the survivor/singing folklore. Catches a
  thrown apple with very fast reflexes but is bewildered by [[trevick|Trevick]]'s friendly gesture.
  Resists [[mags|Mags]]'s attempt to build rapport. At the site he crouches over the rubble and
  declares the Stonehunger took the village from below.

## DM notes / secrets
- **Rell's night-watch observation (confirmed as observed, meaning open):** standing a watch with
  Cressan in thick mist, [[rell-aetris|Rell]] (excellent darkvision) is confident that Cressan can
  **see in the dark better than he should be able to** — better than an ordinary human, though not as
  well as Rell. Rell keeps this to himself. What it means is unestablished — do **not** conclude
  Cressan is a mimic, an aberration, or non-human. It is a flagged anomaly only.
- His fast reflexes and unnatural night-sight, set against the Mirrowen's "don't trust anyone who's
  been alone" culture, are suggestive but unconfirmed. Recorded as evidence for the DM to rule on.
