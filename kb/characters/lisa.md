## Overview
Lisa is [[nan-prentic|Nan]]'s pack donkey, carrying the guides' supplies on the Kethran's Fold
journey. She is plainly well looked after and fond of treats (apples especially). Her main concern
on any trip is how long it will take and when she will be home; she has little interest in where
they are going.

## Appearances
- S2.30: [[trevick|Trevick]] casts Speak with Animals and talks with her at camp. She confirms Nan
  treats her well, that [[cressan-weller|Cressan]] is "strange" but that "Nan likes him," and gives
  his backstory: she and Nan **used to travel with Cressan's mother** (whom Lisa liked, and who also
  brought apples); the mother died, and a few years later Nan took Cressan in. Lisa has no sense that
  this particular route is unusual. Trevick feeds her an apple the next morning.
