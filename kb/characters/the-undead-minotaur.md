## Overview
The creature that comes up out of the pit in [[the-buried-arena|the buried arena]] in S2.36. All of the
party recognise it for a **minotaur** — uncommon creatures, but known to exist — and it is **very
clearly undead**. It is **very large**, roughly **ten to twelve feet** tall.

- It wields **no weapon**. It fights with **unusually long, sharp horns and claws**, lowering its head
  to charge.
- Its fur is **matted**, and its decay is advanced enough that [[rell-aetris|Rell]] reads it at a glance
  as **essentially mindless** — a judgement the fiction bears out.
- It is **immune to psychic damage**, which costs both Rell and [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] their
  opening plays.
- **The mud does not touch it.** It charges straight through the arena floor; the slimes beneath ignore
  it and it ignores them.

## The axe in its chest
Buried in the matted fur of its chest is **[[the-embedded-axe|a weapon]]** — a **very large axe of
dwarven design**, caught in the ribcage and lodged tightly. The **undead skin has grown back around it**,
so it is not an open wound. [[mags|Mags]] reads it as a **holy thing**, and **Calvyran** — a
double-handed axe that would take a strong warrior to wield, and which she plainly could not.

She asks whether it is hurting the creature and is told **it looks angry and in pain** — with the
caveat, stated at the time, that there are other reasons for it to be in pain and the **causality is
hard to read**. Her attempt to pull the axe free with **Thorn Whip** fails.

## Appearances
- **S2.36:** Emerges from the pit as the party is sealed into the arena, and charges. It bounces off
  [[mags|Mags]] once, then claws her for 16; it swipes at [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s illusory griffin
  as readily as at the party, and later turns on [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]. It
  shrugs off [[mags|Mags]]'s first **Turn Undead** and takes little visible harm from Rell's sorcerous burst, Trevick's hasted arrows,
  [[hawk|Hawk]]'s claws or Caspian's magic missile — by the end of the session it is described as
  hurt "a bit," and **not bloodied**. Mags's **second** Turn Undead lands: 20 radiant damage, and the
  creature is **frightened and incapacitated for one minute**. The party deliberately declines to
  attack it, and it **flees back down the pit**.

## DM notes / secrets
- **Who put it here, and why, is unestablished.** The party has not connected it to
  [[the-arena-voice|the voice]] that opened the gate, though Mags assumes a connection.
- **Who buried the axe in it is unestablished.** A Calvyran holy axe lodged in an undead thing inside a
  Calvyr temple complex on [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]] is suggestive; the fiction has not said
  whose it was, when it went in, or whether it is what killed the minotaur. See
  [[the-embedded-axe]].
- It is **not dead** and is **not gone** — it has a hole to hide in and a minute of fear to sit out.
