## Overview
Dunton's townsfolk told the party of **a witch beyond "the castle"** — [[the-keep|the Keep]] — in
S2.39. [[the-patrol-commander|The patrol's commander]] knew of her too: a place of **serious pollution
of the body of the Devourer** that his people can see from far away but that is **gone whenever they
get close**. What he described as hideous was, [[rell-aetris|Rell]] realised, **a garden** — a green
oasis on the plain. He was not sure it was real.

In S2.43 Rell meets her. She **confirms she is that witch**, finds it very funny that the party heard of
her from the Keep's people, and plainly **enjoys running rings round them**.

## Appearance and manner
- **An elf**, **living**, riding fast across [[the-plain|the plain]] on **a large tiger**, bent low over
  its neck to urge it on.
- **Little sparks of light** play around her and speed up when she rides.
- She wears **a very impractical dress** for the conditions and carries **a staff**; Rell is fairly
  sure she is **a caster**. Hailed, she has the staff up ready to cast at once.
- **The tiger is a summoned creature**, not flesh and blood. It paces impatiently while she talks.
- **Impatient but not unkind.** She is sympathetic to the convoy but will not go out of her way for it.
  She takes Rell's telepathic link **readily**, and it feels strong.
- **Cagey.** She gives **no name**, and will not say how long she has been down here — though she
  clearly **lives** here rather than travelling through.

## What she said (S2.43)
- **The Stonehunger is about to return to the depths**, she believes. Anyone who wants out should go
  now.
- **She knows of no ready way out.**
- **She took the convoy for escapees from the Keep**, which takes prisoners and **sacrifices anyone it
  finds down here**.
- Told the villagers were held by a man calling himself a high priest, her eyes narrowed: *that might
  explain a few things.* Told the party had driven him off and that he let them go, she was openly
  surprised — *"He really is leaving"* — and asked what had caused **this change of heart**.
- **On Lae'ethorian:** she was **very surprised** Rell knew the name. They are **allies of a sort**,
  she says, but her feelings are evidently **mixed at best**. **He sent her a message saying he means
  to return to the surface**, and she was riding to him — toward [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] — to find
  out what is going on.
- Asked not to tell him she had met the party, she **acknowledged** it with **no promise and no
  objection**. Rell could not read her.
- Rell asked her to let him know if she learns anything that bears on them.

## The bargain (S2.44)
She rides back the next time the convoy is on the plain, having spoken with
[[the-high-priest|Lae'ethorian]], to ask whether the party means to **betray him** once out — he is
not as worried about that as he should be.

- **She defends him, with reservations.** He is **a significant part of what is wrong among her
  people**, but he has given up much to argue for what he thinks must be done, and **goes back to
  make his case at court**. She **argued against his going**, and says **the party's visit galvanised
  him**. His path **began before his birth and hers**; born as he was, he had little choice. He does
  **not** seek to turn their people deeper into **[[thalparaneth|Thalparaneth]]**'s embrace, but to
  **rally them to a battle the Archon would avoid**, as the Archon did years ago. She would not see him caught, *"not after all this
  time."* It will take him **some weeks** to cross the border.
- **She shudders at his power.** When Rell says it has grown, she looks toward the Ossuary with
  **distaste more than fear**.
- **"How can you blame us?"** She flares at Rell's charge that the elves will use any power: theirs is
  **the one power that can actually do something** — and yet she refused it, so perhaps she is no one
  to speak. Rell, reading her closely, finds her certain that **people do choose corruption**, and
  **bitter**, as if refusing it cost her so much that part of her thinks she was a fool. She means
  them no harm, but he sees her **hardening** toward the idea that protecting Lae'ethorian may mean
  dealing with them.
- **Caspian's word turns it.** He promises they will say nothing of him or of the place, and means it;
  she is relieved. Fabian gives his word too — and **is lying**. In exchange she offers **[[the-witchs-road|her road
  through the Feywild]]**, which she had **refused Lae'ethorian**, not trusting him with it.
- **At her house** she surprises herself again: Lae'ethorian's company has **changed her perspective**,
  or she feels guilty for not offering him the road. She first sees the villagers as **Lae'ethorian's
  discarded possessions**; Caspian's care for them changes how she looks at them, and she gives them
  rest and **a kettle of restorative tea**.

## Who she is (S2.44)
- **An elf druid.** She left **Thal'Mirrith** — fled, not merely left — because she was **under
  pressure to take up necromancy** and would not; she wanted to keep **the purity of her druidic
  practice**. She does not disapprove of necromancy, but knows how it **damages the soul**, and is
  conflicted about her choice.
- She fled by **the Ridgeway Pass**, and came to the Stonehunger **long before Lae'ethorian**.
- **She talks most to [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]**, whom she seems to find the most fun, and wonders
  how and why he would have the sort of magic to repair her wards.
- **Her staff** is strongly magical. She lives with **servants of her house** and her tiger, and has
  spent a long time without other company; it shows.
- **She plays down the risks of her road** — she knows she should not have built it — and is
  fatalistic about the uthrel: if an outbreak comes, it comes.

## DM notes / secrets
- **Her name.** In S2.44 the DM refers to her in narration as **Ithrian** (transcribed variously as
  Ithrian, Ethryn, Etherean, Ithran). **The party does not know it.**
- **Not [[derwyth|Derwyth]].** The players wondered whether she was the exiled elf druid with the
  sabretooth tiger; the DM said she is not, though there are **points of commonality**.
- **Her name was withheld** on purpose in S2.43: *"I'm sure you're going to find out eventually."*
- **She did speak to Lae'ethorian about the party** (S2.44), and he was less worried than she thought he
  should be about being betrayed.
- **The garden is her illusion**, which no one can cross without her permission — see
  [[the-witchs-road]]. What lies beneath it is unknown.
- **The party was split on her (S2.43).** Caspian dislikes "allies of a sort"; Mags mistrusts her on principle;
  Rell reasons that she could have harmed him and did not, and that she keeps apart from the Keep, which
  Lae'ethorian does not.
