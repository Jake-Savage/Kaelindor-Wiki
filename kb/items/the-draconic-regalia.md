## Description
Found in S2.33 beneath a warded flagstone under the northern portico of
[[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]], in a **chest of organic material** so old it came apart
in the party's hands — unlocked, untrapped, and carrying no magical protection of its own (the ward was
on the **flagstone**, not the chest).

Of the contents, **two items are magical**, and are accordingly **pristine and untouched by time**:

- **A crown.** Elaborate and impractical: an outer band, a domed section, and above that a smaller
  tiered rise, "a little bit like a tower" or a layer cake. Pieces hang down at the sides of the head
  that would make **enough noise to make hearing difficult** while it is worn. Bulky, but not heavy.
- **A hand-piece.** Four **finger-rings** joined by fine **chains** across the back of the hand to a
  **bracelet** at the wrist.

The rest has not survived:

- **Ceremonial clothing / regalia**, largely crumbled, but with enough remaining to be carried away and
  examined. It is cut for someone **taller and more slender than a typical human**.
- **Weapons** — ornate but of genuinely **functional** design, not merely ceremonial: **two curved
  swords** and something that **shot darts** of some kind. Non-magical, badly decayed, **unusable**, and
  bearing no markings the party can place.

**Neither magical item has been identified.** The party wrapped everything up to carry out, intending an
Identify or an expert opinion in a city later; they were out of time, with the [[the-gleaners|gleaners]]
due back.

## History
Unknown. The cache sat beneath a flagstone whose abjuration is **bound to** the one protecting
the [[the-golden-dragon-statue|dragon statue]] and holding the crushing rock off the courtyard, and it
could only be reached by working the statue's own mechanism — radiant magic delivered while the dragon
faced the courtyard's original **east**. Whoever hid it did so behind the courtyard's central defence.

[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] can place nothing about the weapons, but on the cut of the costume recalls
legends of the **[[the-dragon-touched|dragon-touched]]** — people of an age well before recorded
history said to have been physically altered by draconic magic. He judges it **plausible** that this
regalia was made for such a person; that is a reading of the evidence, **not** an identification.

## DM notes
- The cache is the most promising thing the party has found for the [[the-furnace-brood|Brood]]'s brief
  of "something that does not belong" (see [[the-korrathian-conspiracy]]) — and, unlike the statue, it
  is **portable**. Whether it is what the Brood want is **not established**.
- Everything else in the courtyard was left as found: the statue restored to its original facing, the
  flagstone replaced.
