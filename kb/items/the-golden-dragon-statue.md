## Description
A **statue of a golden dragon** standing on a large plinth at the centre of
[[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]], beneath [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] (S2.32).

- **Size:** roughly **eighteen feet long** and **ten to twelve feet high** — nothing like a real
  dragon, but large. The neck is sinuously extended; the tail is wrapped around the body.
- **Material:** to a casual glance it **appears to be made of gold**. This is **not verified** — no
  one has touched it, tested it, or cast anything on it.
- **Condition:** the workmanship is **exquisite**, and while the courtyard around it has begun to
  decay, the statue itself is **completely perfect and very shiny**.
- It would be **extremely difficult to carry out**, as [[mags|Mags]] observes.

## History
Unknown. It sits in a buried, decorated courtyard reached by a natural cleft off
[[the-stone-river|the stone river]] — a space the [[the-gleaners|gleaners]] did not build and have
apparently not stripped, which is itself notable given that scavenging is their entire trade. Whether
it belongs to the older complex beneath Kethran's Fold, or was swallowed and carried here by the
[[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]], is **not established**.

Its subject is worth flagging without over-reading it: this is a **gold dragon**, in a world where
[[the-disappearance-of-dragons|dragons have vanished]] and [[dakir|Dakir]]'s draconic inheritance and
[[the-dragon-bones|the dragon bones]] are live threads. Nothing in the fiction links them. The party
has, so far, only looked at it — [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] with an eye to its value.
