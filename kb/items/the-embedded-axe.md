## Description
A **very large axe of dwarven design**, found in S2.36 buried in the chest of
[[the-undead-minotaur|the undead minotaur]] in [[the-buried-arena|the buried arena]], caught in the
ribcage with the creature's **undead skin grown closed around it**. [[mags|Mags]] read it then as a
**holy thing**, and specifically **[[calvyr|Calvyran]]** — a two-handed weapon sized for a strong
warrior, which she could not have wielded.

Freed in S2.37 it proves **pristine and shiny**, a large heavy axe of the sort a serious warrior would
carry. It is heavy enough to be awkward to wield or to climb a rope one-handed with, but not so heavy
that it cannot be carried.

## What it does
**It was keeping the minotaur alive.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] spent his last spell on **Identify**
in the pit:

- The axe **defies ready identification** — he cannot say what it is.
- It is **strongly magical**.
- He is **confident it is the cause of the minotaur's regenerating**.

This matched what the party had already observed: the creature healed while it sat in its den and did
not heal in the open, which [[mags|Mags]] pinned down with two medicine checks.

**Removing it killed the minotaur.** Brute force failed — [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] and
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] both had their sword-in-the-stone moment and neither could shift it — so
[[rell-aetris|Rell]] **cut it out** instead, working around tough undead sinew with a woodsman's sense
of where to cut. Almost as soon as it came free the body **ran through decomposition in about twenty
seconds** and collapsed to dust.

## Where it is now
**[[the-high-priest|The High Priest]] has it.** He appeared minutes later and asked for it back —
*"the axe, which was used in this little experiment"* — before the conversation *"might get a little
less amenable."* [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] tried to leave it in the pit on the grounds that it was
too heavy to lift out, which did not convince; the figure climbed down, held out its hand, and
**[[rell-aetris|Rell]] handed it over** under protest rather than resist.

He says it is **a dwarven axe of some considerable antiquity** — *"not the most difficult deduction
under the circumstances"* — and that it is **an object he is studying and intends to go on studying**.

## DM notes / secrets
- **He handles it with curiosity, not triumph.** [[mags|Mags]]'s read is that it is a **relatively
  unfamiliar object to him in its physical immediacy** — interesting, turned over in the hands and
  looked at, but **not treated as something of great importance to him**. Whatever the experiment was
  about, the axe is apparently not his long-lost treasure.
- **Who put it in the minotaur, and when, is unknown.** The High Priest never claims to have done it —
  only to have recovered it. Nothing establishes whether the blow that lodged it was what killed the
  creature.
- **Why a Calvyran holy axe causes undead regeneration** is unexplained and sits oddly against
  everything the party knows about [[calvyr|Calvyr]].
- [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] offered [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s scholarship on the axe as a way of
  getting rid of the High Priest; he **took the offer completely sincerely**. That debt is outstanding.
