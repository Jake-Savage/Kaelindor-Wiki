## Description
A **very large axe of dwarven design**, buried in the chest of [[the-undead-minotaur|the undead
minotaur]] in [[the-buried-arena|the buried arena]]. It is caught **in the ribcage** and lodged tightly,
and the creature's **undead skin has grown back around it** — it does not sit in an open wound.

[[mags|Mags]] examines it mid-fight and reads it as a **holy thing**, and specifically
**[[calvyr|Calvyran]]**. It is a **two-handed** weapon sized for a strong warrior; she notes she could
not possibly wield it herself.

## What is known
- **It may be hurting the thing it is in.** Asked directly, Mags is told the minotaur **looks angry and
  in pain** — but with the explicit caveat that the party has been hitting it and the **causality
  cannot be read** from the outside.
- **It can probably come out.** "What went in can potentially come out" — but it is in firmly. Mags's
  attempt to draw it with a **Thorn Whip** fails.

## Open questions
- **Whose axe is it?** A Calvyran holy weapon inside a [[calvyr|Calvyr]] temple complex on
  [[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]] invites a guess, and the party has not made one out loud. Nothing
  establishes who struck with it, when, or whether it is what killed the minotaur.
- **Is it why the creature is still up?** Unknown. It is equally consistent with a killing blow that
  did not take, a binding, or nothing of the sort.
