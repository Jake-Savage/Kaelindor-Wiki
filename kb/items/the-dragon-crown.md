## Description
One of the two magical pieces of [[the-draconic-regalia|the draconic regalia]] recovered beneath
[[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]] in S2.33 and identified by [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s
ritual **Identify** in S2.35.

Elaborate and impractical: an outer band, a domed section, and above that a smaller tiered rise — the
party's own description runs to "a tower" and "a layer cake." Pieces hang at the sides of the head that
would make hearing difficult while it is worn. Bulky rather than heavy, and **absurd to look at**. It is
magical enough to **stay on the head** regardless of how badly balanced it ought to be. The name is
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s, coined on the spot.

## What it does
- **Traces of enchantment magic**, such that in **some circumstances** it may enhance
  **charisma-based checks** — not necessarily charisma-based *magic*. The effect is expressly
  **circumstantial**: it will not trigger in every instance, and **what triggers it is unknown**.
- **It turns the wearer's thoughts toward Draconic.** Worn for any length of time, the wearer catches
  their thoughts beginning to express themselves in **Draconic**, the Draconic word rising more quickly
  to mind than its common equivalent. This began for Fabian during the short rest in which he first put
  it on.
- **No attunement was required of it**, unlike the [[the-handlet|handlet]].

## History
Hidden with the rest of the regalia beneath a warded flagstone reachable only by working the
[[the-golden-dragon-statue|gold dragon statue]]'s own mechanism. The clothing it was cached with is cut
for someone **taller and more slender than a typical human**, which is the ground for
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s reading that the set was made for one of the
[[the-dragon-touched|dragon-touched]].

[[caspian-talon|Caspian]] passed it to Fabian on the judgement that he would get more out of it —
and would suit it — than anyone else present.

## DM notes
- The Draconic drift is a **property of wearing it**, established the moment Fabian put it on and kept
  it on. How far it goes, and whether it is doing anything to him beyond vocabulary, is unexplored.
- Together with the archaic Draconic inscription on the statue and the **Lord of Light** it greets, this
  is the second piece of the courtyard to point at Draconic as the language of whoever built the place.
