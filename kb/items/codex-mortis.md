## Description
A tome of necromancy concerning the reanimation of the dead. Pursuing its power destroyed [[kiona-dumois|Kiona Dumois]]: an accident involving the Codex shattered her soul and turned her into a powerful undead. The book itself is lost; what survives in the party's hands are [[kiona-dumois|Kiona]]'s dense infernal notes about it, dangerous to read but said to yield secrets over time.

## What the notes have yielded
- **[[undead-puppetry|Undead puppetry]] (S2.37).** On meeting [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]],
  [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] half-recognised him from a passage in the notes, and read the section
  properly on watch that night: a practitioner can make an **undead puppet** from a fresh corpse and
  channel their awareness and part of their magical power through it, to act in the world while staying
  hidden. The full account is in [[undead-puppetry]]. His conclusion — that **the High Priest was a
  puppet for something else** — he kept to himself at the time, **worried about being asked how he
  knew**, and shared the next morning **without naming the source**.
- **The magic in the book is not like other magic.** Caspian is used to working across schools and
  adapting between them; this **feels different in kind** from anything he has learned. He could not
  work out why.

## Reading it
It is still **perilous to study**. Reading it makes his **skin crawl** and takes an effort of will to
set aside and focus.

**It can be sensed.** Caspian established (S2.37, arcana 31) that the notes **do give off something**
to anyone **attuned to that sort of magic** — but only in **fairly close proximity**, not from miles or
even a distance away. Someone near him would probably realise something was wrong **even if it were
hidden in a stack of books**; simply reading or carrying it is not detectable from further off. He read
it anyway, in a building full of strangers, a few hundred metres from an undead High Priest.

## History
The Codex Mortis is the object [[kiona-dumois|Kiona Dumois]] had built her entire existence around — her [[the-shifting-library|Shifting Library]] roamed the planes stealing books in an attempt to recover the lost Codex (S2.07). The party destroyed Kiona and collapsed her library (S2.09), but did not recover the Codex itself. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] took Kiona's infernal notes on the Codex, which reveal secrets slowly but are perilous to study. The whereabouts of the actual book remain unknown.
