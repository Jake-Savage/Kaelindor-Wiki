## Description
One of the two magical pieces of [[the-draconic-regalia|the draconic regalia]] recovered beneath
[[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]] in S2.33 and identified by [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s
ritual **Identify** in S2.35. "Handlet" is the name the table settled on for it.

Four **finger-rings** joined by fine **chains** across the back of the hand to a **bracelet** at the
wrist. Ornate and decorative rather than obviously martial. Like most magical things of its kind it
**resizes itself to its wearer** — it began far too large for [[mags|Mags]]'s hands and adjusted to fit
as though made for her.

## What it does
- It was **made for a protector or bodyguard**. That is the purpose Identify reads off it, and it
  governs the whole design: nothing about it is a weapon.
- **Mechanically:** when someone within **five feet** of the wearer is subjected to a saving throw, the
  wearer may make that save themselves and, on a success, use their **reaction** to extend that success
  to that one person. The cost is real — in taking the save on, the wearer **exposes themselves to the
  effect**, and a failure catches them too.
- **Requires attunement.**

[[mags|Mags]] took it on the grounds that she backs herself on that kind of saving throw more than
anyone else in the party, which the DM allowed is correct statistically — and on the memory of being
turned into a pincushion in [[the-shifting-library|the shifting library]].

## History
Hidden with the rest of the regalia beneath a warded flagstone reachable only by working the
[[the-golden-dragon-statue|gold dragon statue]]'s mechanism. That the set was cut for one of the
[[the-dragon-touched|dragon-touched]] is [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s reading of the clothing, not an
identification.

## DM notes
- A bodyguard's item cached with a crown and ceremonial robes says something about the household that
  owned them: someone wore the [[the-dragon-crown|crown]] and someone else stood next to them.
