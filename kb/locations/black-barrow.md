## Overview
A **small place** in [[the-mirrowen|the Mirrowen]] that had stood **for many generations, even in these
difficult lands**, until the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] took it **twelve years ago**. Almost
everyone in it died.

It is the origin of **[[dunton|Dunton]]**: the survivors [[the-high-priest|Greyface]] led underground
are Black Barrow's people, and twelve years later they still talk about "the burrow."

## The disaster
Told in S2.38 by [[the-provost|the Provost]] and by one of the young men who lived through it:

- It was **early in the morning**. The first folk had just got up to feed the animals.
- **A great noise, and most of the village was buried** at once. One quarter of it was spared longer
  than the rest, and those people came out into the streets crying and shouting.
- **[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] was there.** He had arrived some days earlier **as a traveller** —
  not an unknown thing — **with a guide**, saying he had **business further south**, and had stayed a
  few nights. *"It was a strange coincidence,"* the Provost says.
- He gathered up everyone he could find and told them to go **in rather than run away** — into the lee
  of the slip. There would not have been time to run; most who tried would have been caught.
- **He made a way.** He worked magic that sheltered them first and then cut a way through, and a little
  distance down it opened out — and it was **not a rockslide at all, but a great cavern**.
- **Forty-two people** went down with him. Not a quarter of the village.
- A **few** others made it out on the surface instead. [[the-provost|The Provost]] spoke to two of them
  afterwards and found them **shaken** — which bears on the surface folklore that people pulled from
  beneath the stone "aren't right" (see [[the-stonehunger]]).

## What changed there
[[kithri|Kithri]], who was in the village, describes what happened to Greyface during the collapse:

- **He had looked like a normal man** when he arrived — *"not a well man, I would have to say, even
  then. But a normal man, right enough, human man."*
- During the chaos he began using **"real magic, serious magic — not just hedge-witch stuff, but wizard
  stuff, priest stuff"** — and **the way he looked changed**. He came to **look like an elf**. Nobody in
  the village had ever seen one in life, and it frightened them; but he was the one keeping them alive,
  so little was said.

## DM notes
- **The spelling is uncertain** — the recording gives "Black Barrow", "Black Barrow" and "Black
  Borough". **Black Barrow** is used here, since the villagers call it "the burrow."
- **Whether Greyface's arrival days before the disaster was coincidence is unestablished.** The Provost
  calls it a strange one and does not push further; nobody in the party asked him directly.
- Nothing says what happened to the site on the surface afterwards, or whether anyone ever went back.
