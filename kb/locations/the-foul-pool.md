## Overview
**The Foul Pool** is a descriptive handle; the place has no in-world name. It lies at the bottom of the stone stair that ends the trapped corridor of
[[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]], about **50 feet** down an irregular, almost square-spiral descent and
then a further very steep flight.

The chamber is **initially natural** rather than cut — irregular, not a designed room — and holds a
**pit of water roughly 70 feet across**. A **ledge** runs the whole way round it, comfortably wide for
one person and tricky for two, and **damaged in places** where sections have fallen away. The ledge
stands about **30 feet above the water**.

The water is about **forty feet deep** and **very unwholesome**: filthy, with matter floating in it,
oily patches on the surface, and **bubbles of gas** rising irregularly — the intermittent slap of water the party heard from far up the
corridor. The chamber is **warm**, and the **water itself is giving off heat**. The air here is the
source, or part of the source, of the corridor's stink; **stagnant water is a fair description of at
least part of that smell** (DM).

## The carved chimney
About **a third of the way round the ledge to the left** of the stairs is a **smooth strip of wall
about 15 feet wide**, carved, running **all the way down into the water** and **all the way up into the
ceiling and beyond it**, like a **chimney**. The carvings continue up out of sight.

- They are in **very much the same style** as the [[the-seven-saints-of-calvyr|saints' reliefs]] in the
  corridor above, but by a **different hand** (DM, confirmed).
- They are **marred by a great many narrow openings** — the same sort of slits as the corridor's
  trap-ports — which were **added after the carving was done**.
- The strip is sheltered by a **canopy**: four stone pillars rising from projections of the ledge,
  carrying a sloping roof over the upper part of the carving. The canopy is **man-made, built by
  whoever made this space** — the DM corrected this point explicitly; it is **not** the work of the
  creatures now living under it.
- **It is the only way on**, and the openings are a **trap**: climbing the carving triggers a whirring
  mechanism and **blades emerge from the slits**. Age and rust have killed some of them; enough still
  work to make the climb genuinely dangerous, and a climber's luck rather than judgement decides it.
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]] took the climb in S2.35 and was grazed rather than pierced.
- **At the top of the carving is a hole in the ceiling**, placed in what looks like a deliberate way,
  opening into darkness. It leads to **[[the-ancient-forge|an ancient dwarven forge]]** — the temple
  complex proper.

## The spiders (destroyed, S2.35)
Roosting in the ceiling **beneath the canopy's sloping roof** are numerous dark shapes — the whole
ceiling there is irregular and lumpy with them. [[theodore|Theo]], sent under the canopy to look, was
rushed **almost at once** by one of them.

- They are **arachnid in form**, and moved with the **clatter of metal**, not the scritch of hairy legs.
  They are **either fully constructs or very significantly modified by artifice** (DM, confirmed) — see
  [[constructs]].
- **Large for spiders, but not large for a threat**: roughly the size of a **small-to-medium dog**.
- They **attack immediately** and are **very quick**. They descend not by scuttling but by **letting
  themselves down on some kind of steel cable**.
- There are **no webs** anywhere. [[rell-aetris|Rell]] reads that, with the instant pounce, as the
  give-away: a real spider would stalk, and would have webbed the place.
- **There were a great many of them**, piled on one another so their number could not be counted, and
  they grew **more wakeful** as the fight in the water went on.
- **They were rusting.** Flakes of rust came down when they shifted; they were plainly **not in good
  repair** after a long time in a damp cave.
- **They sensed magically, and the amulet defeated it.** [[rell-aetris|Rell]] climbed the carving and
  stood beneath them without ever being noticed — they skittered and seemed to look when the blade trap
  opened, but never at him. This **proved** his theory: their means of detection was **magical**, and
  his [[amulet-of-proof-against-detection|amulet of proof against detection]] blinded them to him.
- **Seen close up they were very intricate, and moved naturalistically** — to a standard Rell is **not
  aware of constructs being made to** as a rule (see [[constructs]]).
- **They were destroyed in S2.35.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] moved in under the canopy and cast an
  upcast **Shatter** into it. Old brittle stone and old brittle constructs both gave way: the spiders
  came down, and the **canopy collapsed into the water**.

## Something in the water
There is **something in the pool**, unidentified.

- [[trevick|Trevick]] first spots a shift in the surface patterns that a thrown stone cannot account
  for. With [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s **unseen servant** dropping rations at intervals along an arc
  of the ledge, the movement **follows the drops**, concentrating near them — spread over a wide area,
  but not the whole pool.
- The party cannot agree on **where** in the water it is. The DM ruled explicitly that this is because
  the movement is **genuinely confusing to read**, and **not** because anyone is being magically
  compromised or made to hallucinate.
- **It is a hydra** (S2.35). Five heads on long necks, pale and unhealthy-looking, lizard- or
  snake-like, with jagged teeth. It is strong, tough, and very hard to hurt from the surface; it grabs
  and drags rather than tearing, and it does not use all its heads at once unless it is in a hurry.
- **It is still alive.** The party did not kill it: [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] commanded it to drop
  [[trevick|Trevick]], [[rell-aetris|Rell]] drove it off repeatedly with Dissonant Whispers, and it
  finally dived out of reach. It remains in the pool, active and irritated, and will go for anyone who
  re-enters the water.

## The floor of the pool
Lit from above by [[mags|Mags]]'s **Daylight** during the fight, the bottom — some forty feet down —
shows two features at opposite sides of the chamber ([[trevick|Trevick]], piecing it together
afterwards):

- **A drain.** On one side, detritus and debris have **accumulated** as though drawn toward a point.
  Among the muck around it Trevick can see **one or two shiny objects glinting**, and **bones**: people
  have died in this pit before, and had good things on them. Nobody has gone down for them — the party
  talked itself out of the dive with the hydra still in the water.
- **A clear patch.** On the other side, an area **considerably clearer** than the rest of the floor,
  which the party reads as water coming in.

There are **no strong currents**, and the water has the character of being **largely still** — but the
DM's note is that it is stagnant *now*, which does not mean it always was. Whether there is a mechanism
controlling the flow, or simply a blockage, is unresolved; [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s suggestion is that
clearing the drain might empty the pool entirely.

## Events here
- **S2.34:** The party arrives at the top of the stairs and spends a long time on them, scouting with
  [[theodore|Theo]], an unseen servant and thrown objects rather than stepping onto the ledge. As
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]] finally steps off the stairs to test whether his
  [[amulet-of-proof-against-detection|amulet of proof against detection]] will hide him from the
  spiders, **the remainder of the staircase snaps flat into a steep slope** and pitches everyone else
  **into the pool**, a 30-foot drop. Rell alone keeps his footing. The session ends there.
- **S2.35:** The fight in the water. The hydra takes [[trevick|Trevick]] under and nearly drowns him;
  [[mags|Mags]] gets the party onto the surface with **Water Walk** and lights the pool with
  **Daylight**, [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] climbs out with **Spider Climb** and fireballs the hydra
  underwater, [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] **Commands** it to drop Trevick, and [[rell-aetris|Rell]]
  anchors a rope from the ledge with his climber's kit and repeatedly repels the creature with
  Dissonant Whispers. Everyone gets out. Rell then climbs the carving, springs the **blade trap**, and
  reaches [[the-ancient-forge|the forge]] above; after a short rest the party destroys the spiders with
  **Shatter** and ropes up after him.

## DM notes
- The stairs were a trap in their own right, and the trigger was **not** established at the table.
- Climbing out is **possible but difficult** at most points of the pool — the walls are slick and wet,
  and there is an **overhang** — but easier at the carving, whose reliefs give footholds. After the
  **Shatter**, the upper carving is **badly damaged**: chunks have fallen away and the rest is crumbly,
  so climbing the surface itself is now **very difficult**. The blade mechanism was **not** damaged,
  and some slots are now more exposed than before. The way up is Rell's rope, hung clear of the wall.
- [[mags|Mags]]'s reading of the spiders as **Calvyran automata with authentication protocols** was
  explicitly flagged at the table as the **player's** idea and not the character's knowledge — so the
  party has no in-character reason yet to expect a way of signalling to them.
