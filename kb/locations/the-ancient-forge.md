## Overview
Above [[the-foul-pool|the foul pool]], through the hole in the ceiling at the top of the carved
chimney, lies a room **clearly of dwarven origin** and plainly **part of a forge** — trays of the kind
molten metal is poured into to be worked, and a great many **tools scattered around**.

[[rell-aetris|Rell]] reached it first in S2.35 by climbing the carving, and the rest of the party
followed up a rope after the [[constructs|spider constructs]] were destroyed. Dwarven temples commonly
contain forges, and Rell's judgement is that this puts the party in the **temple complex proper** at
last, rather than in its approaches.

## Layout (S2.36)
The forge is **three large rooms** — large because it was a working forge with multiple smiths in it —
plus a small chamber above:

- **The western room** is the one the party climbs up into, opening onto the shaft above
  [[the-foul-pool|the pool]].
- **The southern room** holds the archway onto the spiral staircase down.
- **The northern room** has a **hatchway in its ceiling** — probably concealed once, no longer — leading
  to **the upper chamber**.

Several **original exits** from these rooms still show in the walls, but each is **filled with rock
pressed forward into the doorway**, bulging slightly, the way something pressed into a mould bulges.

**The upper chamber** is about **20 by 25 feet** — much the smallest room, and **by far the most
ornately decorated**. Its walls carry **relief carvings of religious figures** in a **traditional
dwarven artistic style**, recognisably **[[calvyr|Calvyran]] devotional work**. Beneath them the stone
is **stained from incense** burnt there: these were **active sites of prayer**, not merely decoration.

**Something was taken out of the upper chamber, recently.** The floor carries **straight scrape marks**
where things were dragged away from the walls, and they look **relatively fresh**. A few **scraps of
paper** remain, indicating **written material** — scrolls or the like — was stored here and has been
**removed**. [[trevick|Trevick]] gathers the scraps and hands them to [[caspian-talon|Caspian]];
**they are blank**. Either they are magical and have survived the age of the place, or they are **much
more recent** — the party can test which and has not.

## Condition
Everything is in **considerable disarray**, and the place is **very ancient**: almost anything not made
of stone crumbles at a touch.

- **Other people have camped here.** The lower rooms hold the remains of **fires lit on the floor** and
  a little **low-value debris** left by previous groups — not from the ancient past, but from **months
  and years** back rather than millennia. There is **no sign of a struggle** and no bloodstains; it
  looks like people camped, packed up, and moved on. [[trevick|Trevick]]'s search of what they left
  turns up nothing useful. **Nobody camped in the upper chamber.**

- **Parts of the walls have buckled inward.** There is pressure on this room from the outside — the
  same slow crushing the party has seen everywhere inside the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]].
- **Nothing here is operable.** Rell's search turns up no working mechanism; most of what the room held
  is damaged or has been crushed away under fallen wall.
- The corridor above, the pool, and this forge were **all originally part of the same structure**,
  pushed slightly out of shape since.

## What was made here
The parts laid out in the forge are, even at a glance, **pretty sophisticated** — and Rell got a close
look at the [[constructs|spider constructs]] on his way past them. They were **very intricate**, and
**the way they moved was naturalistic**. He is **not aware of constructs being made to that standard**
as a rule. Whatever was being done in this room was advanced work, and it was done a very long time ago.

## Ways on
- **Two doors** were noted in S2.35 as leading further into the complex, both of a piece with the
  architecture the party has seen. [[rell-aetris|Rell]] looked at them and went through neither.
  Whether the spiral staircase below is one of those two is **not established**.
- **The spiral staircase**, down from the southern room through an open arch with no door in it. It
  **does not match the rest of the complex**: the masonry is **less perfect**, plainer, **workmanlike**,
  with no tiles or decoration. It looks as though it was **taken from a building and positioned here**
  rather than carved out of this rock. Descending it, the **style changes at points** — it is not one
  original staircase but **several, pitched together** and made to serve. It runs a long way down, to
  the raw-stone dead end that opens into [[the-buried-arena|the buried arena]].
- **A significant crack** in one wall opens into natural fissures in the rock.
- **Down** was the way the party came: the ruined carving and Rell's roped climb over
  [[the-foul-pool|the pool]], where the hydra is still alive. **As of S2.36 this route is effectively
  closed** — the western room has collapsed across it (below).

## The night it was eaten (S2.36)
The party never gets its long rest here. It beds down in the **upper chamber** — the most defensible
room, with a single entrance — under [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s **Leomund's Tiny Hut**, a **Glyph of
Warding** [[mags|Mags]] inscribes on the floor of the hut, and a line of **bells and string**
[[rell-aetris|Rell]] rigs in the room below. Over the course of the watches the forge is crushed from
outside:

- **First watch (Caspian):** nothing.
- **Second watch (Trevick):** gravel begins pattering down from the ceiling and Rell's bells tinkle. A
  **fine tracery of cracks** networks the floor of the northern room below, where the floor had been
  pristine hours earlier. From the archway Trevick sees the **western room's wall visibly moving**, and
  at the broken lip of the carvings, **something alive beneath the stone** (see [[the-stonehunger]]).
  He and Rell agree a threshold: move when it reaches the rooms above.
- **Third watch (Rell):** less than half an hour in, **one wall of the western room collapses**,
  loudly enough to wake everyone. Shortly after, **the other side goes too**, throwing up dust and
  leaving the route back down to [[the-foul-pool|the pool]] passable only with great difficulty, and
  about to become impassable. The **northern room** then begins the same process: its floor tiles
  buckle upward, blocks bulge from the walls, and the same cracking is audible from the southern room.

**The upper chamber is untouched.** Through all of it there is **no crack, no dust and no movement** in
the room the party is sleeping in — the only room with the [[calvyr|Calvyran]] devotional carvings in
it. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] raises the possibility that they are **protected** there, drawing the
comparison to the warded [[the-golden-dragon-statue|dragon statue]] in
[[the-golden-courtyard|the courtyard]]. It is a guess, and the party leaves anyway on the reasoning
that a safe room is worth nothing once every route out of it is gone.

## Events here
- **S2.35:** The party assembles here after escaping the pool and destroying the spiders, and stops to
  identify [[the-draconic-regalia|the regalia]] — [[the-dragon-crown|the crown]] and
  [[the-handlet|the handlet]]. It intends to take a **long rest** here, which the DM allows is not a bad
  place for it, and then go on through one of the two doors.
- **S2.36:** The attempted long rest fails (above). The party gets a **short rest** and perhaps five
  hours' sleep, no more, and leaves down the spiral staircase in the **early hours of the morning**,
  with the sounds of continued destruction behind them.

## DM notes
- **The time.** Roughly **eight or nine in the evening** on 30 Dusklin: the party went down to the
  landslide in the late afternoon and entered the complex in the early evening. S2.36 runs through the
  night into the **early hours of the following day**; the DM noted at the table that the in-game clock
  needs sorting out.
- The party has committed to going deeper rather than looking for a way back, and the routes behind it
  are closed: the gate above sealed in S2.33, the carving below was wrecked by the Shatter, and in
  S2.36 the western room collapsed across what was left of it.
- **Whether the upper chamber was actually protected is unresolved.** What is established is that it
  alone showed no damage, and that it alone carries the devotional carvings. The DM's answer to whether
  its architecture was otherwise distinct was **no** — same dressed stone as the rest.
- **Who cleared the upper chamber out, and when, is unknown** — as is whether they are the same people
  who camped in the rooms below.
