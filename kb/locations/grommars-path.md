## Overview
**Grommar's Path** is the name the [[the-gleaners|gleaners]] give to the deep beyond the
[[the-custodian|gate]] at the western face of [[the-stone-river|the stone river]] — where, they say,
"the bigger folk" and the priests closer to "God" dwell, and where they themselves never go (S2.31). It
is named for the legendary dwarven smith [[grommar|Grommar]]. Both the gleaners and the gate itself
call it **deadly**: the face at the gate reckoned the party would "fall and fail," and let them pass
only as a **waste** it was curious enough to permit (S2.33).

The party entered on **30 Dusklin 1568**, at the end of S2.33, and the stone **closed behind them**.

## What is known of it
- **The way in is a dwarven sacred way.** Beyond the gate the passage is deliberately **hewn**, not raw
  rock, and slopes **steeply downward**, going much deeper into the earth. [[mags|Mags]],
  [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] and [[rell-aetris|Rell]] each recognise the style: the sort of **narrow
  ceremonial passageway that leads into the more seriously sacred and secretive dwarven temples of
  [[calvyr|Calvyr]]**. It is unlit.
- **No way back this way.** The gate said plainly that the party would not return by this route, and
  the opening sealed behind them.
- **Other watchers below.** The gate spoke of **"the eyes of God below"** as its **brothers**, and
  doubted they would pass news of the party back up to it (S2.33).
- **The gleaners' account (S2.31, unverified):** priests, wise men and holy men who commune with "God"
  more directly live down here, and a place called **Dunton** is rumoured deeper still, where "godless
  who found a place" live.

## DM notes
- Nothing beyond the entrance passage has been seen. The Calvyr-temple styling is a **recognition of
  architectural type**, not evidence that a Calvyr temple is down there, and it is **not** connected by
  anything in the fiction to [[grommar|Grommar]] himself, to his unfinished weapon, or to
  [[dakir|Dakir]]'s makers. Keep those separate until the source joins them.
- The party carries the gate's **"acknowledgement and blessing"** to show below — which [[mags|Mags]]
  read (natural-20 Insight) as something the creature **has no actual power to grant**.
