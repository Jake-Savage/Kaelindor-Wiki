## Overview
**Unvisited.** Named once, by [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]], when he declines to walk the party
into [[dunton|Dunton]] himself:

> *"My presence in the town causes something of a stir. I don't wish to alarm or distress people
> unnecessarily. I shall return to the Ossuary."*

Asked what he meant by the word, he repeats it and describes it as **"another region of at least
relative stability"** — the same phrase-shape he uses for the pockets inside the
[[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] that have not been absorbed and may never be.

**[[the-provost|The Provost]] can direct the party there**, he says, though he doubts she would be keen
to come along.
