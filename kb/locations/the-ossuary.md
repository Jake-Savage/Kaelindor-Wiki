## Overview
**[[the-high-priest|Greyface]]'s home**, and the place he returns to rather than enter
[[dunton|Dunton]] himself. It lies **north out of the palisade**: a network of rocky pillars that
gradually roofs over into something more like a cave system, with **several ways through** — and
**which of them are open at any time is Greyface's doing**.

The villagers walk the party to the start of the passage and **leave them to go in alone**.

## The place
It is **exactly what the name says**, and it is **obviously a temple**: grand, large, with structures
that read as **altars for offerings**. But its **religious symbols are recognised by nobody** in the
party, and nothing in it resembles anything they have seen on the way down — this is **completely
different** work.

- **One route through it is lit with candles.** Everything off it is dark side-passage; the party stays
  on the lit path.
- **The bones** are all of a **broadly humanoid pattern**, but **very diverse within it**: many
  different sizes, many different skull shapes, considerable variation in the thickness of the bone,
  some from **much more powerful creatures** than others.
- They are **sorted by type, not assembled into skeletons** — piles of arm bones, columns of skulls.
- They are **very old** and **preserved by magic**: past the point where they would otherwise crumble,
  with the sheen of the magic holding them visible on them. **None of them are new**, and none that
  the party can see show **obvious signs of violence**.
- **The arrangement is deliberate and it does something.** [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] judges it placed
  with **a great deal of intentionality**, and that the construction of the place would **influence the
  performance of certain sorts of magic** — the way a [[calvyr|Calvyr]] temple is laid out to promote
  the flow of the magic it specialises in. Which magic, here, he does not say.

**Ossuaries are not a normal thing in this world.** This is not one of a type the party would have met
elsewhere.

## The audience chamber
At the end of the lit route is a chamber that in any other temple would be where **the faithful
gather**. It is large, and focused on **a great chair of bones on a dais** at the far end — **which is
not occupied**.

To one side, clearly **added later**, is an informal, **human-scale** arrangement of chairs. They are
still made of bones, but **put together to be practical objects for use** rather than ornament, and
**cloths are draped on the walls** to make the corner warmer and more colourful. It is where Greyface
actually sits and talks.

## What Greyface says about it
- **It was not built down here.** *"This was certainly wrought down a long time ago."*
- **It survived because of what it is.** *"Like any place of worship and spiritual power, not possible
  to be entirely absorbed or ingested. And in the case of this place, very little of it has gone. It's
  almost entirely intact."*
- **He did not know it was here.** *"I actually didn't know this was here when we came down. It was a
  very lucky find. It has served me extremely well."* He had to **clear out malingerers** on arrival.
- He has found in it **"the resonance here, the concentration of magic"**, which *"opens certain
  possibilities that I had not considered."*

## What happens here
- **In the first weeks after [[black-barrow|the disaster]], people went into the Ossuary and did not
  come back.** That stopped when [[the-provost|the Provost]] arrived and walked in after them.
- **Since then: Dunton's dead are brought here.** *"When someone died, we took them to the Ossuary —
  but not before that."* ([[kithri|Kithri]].) This is the arrangement that supplies Greyface's
  **surrogates** — see [[roscoe|Roscoe]] and [[undead-puppetry]].
- Nobody from Dunton goes in otherwise, and **there are no pilgrims to the place**.

## DM notes
- **It is a sanctuary in the literal sense.** Greyface's advice to the party: *"if you find holy places
  of any sort, I would let them be. They are sanctuaries in this, in a very real sense here."*
  The [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] **has trouble with concentrations of a certain sort of power**
  and he has **not been able to work out why**.
- **Whose temple was it?** Unestablished. The symbols are unrecognised, it predates everything the
  party has walked through, and Greyface places its construction **around the time the Stonehunger was
  imprisoned**. [[the-provost|The Provost]] has heard it was *"a temple of sorts once — a particularly
  unpleasant one."*
- **What the bone-arrangement is tuned for** is not stated, only that it is tuned.
