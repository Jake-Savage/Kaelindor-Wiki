## Overview
The Underdark is the vast network of lightless, dripping caverns where [[mags]] grew up among
gnomish folk — a world "always one bad harvest away from famine," safe from theft only because
it held nothing worth stealing. Survival there depends on tightly controlled population, careful
offerings to the nameless things that haunt the dark, and never disturbing the delicate balance
of the deep. Mags's people kept rigid ordinances for those who dwell beneath the earth.

## What it is (DM-established, S2.30)
The Underdark is a **separate plane**, not merely a deep layer of the material world. Like
[[the-feywild|the Feywild]], it has **geographical commonality** with the material plane — it maps
roughly onto the same terrain, but not exactly — and it is **mostly accessed by things that look
like going underground**, though it does not truly exist beneath the material ground. One cannot
reliably just dig into it; one might, however, strike a spot **thin enough** to break through. This
is **academically established knowledge** on the surface (proven by experiment), though many ordinary
folk still assume it is simply "the deepest caves."

Because the planes only overlap, events cross between them only at scale: a tremor in the Underdark
would have to be **very large and carry a magical component** to shake the surface across the planar
boundary.

Under [[the-mirrowen|the Mirrowen]], the Underdark is **itself twisted** by whatever damaged the
region — though less profoundly than the surface Tangles — and some travellers who must cross the
Mirrowen attempt it **through the Underdark instead**, reckoning it the safer route.

## Planar nativity (DM-established, S2.30)
What makes someone a **native** of a plane is **exposure to that plane's ambient magic**. Both
[[rell-aetris|Rell]] ([[the-tir-van|Tir'Van]]) and [[trevick|Trevick]] carry species-traits of other
planes yet are **natives of the material plane**, having been born and steeped in its magic — though
each would relate differently to their ancestral plane if they went there. **Naturalisation** is
real: living long enough (decades — the DM's example is a tiefling enslaved in **Korrath/Carruth**
for 50–60 years) in a plane of **strong ambient magic** (the Feywild and Korrath among them) reshapes
a person into a native of it, shedding their original planar traits. The Underdark's ambient magic is
**comparatively weak**, so long residence there does *not* imprint nativity the way the Feywild or
Korrath do.

## Notable inhabitants & sites
Gnomish communities; the "nameless things that made the dark somehow darker." It is also named
in the gnomish nursery rhyme and the children's warning that the [[the-uthrel]] "walks the
underways and waits for you beneath."

## DM notes
The Underdark's lore (Mags's *Ordinances for We Þat Dwell Beneþe Þe Eorþe* and the Uthrel
nursery rhyme) hints the Uthrel was known to the deep gnomes long before the party met it —
a possible thread back to [[source-of-the-uthrel]]. See [[the-feywild]] for the contrasting
"hyper-fertile" planes the moss favours.
