## Overview
Mooncross has been a name on the party's maps since S2.05, when the airship route out of
[[bridgemarch|Bridgemarch]] was found to run **Bridgemarch → Mooncross → [[crownspire|Crownspire]]**.
It is also the [[the-mirrowen|Mirrowen]]'s other road connection besides
[[trevaryn|Drake Mount]]. In S2.44 the party comes out of [[the-witchs-road|the witch's road]] in the
foothills of the Stonemarch, near the Ridgeway, with Mooncross the nearest city.

## What it is (S2.44)
The DM's description, given when the witch named the road's destination:

- **A large city of the League** in **Kalredor**, and of all the League's cities **by far the most
  Elvish-influenced**. There is a lot of **Elvish architecture**.
- **Before the war, the Ridgeway was the main passage** between the elves' **Elenfain** and the
  countries that became the League. It is the lowest and most open way across the mountains.
- **A large Elvish population**, then and now. Elves who lived there before the war — traders and
  others, when elves were welcome — were joined about **ninety years ago**, at the war's start, by many
  **refugees who did not support it**. Many of them and their children are still here; plenty moved on,
  further from the front.
- **Regarded as suspect** by a number of the League's other countries, as a potential weak point.
  Kalredor is **garrisoned mostly by League troops from elsewhere**; all the passes are held by
  non-local troops, because local ones are not fully trusted.
- **The Ridgeway today** is **heavily garrisoned on both sides**, with skirmishes and active fighting at
  times. Small groups can still sneak across the mountains, which is one reason elves in the western
  countries are viewed with suspicion.
- **What it offers:** an **airship station**, a civilian government, roads south, and work for
  adventurers. With the League's garrisons drawn from everywhere, [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] might
  find someone in the military who knows his family or has served in Crownspire.

## DM notes
- **Kalredor** is spelled as in [[trevaryn]]; the transcripts give Calredor, Calradore, Calridor and
  Kelvedore. The witch said *"you call it Calridor,"* implying the elves have another name for it.
- The DM has the governments of the League's countries written down but did not give Kalredor's.
