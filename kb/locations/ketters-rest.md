## Overview
The one building in [[dunton|Dunton]] with a sign outside it, and the sign is unmistakably **an inn
sign**. The name — **Ketter's Rest** — is written **in Common**. It is a little bigger than the houses
around it, but not much.

The windows have **no glass**, only cloth pinned across them, easy enough to lift aside and peer
through.

## Inside
By a very loose definition it is a **taproom**. Perhaps half a dozen tables. A few proper chairs, some
stools, and a good many **conveniently sized boxes and other articles pressed into service** as seats.
There is something that does resemble a **bar**, with **mismatched, unlabelled bottles** behind it —
somebody's attempt to make do with what they have.

The drink is a **rough, quite alcoholic spirit** brewed from mushrooms and whatever else can be
mustered. It is unpleasant but harmless.

Upstairs, reached by a staircase that opens directly into the taproom, is a room with **no beds at all**
— blankets on the floor and cushions stuffed with rags. With camping gear it is comfortable enough,
and cramped for a party.

## [[varen|Varen]]
The landlord: a middle-aged human, bleary-eyed when roused in the small hours, and openly **astonished**
to see visitors. *"This is my place."* He is courteous and does his best by them — he will fetch every
pillow in the house if asked — while making no secret of wanting no part of their business.

## Events here
- **S2.37:** The party arrives around four in the morning, announces it was sent by
  [[the-high-priest|the High Priest]], and is given drink and a room. Varen's relief when he learns
  they have not lost anyone is obvious. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] casts **Alarm** on the doorway; the
  party sets watches and takes a **long rest**.
