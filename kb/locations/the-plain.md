## Overview
**The plain** is what the people of [[dunton|Dunton]] call the great open space beyond their palisade.
They named it for what it reminds some of them of; it has **no grass and no trees**.

- It is **large** — a stone ceiling overhead, and the floor running on **as far as the eye can see in
  the gloom**.
- It is **dark**. The light in and around Dunton is [[the-high-priest|Greyface]]'s enchantment; the
  plain itself is unlit.
- It is **dotted with the remains of piles of masonry**, parts of buildings, and **things sticking up**.
- **It does not stay the same.** According to the villagers, those features **are not always in the
  same place**. It is a **dynamic landscape**.
- **It is where material ends up.** [[the-provost|The Provost]] describes "a place where eventually
  material is deposited," and says they have found things there they were sure came from the surface —
  which is how she guessed the Stonehunger had surfaced again, some weeks ago.

## Ways across it
- **The Keep** lies out across it. The villagers **know its direction and the direction stays
  consistent**, though most of them have never gone far enough to see it. Greyface has warned them that
  **once the thing submerges again, things may move around far more profoundly**, and they expect that.
- **[[the-ossuary|The Ossuary]]** is close. Out of the palisade and **north**, a network of rocky
  pillars gradually roofs over into something more like a cave system, and several ways through it
  reach the Ossuary. **Which of them are open at any given time is Greyface's doing** — he opens and
  closes them at will.
- **[[the-gleaners|Gleaners]]** are sometimes seen on the plain, and so, sometimes, are **priests from
  the levels below** — *"very, very many of them,"* per the Provost.

## The creatures
Large things roam the plain, and they are **the main cause of death** among villagers who go out to
gather:

- Broadly **insect-like or crab-like**, and **quite large**.
- **Magical** rather than ordinary — but **not constructs** and **not mechanical**, unlike the spiders
  at [[the-foul-pool|the pool]]. "I don't want to say they're alive, exactly."
- They **move in packs**, and can usually be **avoided if you watch for the signs**.
- They **do not need light** and see perfectly well in the dark.
- They are **edible**. The villagers occasionally kill and eat one that is injured or alone, but it is
  dangerous enough that they do not make a habit of it.

One of them is what killed **[[roscoe|Roscoe]]**, whose body Greyface now wears.

## The wards
Beyond the palisade are **two lines of ward-marks — great half circles** — cut into stone.
[[kithri|Kithri]] recognises them as *"part of a boundary"* and says the work needs stone, since cloth
would not keep still enough. **They were here before the Black Barrow survivors arrived.**
[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] told the villagers at the outset to **stay back behind the ward signs**.

## DM notes
- **Who cut the wards, and what they hold out, is unestablished** — only that they predate Dunton and
  that Greyface treated them as the safe line.
- The party has **not yet set foot on the plain**.
