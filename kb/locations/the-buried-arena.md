## Overview
At the foot of the spiral staircase that leads down from [[the-ancient-forge|the ancient forge]], past
a stretch of apparently blank raw stone, lies **an arena or amphitheatre**. It is the first place the
party has found down here that is **lit**: bright torches burn in braziers around its edge.

The [[the-arena-voice|voice]] that talked the stone open calls the party "visitors" and welcomes them.
Once they are through, **the stone door closes behind them**.

## The structure
It is **patched together out of several different things**. Roughly **70% of it is clearly one
structure** — the main substance of the amphitheatre — and the remaining **30% is odds and ends
collected from elsewhere** and added on to make it grander: extra statuary crammed in, seating sections
that do not match one another.

- **Banners** hang around the edges. They are **not old, and not threadbare** — which sits oddly
  against everything else down here.
- A **mosaic of a bull** is set into the floor. Nobody in the party recognises the iconography, and
  [[mags|Mags]] is told that dredging heraldry for it would only mislead her.
- **A pit** opens in the middle of the floor. A **grill** covered it once; what remains of it is rusted
  fragments around the rim, most of which look to have simply crumbled and fallen in, though parts
  **could** have been bent by force. The pit's mouth is probably wider now than it was built to be.
- **The rock has flowed over it.** Above the arena walls is a rock ceiling, and in places the stone
  **bulges over the top of the structure as though it had flowed across it** and settled, cradling the
  thing. There is no sign of it moving now — but the party has just spent a night watching exactly that
  kind of stone move (see [[the-stonehunger]]).

## The floor
The arena floor is **muddy, wet, and carries an unusual oily sheen**. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s read
of it (survival, 22) is that the oiliness looks **more biological than geological**, and that something
is **lurking below the mud** — which proves right.

[[mags|Mags]] walks an illusory rabbit across it with **Minor Illusion** and nothing reacts; a **stone**
thrown in behaves differently, and she catches the mud **rising to meet it**, engulfing it rather than
simply letting it sink.

**Arena slimes.** Beneath the mud live creatures the party comes to call slimes: flat, featureless
things without faces, likened to the flat creatures that live on a seabed. One takes hold of
[[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s legs when he steps off the clear ground, **restrains** him, and works to pull him
down and smother him. They **can** be hurt by psychic damage, and the one Rell kills **bursts in acid**
as it dies. They are **not undead**. A **Shatter** drives several more of them briefly up out of the mud
before they burrow back down.

Notably, **the minotaur walks through the mud unmolested**, and the slimes take no interest in it.

## Sealed in
The arena is enclosed by an **invisible barrier**. It runs along the edges of the space, level with the
torches, and it:

- **stops movement** — [[mags|Mags]] walks into it and finds it **smooth to the touch**;
- is **completely transparent**, with nothing visible to the naked eye;
- **encircles the whole arena**, on every side tested, **including the other staircase** out;
- **splashes spells off it like a physical wall** — [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]'s flute notes tell
  nobody anything, but a Firebolt or Prestidigitation breaks against it visibly.

With the stone door shut behind them and the barrier around them, the only way on the party can find is
**through the mud, and the pit**.

## Events here
- **S2.36:** The party arrives in the early hours, is sealed in, and is charged by
  [[the-undead-minotaur|an undead minotaur]] that climbs out of the central pit. Rell is taken by a
  slime in the mud and kills it. [[mags|Mags]] turns the minotaur on her second attempt and the party
  lets it flee back down the pit rather than break the effect. The session ends there, with about a
  minute of the turning left to run.

## DM notes
- The party has **not** been told what the arena is for, who lit the torches, who hung the fresh
  banners, or whose bull it is. The [[the-arena-voice|voice]] has answered nothing since the door shut.
- [[mags|Mags]]'s reading in the moment is that **beating the minotaur is what earns a conversation**
  with whoever spoke — her words, not the fiction's.
