## Overview
North of [[the-stone-river|the stone river]], among the many gleaner-built doors that open off the
cavern, there is a **cleft in the rock** — wide enough for two people abreast, but plainly **not a made
doorway**: the rock has split apart to create it. Through it [[mags|Mags]] and
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] first glimpsed a much more lavishly decorated space than anything the
[[the-gleaners|gleaners]] have built, and a flash of something bright (S2.32).

A high investigation check on the cleft (S2.33) reads its history: the rock here is under great
**stress**, **cracked**, and in places **compressed to an almost glass-like texture**. Something has
been **trying to force the opening closed** and has **not succeeded**.

## The courtyard (S2.33)
Roughly **forty feet across** (about a twenty-foot radius), open to the middle, with an **arched
portico** running around three sides — now pressed into and half-swallowed by the surrounding rock,
leaving perhaps a metre and a half of usable flagstone beneath the arches. The fourth side, where the
party enters through the cleft, has **crumbled away** entirely; only the jagged stumps of its portico
remain. There are **no other exits**, though there plainly once were: the rock has closed over them.

- **The floor.** The open centre is laid with elaborate, colourfully-dyed **abstract tiles** — no
  pictures — of good craftsmanship, much like the heraldic floor of the pillar chamber above. A few are
  cracked or broken; most are intact. There is protective magic on them, but **weaker** than
  elsewhere in the room. Under the porticoes the paving is broader **flagstones**.
- **The builders were tall.** From the height of the arches, the courtyard was built for people of
  about **six to seven feet** rather than the usual five to six.
- **It is not where it was built.** The courtyard is demonstrably **no longer in its original position
  or orientation** — [[mags|Mags]] recovers the original alignment from the **weathering on the tiles**,
  and the statue's mechanism answers only to that original bearing, not to the present compass. **How
  it came to be here is not established.** The party's supposition is that the
  [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] swallowed it and that the courtyard's magic is what has kept it from
  being digested; that is their reading, **not** a confirmed fact.
- **The rock is pressing in.** It encroaches **actively** on every side and is being **held back** by
  some resistant effect. That the effect is the statue's is again the party's inference.
- **The statue.** At the centre, on a large plinth, the
  **[[the-golden-dragon-statue|golden dragon]]** — see its own entry for the inscription, the wards, and
  the rotation mechanism.
- **The cache.** Beneath a flagstone under the **northern portico**, outwardly identical to its
  neighbours, lay a chest of **[[the-draconic-regalia|ceremonial regalia]]**, sealed by an abjuration
  bound to the statue's own. It was opened in S2.33 and the contents taken.

## Events here
- S2.32: [[mags|Mags]] and [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]], still disguised as gleaners after clearing the
  cavern, slip through the cleft and find the courtyard and the statue. Nothing is touched or examined
  closely.
- S2.33: The whole party works the room. Mags tests the statue's gold and triggers its ward;
  [[trevick|Trevick]] reads the Draconic inscription through his
  [[glasses-of-comprehend-languages|glasses]]; Fabian finds the plinth rotates; [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]
  maps its magic with Detect Magic and locates the hidden flagstone; and a **Guiding Bolt** into the
  statue, aligned to the courtyard's **original** east rather than the present compass east, opens the
  cache. The party takes the
  [[the-draconic-regalia|regalia]], **restores the statue's facing and replaces the flagstone**, and
  leaves.

## DM notes
- **Two separate suppositions, both the party's.** (1) That the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]]
  **swallowed** this courtyard, and (2) that the courtyard's **magic is what stops it being digested**.
  Neither is established. What *is* established: the courtyard is not in its original position or
  orientation, the rock is actively encroaching and something is holding it off, and the flagstone's
  ward is **bound to** the statue's. The party declined both to move the statue and to Dispel Magic on
  the strength of their reading.
- **Who built it is unknown.** Six-to-seven-foot builders, archaic Draconic script, a naturalistic gold
  dragon, and (below the gate nearby) a [[calvyr|Calvyr]]-style dwarven ceremonial passage do **not**
  yet resolve into one people. See [[the-dragon-touched]] for the party's best guess and its limits.
