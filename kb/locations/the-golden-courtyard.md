## Overview
North of [[the-stone-river|the stone river]], among the many gleaner-built doors that open off the
cavern, there is a **cleft in the rock** — wide enough for two people abreast, but plainly **not a
made doorway**: the rock has split apart to create it. Through it, [[mags|Mags]] and
[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] glimpse a much more lavishly decorated space than anything the
[[the-gleaners|gleaners]] have built, and a flash of something bright (S2.32).

The space beyond reads as a **courtyard** — possibly a temple, possibly a villa. **Archwayed
porticoes** run around three sides of it, but they **lead nowhere**: behind each is bare rock. The
walls carry **murals**, described as not that dissimilar to the [[the-broken-map|map mural]] in the
pillar chamber above, though what these ones depict is not yet examined. The room as a whole is **not
especially aged-looking, but has begun to decay**.

At the centre, on a large plinth, stands the **[[the-golden-dragon-statue|golden dragon statue]]** —
roughly eighteen feet long, ten to twelve feet high, its neck sinuously extended and its tail wrapped
around itself, of exquisite workmanship, and **apparently made of gold**. Unlike its surroundings the
statue is **completely perfect and very shiny**.

## Events here
- S2.32: [[mags|Mags]] and [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]], still disguised as gleaners after clearing the
  cavern, slip through the cleft and find the courtyard and the statue. Nothing is touched, taken or
  examined closely before the session ends.

## DM notes
- The courtyard is presumably part of the **older complex** the gleaners squat rather than anything of
  theirs — porticoes, murals and a monumental statue are of a piece with the marble stair and the
  pillar chamber — but the source does **not** state this, and the cleft entrance means it may equally
  be a fragment of something else the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] has swallowed and carried. Both
  readings are open.
- The statue "appears, at least at a casual glance, to be made of gold." That is the only assay so
  far.
