## Hook
Sent (as the Brood's agent) to investigate the buried village of [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]]
under [[the-korrathian-conspiracy]], the party descends through an ancient buried complex and finds
something far larger than a ruin: the whole site lies **inside a living, moving, devouring entity**.
The [[the-gleaners|gleaners]] — pale goblins who dwell within it — worship it as their **"God,"** which
[[trevick|Trevick]] names the **[[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]]** (the surface folklore of S2.30 made
real). This thread follows the descent itself — past the gleaners toward the deep — and the question
of **what the Stonehunger is**, against the gleaners' warning that it will soon "go below" and move on,
crushing whatever has no "place."

## Progress log
- S2.30 (background): The surface folklore of the [[the-stonehunger|Stonehunger]] is first heard from
  the guide [[cressan-weller|Cressan]] and dismissed by [[nan-prentic|Nan]]; [[mags|Mags]] judges the
  [[kethrans-fold|Kethran's Fold]] collapse **unnatural** (stone welled up from below), and the party
  finds the ruin deliberately excavated and a buried marble stair with a lit, guarded door. (See
  [[the-korrathian-conspiracy]] for the approach.)
- S2.31: Beyond the door the party finds a **pillar chamber** — six [[the-glass-smoke|"glass smoke"]]
  (bound undead), an ancient [[the-broken-map|map]] of the region in an older configuration, and a
  repaired **gargoyle golem** clutching a key. Past a gargoyle-guarded stone door and a spiral stair
  lies an inhabited **dwarvish** complex, where [[theodore|Theodore]] scouts and [[rell-aetris|Rell]]
  twin-casts **Suggestion** on two [[the-gleaners|gleaners]] ([[turley|Turley]], [[glorta|Glorta]]).
  Under questioning they reveal the site is **inside the living Stonehunger** ("God") — which moves,
  devours the surface, digests, and moves on, crushing the placeless "godless" — and that the gleaners
  earn a place by scavenging its kills (they gleaned this village's collapse). The route deeper runs
  **past the sleepers** (creatures resting among the stinking crates, who wake at a scent), over the
  **bridges** across a chasm of **churning stone** (~40–50 gleaners under [[tuffin|Tuffin]]), to the
  **[[the-custodian|custodian]]**'s gate onto the deadly deep ("Grommar's Path"). [[trevick|Trevick]]
  casts **Pass Without Trace**; the party slips past the sleepers, puts both gleaners to sleep, and
  (via Theodore) glimpses the **bridge-web over the churning stone**. Split over whether to fight or to
  cut bridges / Mass-Suggest their way across, the party ends the session resolving to devise a plan.
- S2.32: The party clears the chasm **without a fight**. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] gives
  [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] a **potion of diminution**; disguised as a gleaner, Fabian casts
  **Mass Suggestion** from [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s scroll — God has brought more gifts, there are shiny
  things on the surface, go up at once — and empties most of the cavern, with [[mags|Mags]] and Fabian
  (both under Disguise Self) talking round a wavering group and then persuading the last suspicious
  dozen to go and check on their companions. The party crosses the **middle bridge** of
  [[the-stone-river|the stone river]]; from it Fabian sees, among the Kethran's Fold debris in the
  churn, **worked stone that could not have come from the village** (a column fragment, a marble
  tile). North of the canyon, Mags and Fabian slip through a **natural cleft** into
  [[the-golden-courtyard|a buried courtyard]] — porticoes leading nowhere, murals, and a pristine,
  apparently gold **[[the-golden-dragon-statue|dragon statue]]** on a plinth. At the archway the rock
  **flows into a huge face**: [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s Arcana names it an **earth elemental**
  (which can be **bound into service by magic**), and it asks, in a deep earthy voice, whether they
  intend to walk **[[grommar|Grommar's Path]]** — after a smith of legend who sought to forge a weapon
  that could kill death and vanished. The party takes it for the [[the-custodian|custodian]]; the
  session ends mid-conversation.
- S2.33: The party works [[the-golden-courtyard|the golden courtyard]] out. The
  [[the-golden-dragon-statue|gold dragon]] proves **warded** (Mags's fingernail triggers it), inscribed
  in archaic Draconic — **"The Lord of Light greets the dawn of a new age"** ([[trevick|Trevick]]'s
  glasses) — mounted on a **rotating plinth** that has been turned before, and possessed of both
  **evocation** and a capacity to **absorb** magic. Rotation alone does nothing and every spell thrown
  at it is swallowed; but [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] locates a **hidden flagstone** under the northern
  portico (Detect Magic, advantage, Mags's Guidance) whose ward is **bound to** the statue's,
  and [[mags|Mags]] recovers the courtyard's **original orientation** from the weathering on the tiles —
  the bearing that matters being east relative to the courtyard's **original construction and
  position**, not the present compass. With the dragon so aligned, her **Guiding Bolt** makes the statue glow and throw
  a beam onto the flagstone, which lifts freely: beneath is a chest of
  **[[the-draconic-regalia|ceremonial regalia]]** — a magical crown and hand-piece, crumbled robes cut
  for someone taller and slimmer than a human, and decayed functional weapons.
  [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] connects the cut to legends of the [[the-dragon-touched|dragon-touched]].
  The party takes the cache, restores the room, and goes to the gate — where the face (see
  [[the-custodian]]) calls itself **"the eyes of God in this part,"** refuses on grounds of waste, is
  won over by Fabian's tale of [[shadows-meet|Shadows Meet]], betrays **frustration and shame** at a
  **limit to the lands its kind can now claim** (Mags, natural-20 Insight), and takes
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]]'s **wager** — a thread to pull if they fail, its acknowledgement if they succeed
  — despite having, as Mags reads, **no power to grant either**. The stone opens onto
  **[[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]]**: a hewn **[[calvyr|Calvyr]]-style dwarven ceremonial passage**
  sloping steeply down. The party descends and the gate closes behind them.

- S2.34: The party walks the first leg of **[[grommars-path|Grommar's Path]]**, which proves to be an
  actual **[[calvyr|Calvyr]] temple corridor** — devotional reliefs of **all
  [[the-seven-saints-of-calvyr|seven classical saints]]** alternating down about a hundred feet of
  cleanly-dressed stone — that has been **comprehensively trapped after the fact** by an unknown hand,
  the trap-ports cut into and around the carvings. They pass, in order: a spent **needle-or-gas** trap already triggered by
  [[mags|Mags]] and [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] walking up to the first relief; a **ceiling hammer**,
  edged around and then deliberately sprung from a distance (it destroys Mags's staff and reveals no
  service passage above, only a gear); a **spike drop**, jumped, with [[rell-aetris|Rell]] chalking the
  safe standing spot; **two portcullises and a sweeping axe** at Vero's relief, sprung when Rell moved
  in to prise out its **green gem eyes**, and beaten by a **piton-and-rope pulley** that turned the
  axe's own momentum into hauling the far grate open — the DM confirming afterwards that the trap would
  have escalated on its **seventh** swing and Rell stopped it on the sixth; and finally a field of
  **magically warded floor and wall stones** at Sargrim Hexward's relief, which Rell reads and chalks a
  path through. Beside the relief of the **brewing saint** — the one at which no trap is found — is a
  Dwarvish line cut into the floor later than the rest of the work and with care:
  **[[khazar-vuk-varag|"Khazar Vuk Varag — I defy you"]]**, a proper name meaning *oldest enemy* or *ancient
  hatred*. Read against the [[grommar|Grommar]] legend the party would almost certainly take it for
  **death**, though it is expressly not a dwarvish or Calvyran way of naming death; Mags's religion
  check turns up nothing further. The corridor ends at a stair down to **[[the-foul-pool|a wide pool of
  warm, filthy water]]** in a natural chamber, ringed by a damaged ledge 30 feet above the surface.
  **Construct spiders** — dog-sized, metallic, webless, immediately hostile — nest under a built canopy
  over the one carved strip of wall that runs from the water up into the ceiling like a chimney, and
  which is the only apparent way on. **Something unidentified moves in the water**, following objects
  dropped in. The party scouts at length from the stairs with [[theodore|Theo]], an unseen servant and
  thrown rations; as Rell finally steps off onto the ledge, **the staircase snaps flat** and pitches
  everyone else into the pool. Rell alone is left standing.

- S2.35: The party gets out of the pool and up into the temple. The thing in the water proves to be a
  **five-headed hydra**, pale and unhealthy-looking; it takes [[trevick|Trevick]] under and nearly
  drowns him. [[mags|Mags]] lifts everyone onto the surface with **Water Walk** and then lights the
  whole pool with **Daylight**; [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] climbs out on **Spider Climb** and drops a
  fireball on the hydra underwater; [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] **Commands** it to drop Trevick; and
  [[rell-aetris|Rell]] anchors a rope from the ledge with his climber's kit and repeatedly drives the
  creature off with Dissonant Whispers. Everyone gets out alive and the hydra dives, **still alive and
  still in the pool**. With the pool lit, Trevick makes out the floor forty feet down: a **drain** on
  one side where detritus has gathered — with **bones and a glint of shiny things** among it, from
  people who died here — and a **clearer patch** opposite where water comes in. Nobody dives for the
  loot. Rell then climbs the carved chimney, which turns out to be a **blade trap** — rusted in places
  but lethal enough — and is grazed rather than skewered; crucially the **spiders ignore him**, which he
  **proves** that his [[amulet-of-proof-against-detection|amulet]] blinds their sense — it is magical.
  Above, he
  clambers into **[[the-ancient-forge|an ancient dwarven forge]]**: buckled walls, scattered tools, and
  sophisticated but ruined construct parts — the temple complex proper, and almost certainly where the
  spiders were made. Over a short rest [[caspian-talon|Caspian]] ritually identifies
  [[the-draconic-regalia|the regalia]] — [[the-dragon-crown|a crown]] that sharpens charisma in
  circumstances nobody can pin down and turns its wearer's thoughts to **Draconic**, and
  [[the-handlet|a handlet]] made for a **bodyguard**, which lets its wearer take a saving throw on
  someone's behalf. Fabian takes the crown, Mags the handlet. The party then destroys the spiders with
  an upcast **Shatter** — wrecking the carving's climbable surface in the process, though not the blade
  mechanism — and ropes up into the forge, where it means to take a long rest before going on through
  one of the two doors.

## Open threads
- **What is the Stonehunger, truly?** Corroborated as a real, vast, moving devourer the party is
  **inside** — but whether it is a deity, a living titan/beast, an ancient construct, or something
  else is **not established**. The gleaners' account is theology, not settled cosmology. S2.33 adds
  that it is watched over by **"the eyes of God"** — bound elemental gatekeepers who call each other
  brothers (see [[the-custodian]]).
- **Why do the eyes of God believe their reach has shrunk?** The gate genuinely believes there is now
  a limit to the lands they can claim, and is ashamed of it. Cause unknown; kept **separate** from the
  Mirrowen's fragmentation.
- **Who built the [[the-golden-courtyard|golden courtyard]]**, and who was the **"Lord of Light"** its
  inscription greets? Six-to-seven-foot builders, archaic Draconic, a gold dragon, and a Calvyr-style
  dwarven way below do not yet make one people (see [[the-dragon-touched]]).
- **What do the crown and hand-piece do?** Unidentified.
- **Does it relate to the Mirrowen's damage?** Is it a cause or a symptom of the region's planar
  fragmentation and its shifting, village-swallowing land? Unknown — kept separate until the fiction
  links them.
- **No established link to the [[the-uthrel|uthrel]].** Both "devour," but nothing connects the
  Stonehunger to the uthrel incursion. Do not assume one.
- **Who built the buried complex** (marble stair, dwarvish machine-room, [[the-broken-map|map]],
  [[the-glass-smoke|glass smoke]], gargoyle golem) before the gleaners squatted it? Unknown.
- **What the [[the-glass-smoke|glass smoke]] are**, what lost "home" their lonely half remembers, and
  **what the [[the-broken-map|map]] records** (and whether it predates the Mirrowen's fragmentation).
- **The clock:** [[turley|Turley]] says God will "go below" (move on, crushing the placeless) within
  days or weeks — a hard limit on how long this place can be explored.
- **Who binds the gate?** The [[the-custodian|custodian]] is an **earth elemental**, and such
  creatures can be **compelled** into service by magic. Whether this one serves willingly, and if not
  who or what holds it, is unknown.
- **What is Grommar's Path?** Named for [[grommar|Grommar]], the dwarven smith who sought a weapon
  that could kill death and disappeared. S2.34 establishes that its first leg is a **Calvyr temple
  corridor, trapped after it was built** by a hand the party cannot name. It fits the legend of a
  trap-happy Grommar, which is why they assume him.
- **Who is [[khazar-vuk-varag|Khazar Vuk Varag]]?** The name cut into the temple floor and defied. The
  Grommar-shaped reading is *death*; [[mags|Mags]] keeps pressing that "oldest enemy" and "death" are
  not the same claim, and no tradition she knows carries the name. Unresolved.
- **Who carved the inscription, and for whom?** It was cut carefully, after the corridor was built, in
  a place others would read it. [[rell-aetris|Rell]] reads it as Grommar's own victory note on having
  survived the trap-run; that is an inference.
- **Who made the spiders, and to what end?** Destroyed in S2.35, but Rell saw them close up first:
  **very intricate**, moving **naturalistically**, and better than he knows constructs to be made. The
  forge directly above them held sophisticated construct parts. Nobody has said the two are connected.
- **What is under the drain?** Bones and shiny things lie in the silt forty feet down, along with
  whatever the drain leads to. The party left it, with the hydra still in the water.
- **Is the pool meant to empty?** A drain, an inflow, and water that is stagnant *now* — the DM's phrase
  — suggest a system that once ran. Whether there is a mechanism or merely a blockage is unresolved.
- **What trips the staircase?** The stairs are themselves a trap and the trigger was never established.
- **What lies through the forge's two doors**, and through the crack in its wall?
- **What is the [[the-golden-courtyard|courtyard]], and the [[the-golden-dragon-statue|gold dragon]]
  on its plinth?** A monumental, undecayed dragon statue in a decaying buried courtyard the gleaners
  have not stripped. Unexplained, and **not** to be linked to
  [[the-disappearance-of-dragons|the dragons' disappearance]] or [[dakir|Dakir]]'s heritage without
  the fiction doing so.
- **Where does the non-Kethran worked stone in the churn come from?** Column fragments and marble
  tiles pass in the river that cannot be from the village. Kept distinct from the question of who
  built this complex.
