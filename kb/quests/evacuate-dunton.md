## Hook
[[mags|Mags]] puts it to the party in [[dunton|Dunton]] in S2.39, once they know what the settlement is
and how long it has been there:

> *"There were forty-two of them when they came down. That was twelve years ago. The next time it
> dives, that's another twelve years. I don't think they'll be here. I think they'll all die, including
> the children. **I think we have to try.**"*

**[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] has told the party the Stonehunger will submerge within days — a week
perhaps** — and that if they want out they should go now. The same window is the villagers' only one.
The next surfacing, on the evidence, is twelve or sixteen years away.

## Who is being evacuated
**Since S2.42: nineteen people and the dog** — [[kithri|Kithri Munn]] died fighting the patrol.

**After S2.41: twenty people and the dog.** Seven are children aged one to eleven, six of them missing
a parent and Daisy Thorngage both; the oldest is Sarah Farlin, seventy-six; one, Mothrigg Tosscobble,
is a long-term survivor of the earlier settlement. The full roll of the living and the dead is on
[[dunton]].

**At the outset, thirty-four**, per [[the-provost|the Provost]]'s own count:

- **Five young children** too young to fend for themselves, plus some older ones.
- **Two of the four original long-term survivors**, who have been underground close to a quarter of a
  century and are **not coherent**.
- One **very old dog** that came down from the surface.

With the party that is **close to forty**, moving **slowly and visibly**, many of them unsuited and
unprepared for it.

## The routes
From [[the-provost|the Provost]], who has had twelve years to think about it:

- **The way the party came in is gone.** What remains are **"paths that are more contingent."**
- **Over [[the-plain|the plain]]** — dark, shifting, roamed by large creatures in packs, with
  **[[the-keep|the Keep]]** more than a day's walk out across it. The villagers' view is that you
  simply cannot go that way; you would be set upon the whole distance and never arrive.
- **Under the plain.** Stories years ago of another intact area **past the Keep**, in what she calls a
  **honeycomb of tunnels**. She knows **the start of those paths** and that **they lead up** — not that
  they reach the surface. If she believed this place was about to be lost, that is the way she would
  take, because *"I wouldn't trust again in another power I don't understand."*
- **Ask Greyface.** He **brings things in** from somewhere, so he knows a way. Whether he would share
  it — or permit the village to leave at all — is the whole problem.

## The problem
**[[the-high-priest|Greyface]] needs them.** The Provost says so plainly: *"he needs us too. I don't
think he will willingly let us go. Not all of us, at any rate… I think he must. Or give up whatever it
is he is about."* [[necromancy|His magic]] runs on the life energy of other people, and Dunton is his
supply — its dead go to [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] as a matter of course, and very occasionally its
living.

He is also **not someone the party believes it can fight**. [[caspian-talon|Caspian]]'s assessment,
after a day in his company: *"He has more power and more magic than I have ever seen, and I cannot
fight that. You cannot fight that."* And what the party would be fighting is **a puppet**, not the man.

## Where the party stood (S2.39)
S2.39 **ended on a genuine disagreement**, settled in S2.40 by running (see Progress log):

- **[[mags|Mags]]** — go, and go now, all of them. She would accept a **high death rate** against the
  certainty of leaving them, and would **hang back herself** to slow Greyface down. Her instinct is to
  run rather than warn him.
- **[[caspian-talon|Caspian]]** — they cannot fight him and should not try; the party has been given
  free rein to leave and should take it. He does **not** believe the village can realistically be
  brought out. But he argues **against** running: better to **talk to Greyface first** than to be
  caught fleeing across land he controls. He will hang back with Mags if it comes to it.
- **[[trevick|Trevick]]** — **it is not the party's decision to make.** The villagers have lived this
  reality for twelve years and must choose for themselves; he would prioritise **the children and their
  families**. He also holds that **excluding Greyface from the decision guarantees most of them die**.
- **[[rell-aetris|Rell]]** — certain they must try: *"leaving them here to die is as much as killing
  them."* But make the case to Greyface, who **plainly possesses some degree of morality**, and
  **offer him an alternative**.
- **[[fabian-perennius|Fabian]]** — save them; if not all, then at least **the youngest generation**.
  His worry is that if Greyface realises what they are doing, they lose the chance entirely.

**What they agreed on at the time:** that next they would go to [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]] — with
[[the-provost|the Provost]], who insists on speaking for the village herself — and **ask for
everything**: let the villagers leave, give us an easier way out, and **you could come too**.
In S2.40 they dropped that plan and left without asking.

## Progress log
- S2.42: **The patrol.** Ten minutes after the swarm, **the Keep's patrol**, seven strong, comes at the convoy under
  Mags's Daylight. Two **Fireballs** — Caspian's into them, then their commander's into the party —
  drop Mags, Fabian and [[kithri|Kithri]]; Kithri is killed where she lies. The party gets back up, and
  **the villagers fight beside them**: Maddie Pruett, Finnan Highcroft, Rosie Tryn, Bohdan Tarek and the
  dog take on a caster in their midst until Trevick kills him. Fabian's magic leaves the **commander**
  helpless with laughter; Mags turns two, one of whom **gets away**; Rell and Trevick cut down the rest. **Rell binds and gags the
  commander.** He speaks **an old Draconic**, and Fabian's crown holds his eye. **Nineteen villagers
  survive.** The party means to question him and go on.
- S2.41: **Found, and fought.** The convoy hides in the dark while Rell's diversions and Hawk's firebomb
  try to set the Keep's patrol and [[the-bone-swarm|the bone swarm]] on each other. Caspian argues
  they have made a terrible mistake and should let Greyface take the villagers back; the party instead
  **stands with them**, tells them the truth, and gives them the choice to fight or run. Mags's Daylight
  shows the swarm splitting in two. It rolls over the front of the convoy, killing or taking up most of
  those beneath it. Caspian dispels its darkness to reveal **an elf on a throne of bones** —
  Lae'ethorian in person — and Rell tackles him off it; Trevick's arrow breaks his necromantic hold on
  Rell; Mags shelters **seven souls** of the dead. **He withdraws.** **Twenty villagers survive** —
  every child among them — and the dog; **fourteen are dead**, among them [[varen|Varen]] and
  **[[the-provost|the Provost]]**, with her knowledge of the tunnels. The roll is on [[dunton]].
- S2.40: **The party runs, tonight, without asking.** Weighing that the puppet is far less capable
  while its operator sleeps, and on a [[mags|Mags]] Augury of **weal** to whether
  [[the-high-priest|Greyface]] would catch them if they left within thirty minutes, they drop the plan
  to petition him. [[fabian-perennius|Fabian]] gathers the village with a song and a speech — telling
  them, when asked, that the party **has** spoken to Greyface and that he says the place goes down in
  five days — and Mags, [[rell-aetris|Rell]] and Fabian win them over. Some try to go and warn Greyface
  and are **physically stopped**. **All thirty-four leave, with the dog**; five under protest. No
  message is left. The [[the-provost|Provost]] navigates for a stretch of porous rock with **upward
  shafts** like the Ossuary's entrance; [[trevick|Trevick]] keeps the column under Pass Without Trace;
  Rell lays a false trail and a timed flare two miles off; [[hawk|Hawk]] flies a decoy and watches the
  village. Some hours out, Hawk returns in alarm: **something very large, eighteen to twenty feet,
  sounding of clattering bone**, has come from the village. Caspian, Mags and Rell drop back as a
  rearguard; a second Augury on whether that will save the convoy comes back **weal and woe**. The
  thing veers toward Rell's flare. Up front, a **patrol of six from the Keep** crosses ahead; Hawk drops
  a firebomb near it to draw it off and the column moves on. **The caves are still hours away.**
- S2.39: Opened. [[mags|Mags]] puts the case; the Provost counts thirty-four villagers and describes
  the routes; the party splits five ways on run, negotiate or leave, and agrees to petition Greyface.

## Leads / next steps
- **Question [[the-patrol-commander|the prisoner]].** The party wants a way out, a secret exit if there
  is one, and to know whether more will come. Rell will ask telepathically; Fabian, crowned, relays.
- **Don't linger.** One of the patrol got away, and Rell argues against resting here.
- **Find the tunnels without the Provost.** She was making for **porous rock and caves** like those at
  [[the-ossuary|the Ossuary]]'s entrance, with **cut shafts leading upward**, several hours' walk on.
  Trevick found the way north-east **choked with rocks and ruins**.
- **Move.** The survivors are fewer and younger but no faster, and frightened. The swarm is moving
  away.
- **Look after Rell**, healed but drained by Lae'ethorian's necromancy.
- **The seven souls.** Mags carries them. What can be done for them?

## Open threads
- **Will Lae'ethorian come back?** He withdrew rather than lost. What did he come for, and how much of
  it did he get?
- **The dead.** Their bodies were taken into the swarm. What becomes of them, and of the souls Mags did
  not reach?
- **Was Fabian being scried?** He felt suddenly watched; Mags thinks so; nobody knows.
- **The patrolman who got away.** Will he report the convoy to the Keep, and what comes after it?
- **The patrol.** It was near Hawk's bomb, speaking a language Trevick does not know — **to
  Lae'ethorian, it is inferred**. What passed between them, and is the Keep now looking for the
  convoy?
- **The lie.** The village left believing the party had Greyface's word. The survivors have now seen
  what he does to those who leave. How do they see the party?
- **Will the shafts reach the surface?** Nobody ever knew that they did.
