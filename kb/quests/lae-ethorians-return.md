## Hook
In S2.44 [[the-witch]] tells the party that [[the-high-priest|Lae'ethorian]], who had held
[[dunton|Dunton]] for twelve years beneath the Stonehunger, is **leaving to go back across the border
and make his case at court**. She argued against it; **the party's visit galvanised him**. She asked the
party not to betray him, and her road out was the price of their answer.

## What is known
- **He is going home.** It will take him **some weeks** to cross the border. After that, the witch says,
  it hardly matters what the party says, but he must have the chance to make his case.
- **What he argues for**, per the witch: not to turn the elves deeper into
  [[thalparaneth|Thalparaneth]]'s embrace, but to **rally them to a battle that the Archon would avoid**,
  as the Archon avoided it years ago. His path **began before his birth**; born as he was, he had very
  little choice.
- **His power has grown**, and the elves respect power, at present. She does not know how things stand
  at court, and neither, she says, does he.
- **She calls him a significant part of what is wrong among her people**, but has come to see more
  wisdom in him than she expected.
- **Necromancy holds back the [[the-uthrel|uthrel]]**, the witch says — *"the one power that can
  actually do something."* He supplied the necromantic ward-stones for [[the-witchs-road|her road]].
- **He is going up by another way.** The DM said that had the party lingered on the surface by the
  caves, they might have met him there.

## The promise
- **Rell argued he should be reported**: wanted for many terrible things, with power that has grown
  considerably.
- **Caspian promised silence** — none about him, none about the place — and **meant it**, suggesting ways
  to stop the villagers speaking too.
- **Fabian gave his word** that they would not hunt him or send others after him. **He was lying.**
- The witch would have preferred all of it to be true.
